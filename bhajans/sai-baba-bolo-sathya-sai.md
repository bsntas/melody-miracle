## Lyrics

Sai Baba Bolo Sathya Sai Baba Bolo

Sai Rama Bolo Sathya Sai Shyama Bolo [ Shirdi Sai … ]

## Meaning

Chant the Holy Names of Lord Sri Sathya Sai Baba and Shirdi Sai Baba. Lord Sai is verily Lord Rama and Allah (also glorified as Rehman and Maulah).
