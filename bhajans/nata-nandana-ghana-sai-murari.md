## Meaning

Oh Lord Krishna! You are the divine dancing child! You destroyed the demon Mura, You are the blue-colored Lord of enchanting beauty, holding the flute. You are the Lord of Liberation, the consort of Lakshmi, You lifted the mountain Govardhan. You danced with the cowherd girls and played in Brindavan. I am enchanted by Your captivating dark-colored form.
