## Meaning

The Mother Goddess is personification of Shakti (power) which pervades the Creation. She is revered as power of the Sun (Savitri), the bestower of wisdom (Saraswathi) and Mother Gayathri, who is the personification of Durga, Lakshmi and Saraswathi.
