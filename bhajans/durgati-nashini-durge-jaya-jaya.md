## Lyrics

## Meaning

Victory to Goddess Durga, who destroys misfortunes and distress. Victory to Goddess Kali, who is the destroyer of demonic qualities. Victory to Goddess Uma, Sita, Radha and Rukmini. Victory to Lord Sai, who is affectionate, tender and loving towards us.
