## Meaning

Glory to Lord Rama, the Supreme uplifter of the world, consort of Mother Janaki and the crowned King of Ayodhya. Lord Rama is served by His younger brother Lakshmana. Glory to the handsome and lotus eyed Lord Rama. He is the crown jewel of the Solar dynasty who vanquished the evil Ravana. Lord Rama who has a pleasing personality, is worshipped by Maruti. Glory to the Lord who is the guardian of His devotees and who grants Liberation.
