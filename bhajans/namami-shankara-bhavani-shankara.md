## Lyrics

Namami Shankara Bhavani Shankara
Uma Maheshwara Tava Sharanam
Om Shiva Om Shiva Parathpara Shiva
Omkareshwara Tava Sharanam
Namo Namo Nataraja Namo
Hara Jata Jootadhara Hara Shambho
Namo Namo Shiva (Sai) Raja Namo

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Shankara (Shiva), the consort of Mother Uma (Parvathi). Surrender to the Supreme Lord who is the very embodiment of "Om". Salutations to Lord Nataraja, the Cosmic Dancer and the one with matted hair. Salutations to Lord Sai who is verily Lord Shiva.
