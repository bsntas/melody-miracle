## Lyrics

Bhaja Sai Natham Devadi Devam
Devadi Devam Shritha Jana Palam [ Bhaja Sai ... ]
Parama Pavitram Pavana Roopam

Janana Marana Vimochaka Roopam

Sathya Sai Natham Sadananda Roopam

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Sai who is the God of gods protecting His devotees. His form is highly sacred and divine which grants auspiciousness. His blissful form redeems one from the cycle of birth and death.
