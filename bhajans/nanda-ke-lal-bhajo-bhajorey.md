## Lyrics

Nanda Ke Lal Bhajo Bhajorey Gopi Gopal Bhajo
Murali Mohana Shyama Bhajorey

Nanda Kishora Navaneetha Chora
Parthi Vihara Sai Gopala [ Giridhari Lal ... ]

## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Krishna, the darling of Nanda and the Lord of the cowherd. Sing of the dark-hued one with the enchanting flute. Sing of Nanda's child who lifted the Govardhana mountain and the stealer of butter (hearts of devotees). Sing of the one who dwells in Parthi, Lord Sai who is verily Lord Krishna.
