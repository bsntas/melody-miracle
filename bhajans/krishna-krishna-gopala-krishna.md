## Lyrics

Krishna Krishna Gopala Krishna

## Meaning

Chant the glorious name Krishna whose other names are Govinda, Mukunda (one who liberates), Madhava (one who is having Lakshmi on the chest), beloved of Nanda of Gokula and embodiment of existence- awareness-bliss.
