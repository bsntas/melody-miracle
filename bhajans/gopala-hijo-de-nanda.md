## Lyrics

Gopala, Hijo De Nanda

## Meaning

O Lord Gopala (Krishna), son of Nanda, so sweet is Your flute, I pray that You make it sing. O Keshava, free us from evil. O Holy Lord of Brindavan, play Your flute and fill my dreams.
