## Lyrics

Neela Megha Shyamala Roopa Gopala
Nithyananda Swaroopa Sai Gopala
Eshwaramba Nandana Sai Gopala [ Nithyananda Swaroopa ... ]

## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Gopala (Krishna), the dark complexioned one. Glory to Lord Sai, the ever blissful one, the beloved son of Mother Eshwaramba. Lord Sai is verily Lord Krishna.
