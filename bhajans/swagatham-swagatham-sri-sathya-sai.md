## Lyrics

Swagatham Swagatham Sri Sathya Sai Suswagatham
Prashanthi Nilayam Paramatma Roopam
Parthi Pureesham Suswagatham [ Swagatham Swagatham ... ]

Dharma Swaroopam Suswagatham

## Meaning

Welcome, O Lord Sathya Sai, Lord of Prashanthi Nilayam, You are the very embodiment of the Supreme Consciousness. Welcome, O Divine Preceptor, You are the personification of Truth and Righteousness. Glory to Thee, O Lord.
