## Lyrics

Guru Devaya Guru Devaya Guru Devaya

## Meaning

Praise be to the Guru who is the Supreme Lord. Lord Sai is my own Guru. O Lord, always be my constant companion. You are the light of eternal Truth. Please never go away from me, and always be by my side.
