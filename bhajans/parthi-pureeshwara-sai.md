## Lyrics

Parthi Pureeshwara Sai Allah Maulah Sai
Gurudware Mey Nanak Sai
Girjaghar Mey Yesu Sai
Buddha Zohrashtra Mahavir Sai
Sarva Dharma Priya Sri Sathya Sai

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, the Lord of Puttaparthi, You are verily Allah. You reside in Gurudwaras as Nanak and in churches as Lord Jesus Christ. You are none other than Buddha, Zohrashtra and Mahavira. Glory to Thee, O Lord Sathya Sai who fosters all religions.
