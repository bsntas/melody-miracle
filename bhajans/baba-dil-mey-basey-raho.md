## Lyrics

Baba Dil Mey Basey Raho
Janam Janam Mey Saath Raho
Haath Na Chodo Anatha Natha
Saath Na Chodo Sai Prabho (Merey)
Terey Charan Mey Lelo Sai Prabho

## Meaning

Baba, please install thee in our hearts, be with us for all births. Oh supreme Lord Sai! Do not leave our hands and deprive us from your grace and always place us at your lotus feet.
