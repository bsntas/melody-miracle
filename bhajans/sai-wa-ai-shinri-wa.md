## Lyrics

Sai wa Ai, Shinri wa Ibuki, Shifuku wa Ikiru Kate
Sono Iki Kata ga Tsutaeru Ai
Riyuu wa Naku, Toki ni Yorazu, Kawaranu Shin no Ai

Tatae yo Shifuku he Michibiku Keshin

## Meaning

Sai is Love, Truth is Your breath, Bliss is the food that keeps us alive. This is the way of living to convey true love. Your love is not bound by reason or by time, and it is a constant truth. You gently bring out our Dharma, Love, and Truth which brings us bliss. Shirdi Sai, Sathya Sai, Prema Sai, You are everything. We praise Your heavenly incarnation who guides us to supreme bliss.
