## Lyrics

Jaya Guru Govinda Sadguru Govinda
Madhava Govinda Paahi Maam
Deena Dayala Baba Deena Natha
Parama Dayala Hari Prashanthi Vasa

## Meaning

Protect us O Sacred Preceptor Sai, You are verily Madhava and Govinda (names of Krishna). You are the refuge of the forlorn, the extremely merciful Lord who dwells at Prashanthi Nilayam.
