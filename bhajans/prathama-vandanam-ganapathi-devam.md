## Lyrics

Prathama Vandanam Ganapathi Devam
Nithyam Bhajarey Vinayakam (2)
Pranavakaram Parvathi Sutham
Sri Sai Natham Gananatham [ Nithyam Bhajarey ... ]

## Meaning

Offer your first prayer (obeisance) to Lord Ganapathi (Ganesha), chant incessantly the name of Vinayaka. He is the embodiment of Omkara (Pranava, the primordial sound) and the beloved son of Mother Parvathi. He is none other than our Lord Sainatha.
