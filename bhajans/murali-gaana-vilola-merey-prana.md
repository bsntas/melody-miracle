## Lyrics

Murali Gaana Vilola Merey Prana Gopala
Murahara Madhava Maya Vesha
Meera Ke Prabhu Giridhara Naagara

## Meaning

Salutations to you Oh Lord Krishna who is fond of music on the flute, you are my life Oh Lord Gopala, you are the Lord of Meera.
