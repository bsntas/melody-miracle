## Lyrics

Nanda Nanda Mukunda Hey Sri Hari
Indiresha Yadu Nandana Hari
Manda Haasa Madhusudhana Hari
Sundarakara Sayeesha Sri Hari

## Meaning

O Lord Krishna, the son and joy of Nanda, You are the child of the Yadavas and the bestower of liberation. You are the Lord of Indra. You are the one with a pleasant smile and the one who killed the demon Madhu. You are the one who has taken the beautiful form of Lord Sai.
