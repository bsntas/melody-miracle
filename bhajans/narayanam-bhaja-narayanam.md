## Meaning

Chant the name of the lotus-eyed Lord Sathya Narayana (Sai, Vishnu), who dispels devotees' grief and suffering. He destroys ignorance (darkness) and bestows intelligence (knowledge). Let us devotees always surrender to this treasure house of compassion, Lord Sai.
