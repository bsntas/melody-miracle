## Meaning

How much we love You is something that You alone know, dear Lord Sai. You Sai, are the rhythm of our heart - our heartbeat itself. You are our life. We love You so much.  Our life without You, Is like the plight of a fish without water. Proximity to You is our greatest wealth. One glance from You is as dear as life itself. Only one bond exists between You and us, and that is the bond of love.
