## Lyrics

Kagari naki Ai to Hikari wo Warera ni Atae Tamou
Michibiki tamae Watashi no Sai Rama
Buddha Krishna Subete no Kamisama Warera wo Sukui Tamou
Tada Hitotsu no Ai no Chikara de
Sri Sathya Sai Ai no Hikari yo Towa ni Kagayaki Tamou
Yasashiki Haha yo Watashi no Sai Rama

## Meaning

O Sai, You are the Lord who gives us love and light without end. You guide us down the right path. Whether You are called Buddha or Krishna, You are the one being who always helps us with the power of love. Kind Mother Sai, Your light and love are endlessly brilliant.
