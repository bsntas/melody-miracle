## Lyrics

Anata no Miashi ni Watashi no Ai wo Sasagemasu
Sai Baba Sai Baba Anata wa Buddha, Kannon, Iesu
O Baba O Baba Anata wa Haha, Anata wa Chichi
Subete no Mono ni Yadoru, Subete no Kami wa Hitotsu (Oh)

## Meaning

We pray at Your divine feet dear Lord. You are Sai Baba, Buddha, Jesus, and the Kannon Bodhisattva. You are our mother and father. All names are one, and all forms are one as You dwell in everything.
