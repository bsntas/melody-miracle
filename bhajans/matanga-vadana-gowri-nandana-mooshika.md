## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Ganesha, the elephant-faced son of Mother Gowri and Lord Shiva, whose vehicle is the mouse. Supremely pure son of Shiva fulfills all wishes and removes sins and obstacles. Lord Sai residing in Puttaparthi, is the embodiment of Lord Ganesha.
