## Lyrics

Sri Gopala Gokula Bala
Tulasi Mala Sai Gopala [ Sri Gopala ... ]
Radha Vallabha Raasa Vilola [ Tulasi Mala ]

## Meaning

Pray to Lord Sai Gopala of Gokula who is the beloved of Radha. He wears an ornament made from the beads of holy Tulasi plant.
