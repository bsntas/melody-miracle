# Sri Rama Sai Rama Jaya

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Rama, Guru |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Sri Rama Sai Rama Jaya Jaya Rama
Hey Rama O Rama Omkara Rama
Parabrahma Param Dhama Prashanthi Rama
Shirdeesha Partheesha Sadguru Rama

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Sai Rama of Shirdi and Parthi. He is the true Guru (Preceptor). He is the embodiment of 'Om'. He is verily the Supreme Soul whose abode is Prashanthi Nilayam.
