## Meaning

Oh Lord Narayana! the author of Vedas! You are of ever renewing beauty Oh Narayana! You recline on the Lotus in the Ocean of milk You are lotus-eyed You have the beautiful form, with a blue color like the clouds, You are the Avatar or incarnation of this Kali age ( Sai ).
