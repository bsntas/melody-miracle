## Meaning

O Auspicious Lord Sai, You are our Mother, You are Lord Govinda (Krishna) and You are the Bhagwad Geeta. You are our Father, O Supreme Lord. You are also our child and kin, O loving and beautiful Lord Sai. You are the embodiment of Shakthi (Parvathi) and is verily Lord Shankara (Shiva).
