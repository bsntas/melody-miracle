## Lyrics

Raghupathi Ram Jaya Jaya Ram
Dasharatha Nandana Sri Raghu Ram [ Raghupathi Ram ... ]
Sita Mohana Janaki Ram
Pavana Naam Mangala Dham
Jaya Jagavandana Prashanthi Ram

## Meaning

Glory and victory to Prince of King Dasharatha, Lord Rama of the Raghu Dynasty. He is dearest to Sita (Janaki). Reciting His name is purifying and helps one to attain the auspicious abode. He has incarnated as Lord Sathya Sai, the Lord of Prashanthi Nilayam who is adored by the entire world.
