## Lyrics

Neela Megha Shyamala Roopa Sai Gaana Lola Gokula Bala
Geeta Quran Bible Mey Tera Rang Hai
Brahma Vishnu Maheshwara Tera Roop Hai
Sathya Dharma Shanthi Prem Sab Ko Diya
Hum Terey Charanon Mey Sharan Liya (Baba)

## Meaning

Lord Sai, who loves music, bestows the bliss as conferred upon Gopis by the dark-hued child of Gokula, Lord Krishna. All celestial books like Quran, Bible and Gita are filled with Your hue. Even the trinity of Brahma, Vishnu and Maheshwara are Your manifestations. You shower Truth, Right Conduct, Peace and Love on us to emulate by taking refuge at Your lotus feet. O Baba, this surrender is our submission for redemption.
