## Lyrics

Madhava Madhusudhana (Hey)
Yadava Kula Bhooshana Harey Krishna [ (Hey) Madhava ... ]
Jagat Palana Jaganmohana Hey Nandana

## Meaning

Praise be to Lord Krishna, the annihilator of demon Madhu, the ornament of Yadava Clan, son of Nanda and the one who enchants and nourishes the Universe.
