## Meaning

O Lord, (Govinda) the finder of the cows (Vedas), (Radhe), the supremely charming, beautiful . O mind! sing the glorious names of Lord Murali, the one who is holding the flute, Lord Shyam the one who has a dark blue complexion, Radhey Gopala, the beloved of goddess Radhe.
