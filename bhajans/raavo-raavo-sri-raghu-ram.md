## Meaning

Oh Sri Rama! Won't you come to us and protect us? O Rama of the Bhadrachala temple, the destroyer of the cycle of birth and death, help us. Protect me, Oh Rama, who bears the Bow and Arrow (Kodanda). Oh Lord Sri Sai Rama - the one with the holy name.
