## Lyrics

Mana Mohana Hey Giridhari Shyama

## Meaning

Krishna otherwise known as Shyama is the charmer of Minds, the one who held aloft the mountain, is the Lord of Mathura and ever Handsome. Krishna is an absolute pretender and has dubious ways, a treat to the eyes who is none other than Sri Sai Rama.
