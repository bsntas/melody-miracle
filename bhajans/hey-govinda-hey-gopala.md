## Lyrics

Hey Govinda Hey Gopala

Radhey Shyam Muralidhari

## Meaning

Sing the attributes of Lord Krishna (Gopala, Govinda), the one with dark complexion, having an enchanting form, who adorns the Conch, the Disc and the Mace. Praises be to the one who is dear to Meera and Radha and who holds the Flute in His hands.
