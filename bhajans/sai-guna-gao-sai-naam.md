## Lyrics

Sai Guna Gao Sai Naam Gao

## Meaning

Oh Man, Sing the Name of the Lord and His Virtues. The name of Sai confers infinite bliss. Chant and contemplate on this name. Hear and meditate on the Sai name. Sing about the name of Sai.
