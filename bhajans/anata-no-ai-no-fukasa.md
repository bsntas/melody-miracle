## Meaning

O Lord Sai, Your love is as boundless as Your eternal form. You are the root of the love that is in our hearts. We dedicate our lives to following Your love. Without You, we cannot continue living. Your love, as deep as the oceans, is what gives us our life. This pure love is what binds us together.
