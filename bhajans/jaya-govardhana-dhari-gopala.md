## Lyrics

Jaya Govardhana Dhari Gopala
Radha Hrudaya Vihari Gopala [ Jaya Govardhana ... ]
Janam Maran Bhaya Haari Gopala
Meera Prabhu Giridhari Gopala
Parthi Vihari Sai Gopala

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Gopala (Krishna) who lifted the Govardhana Hill to protect His devotees. Victory to the Lord who resides in Radha's heart. Victory to the one who destroys the fear of birth and death, who is the Lord of Meera and who is verily Lord Sai residing at Puttaparthi.
