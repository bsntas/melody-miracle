## Lyrics

Sai Rama Jagadeeshwara
Sarva Bhauma Sarveshwara [ Sai Rama ... ]

Shambho Mahadeva Shiva Shankara

## Meaning

O Lord Sai Rama, You are the greatest of the Emperors and the Lord of the Universe, full of virtues, adored by the entire humanity. O Lord You are none other than the Lord Shankara (Shiva).
