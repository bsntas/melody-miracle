## Lyrics

Ganga Jatadhara Gowri Mahesha
Shiva Shiva Shankara Shaila Girisha [ Ganga Jatadhara Gowri ... ]
Parvathi Ramana Paahi Mahesha
Paahi Mahesha Hey Paramesha
Parthi Pureeshwara Sai Mahesha (Sarvesha)

## Meaning

The one who is holding goddess Ganga in his matted locks, please protect us. Lord Mahesha, who is the lord of goddess Gowri, please protect us. The one residing in the Shaila mountain, having goddess Ganga in his matted hair, lord Shiva, who is the beloved of goddess Parvathi please protect us. O supreme lord Eshwara, who lives in Puttaparthi, please protect us. Lord residing in Puttaparthi is lord Sai, who is everywhere.
