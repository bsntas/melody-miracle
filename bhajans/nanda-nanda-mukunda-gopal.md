## Lyrics

Nanda Nanda Mukunda Gopal
Nanda Mukunda Hari Shyama Gopal
Radha Manasa Krishna Gopal
Murali Manohara Mukunda Gopal
Parthi Pureeshwara Sai Gopal Sathya Sai Gopal

## Meaning

You are the son and joy of Nanda and the bestower of liberation. You are the cowherd boy Krishna. Oh blue colored Lord! You are the One Who resides in the heart of the cowherd girl Radha (symbolizes ardent devotee). You are the One Who plays the flute and conquers the mind. You are the One Who is the Lord of Puttaparthi and also known as Lord Sathya Sai Gopal.
