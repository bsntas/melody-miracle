## Lyrics

Sri Mata Jaganmata
Sai Mata Shashi Gowri Mata
Dharani Mata Paripoorani Mata

## Meaning

O Mother Sai, the mother of the Universe, the mother of auspiciousness, the one who fulfills all our wishes and liberate us from this materialistic world, who is none other than the Mother Earth, our salutations to You.
