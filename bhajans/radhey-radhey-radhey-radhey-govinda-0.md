## Lyrics

## Meaning

Chant the names of the Lord of Radha, Lord Govinda, the one who is the son of Nanda, Lord Mukunda, who liberates the soul, Giridhara the holder of the mountain and Lord Gopala, the enchanter with the flute. He is the bearer of the conch, disc and mace (Lord Vishnu). Sing the praise of Radhey Gopal.
