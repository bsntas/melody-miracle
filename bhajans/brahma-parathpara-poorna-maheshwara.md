## Lyrics

Brahma Parathpara Poorna Maheshwara
Adi ( Sai ) Natha Sri Kedareshwara
Bilva Dala Priya Chandrakaladhara
Dhimi Dhimi Damaru Ninaada Kara (3)

## Meaning

Worship the supreme Brahman who has taken the form of Maheshwara with full divine qualities. He is the eternal Lord from beginning resident of Kedareshwara, who loves worship with Bilva leaves, adorn with moon on the head and who dances to the rhythm of Damaru.
