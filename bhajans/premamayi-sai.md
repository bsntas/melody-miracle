## Meaning

Lord Sai, Love personified, Sai, the Cosmic consciousness, Sai, who endows one with bliss, Sai, who preaches the ultimate and the highest Truth, Sai, who is blemishless, Sai, Present in moving and non - moving objects and creator of illusion (Maya) of this world.
