## Lyrics

Gopala Gokula Bala
Nandalala Goparipala [ Gopala Gokula ... ]
Shyama Govinda Krishna Gopala
Raasa Vilola Venu Gopala
Nanda Nanda Ke Mukunda Bala
Kamala Vadana Sai Krishna Gopala

## Meaning

Sing the Glory of Lord Gopala (Krishna), the beloved son of Nanda of Gokula. He is the dark-hued Govinda, the protector of the cows and the one who performed the Raasa Leela. He plays Divine Music with His Flute and is the Liberator of all. He is none other than the lotus-faced Lord Sai.
