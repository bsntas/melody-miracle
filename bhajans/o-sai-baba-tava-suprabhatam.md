## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Sai |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

O Sai Baba Tava Suprabhatam
Mangala Maya Baba Prathah Smaranam [ O Sai ... ]
Sathya Sanathana Tum Ho Baba
Sarva Dharma Priya Sai Baba

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, we greet You early at the break of dawn to remind ourselves of Your auspiciousness. You are the eternal truth, and You foster and love all religions and faiths.
