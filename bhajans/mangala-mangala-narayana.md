## Meaning

Sing the glory of the most auspicious Lord Narayana, the one who fosters the Vedas, the protector and sustainer of the devotees. Praise be to the Lord who incarnated as ten different forms (Mathsya, Kurma etc.). He has now incarnated in Kaliyuga as Sri Sathya Sai, the supreme personality of Godhead, the very embodiment of love.
