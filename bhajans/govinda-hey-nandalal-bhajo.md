## Lyrics

Govinda Hey Nandalal Bhajo

## Meaning

Chant and glorify the beloved of Nanda, who is the little Shyam of Brindavan and the beloved of Radha. Victory to the Lord of Radha who is none other than our beloved Lord Sai.
