## Lyrics

Krishna Bhajo Krishna Bhajo Murali Gopal
Radhey Govinda Bhajo Radhey Gopal
Nandatmaja Nanda Navaneetha Chora
Ghana Shyama Radha Karuna Kataksha [ Radhey Govinda ... ]

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Krishna who plays the enchanting flute, who fosters cows and people. He is Govinda with dark blue-complexion and the compassionate beloved of Radha. He is the son of Nanda and the stealer of butter.
