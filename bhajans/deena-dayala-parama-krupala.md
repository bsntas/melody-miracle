## Lyrics

Deena Dayala Parama Krupala
Darshana Deejo Sai Gopala [ Deena Dayala ... ]
Parthi Pureeshwara Sai Nandalala
Bhaktha Paripala Sai Gopala

## Meaning

Please let us have the vision of Your divine form, O Lord Gopala (Krishna), son of Nanda. You are the Lord who is extremely compassionate towards the downtrodden and protects His devotees. You are verily Lord Sai.
