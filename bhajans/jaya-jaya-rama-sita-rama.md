## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Rama Sita Rama
Bandha Vimochana Jaya Raghu Rama [ Jaya Jaya ... ]
Ravikula Mandana Atma Rama
Asura Nikhandana Kodanda Rama

## Meaning

Victory to the consort of Sita, Lord Rama of Raghu clan, who releases all from bondage, who is the indweller, the jewel of Solar dynasty, the one wielding the Kodanda bow and the one who wiped out the demonic clan.
