## Lyrics

Itsuka Dokoka de Atta Omoi, Tooi Kanata no Natsukashii Omoi

## Meaning

I have these thoughts all the time no matter where I go, distant, beloved thoughts. These kind thoughts wrap around me all the time, these thoughts of Your divine love. O Lord, please come to us. Sai Baba, we love You.
