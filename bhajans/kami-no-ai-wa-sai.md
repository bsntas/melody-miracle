## Meaning

O Lord Sai, Your love is like the love of a mother. You present Your precious love to us, suffering children. You eliminate the illusion from this world so that we may journey towards You. You give us guidance with Your kind love that spreads through our hearts. Mother Sai, You live within all things in the universe.
