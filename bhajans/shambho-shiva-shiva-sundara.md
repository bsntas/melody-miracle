## Meaning

Glory to the beautiful Lord Shiva, who resides on Mount Kailasa. He is the Lord of all the cosmos, the Supreme Being who is worshipped as the destroyer. The Lord of compassion, He is none other than our Lord Sathya Sai of Puttaparthi.
