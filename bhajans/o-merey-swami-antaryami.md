## Lyrics

O Merey Swami Antaryami
Antar Jyothi Jalao Sai
Tu Merey Pitha Tu Hai Meri Mata
Tu Merey Bandhu Tu Merey Daata
Tu Merey Malik Mein Tera Balak
Daya Karo Merey Sai Natha

## Meaning

Kindle the light of wisdom in my heart, O Lord Sai, the indweller of hearts. You are my father, mother, kith and kin, master and my sustainer. I am Thine, O Lord, please be compassionate towards me.
