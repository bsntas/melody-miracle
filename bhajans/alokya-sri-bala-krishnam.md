## Lyrics

Alokya Sri Bala Krishnam
Ananda Sundara Tandava Krishnam
Neela Megha Shyama Sundara Krishnam
Nanda Sunandadi Vanditha Krishnam

## Meaning

Oh Bala Krishna! you are unworldly and while dancing on the Kalinga serpent, you look beautiful and blissful, presenting the cloud like complexion adored by Nanda and Sunanda.
