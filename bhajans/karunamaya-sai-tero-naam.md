## Lyrics

Karunamaya Sai Tero Naam
Premamaya Sai Pavana Naam
Sundara Roopa Sulabha Prasanna
Siddhi Pradatha Sri Sai Natha
Sharanam Sharanam Sadguru Deva

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, Your name is filled with compassion, love and purity. You are endowed with a beautiful form and You are easily pleased. You bestow spiritual success, O revered Lord Sai. We seek refuge in You, O true Guru.
