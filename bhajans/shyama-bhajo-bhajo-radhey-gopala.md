## Lyrics

Shyama Bhajo Bhajo Radhey Gopala
Shyama Manohara He Nandalala [ Shyama Bhajo ... ]
Murali Manohara Gopi Lola
Meera Ke Prabhu Giridhara Bala

## Meaning

Worship Gopala, the Lord of Radha. Lord Krishna, the dark-complexioned son of Nanda plays the enchanting flute. He is the beloved of the Gopis, and the Lord of Meera. He held the mountain up to protect His devotees.
