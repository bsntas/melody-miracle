## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Ganesha, who removes all obstacles. We pray to Lord Sathya Sai who upholds human values to grants welfare to humanity. Lord Ganesha is the bestower of education, intelligence and success. He is the son of Lord Shiva and Mother Parvathi and is the brother of Lord Subrahmanya. Our salutations to the greatest Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi.
