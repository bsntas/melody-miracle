## Lyrics

Hari Hari Govinda Hari Hari Giridhara Bala Hari Hari Shyama Gopala
Madhava Hari Keshava Hari
Yadava Hari Nandalala (Sai)

## Meaning

Worship Lord Hari in the form of Govinda, the dark-complexioned Krishna who lifted the Govardhana hill with His little finger, Madhava, Keshava, who incarnated in the Yadava lineage as the son of Nanda.
