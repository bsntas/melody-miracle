# Bhajagovindham Bhajagovindham

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/bhajagovindham-bhajagovindham)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Rama |

## Lyrics

Bhaja Govindam Bhaja Govindam
Bhaja Govindam Hare Hare
Bhaja Govindam Bala Mukundam
Paramanandam Hare Hare
Sri Ram Ram Jaya Raghu Rama
Sri Raghu Nandana Sita Rama
Hari Narayana Bhaja Narayana
Sai Narayana Hare Hare
Sathya Narayana Hare Hare

## Meaning

Chant incessantly the names of Govinda, Hari, Mukunda, Rama, Narayana and Sai which will give us supreme bliss.
