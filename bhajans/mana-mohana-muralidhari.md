## Lyrics

Mana Mohana Muralidhari
Krishna Krishna
Radhey Radhey

Ghana Shyam Ghana Shyam Jaya Sai Harey (2)

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Krishna, the one who attracts the mind, who holds the Flute and who is adored by Radha. Lord Krishna's complexion is that of dark thick clouds and He is the one who slayed the demon Madhu. Victory to Lord Sai who is verily Lord Krishna.
