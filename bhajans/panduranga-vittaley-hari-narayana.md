## Lyrics

## Meaning

Oh Lord Panduranga, the dweller of Pandharpur, thou art Lord Hari. Oh Lord of Pandharpur, Oh Sathya Narayana, the enchanting director of the drama of life, victory to You, Oh Sai Vittala.
