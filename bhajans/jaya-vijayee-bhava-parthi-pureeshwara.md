## Lyrics

Jaya Vijayee Bhava Parthi Pureeshwara
Jaya Bhakthashraya Krupa Maya
Jaya Jaya Jaya Sarva Jagat Rakshaka
Jaya Jaya Jaya Janardhana
Jaya Jaya Jaya Sayeeshwara

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Sai, who dwells at Parthi who has utmost compassion for His devotees. He is the Protector of the whole Universe and the motivator for His followers.
