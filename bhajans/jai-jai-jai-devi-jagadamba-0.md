## Meaning

Sing the Glory of the Universal Mother riding the Lion, the Energy Principle, causing Auspiciousness, the consort of Shankara and the most beautiful of all the three worlds. Oh Mother, You dwell at Kanchi as Kamakshi, as Meenakshi in Madurai and as Vishalakshi in Kashi. Your Eyes are full of compassion. We offer ourselves at Your feet for You are verily Saraswathi (Knowledge), Wealth (Lakshmi) and Supreme Energy (Durga).
