## Lyrics

Mana Mohana Muralidhari Sri Hari
Sai Narayana Sai Narayana
Radha Vallabha Gaana Vilola [ Mana Mohana ... ]
Giridhara Bala Shyama Gopala
Govardhana Giridhari Krishna
Eshwaramba Nandana Sai Krishna
Karuna (Prema) Sagara Parthi Krishna

## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Krishna, the enchanting one who holds the flute and who steals the hearts of devotees. O Lord, You are the beloved of Radha. You are the one who revels in divine music and is immersed in bliss. O youthful dark-hued Gopala, the one who held aloft the Govardhana mountain to protect His devotees, You are none other than the beloved son of Mother Eshwaramba, Lord Sai, who is an ocean of compassion and divine love. Chant the Holy names of Lord Narayana, Krishna and Sai.
