## Lyrics

Sundara Vadana Gopala
Nayana Sunayana Hey Nandalala [Sundara Vadana ... ]
Meera Ke Prabhu Giridhara Bala
Radha Jeevana Sai Gopala

## Meaning

Oh Lord Gopala (Krishna), You are most enchanting with beautiful eyes. Oh son of Nanda, Lord of Meera and the very life of Radha, You are none other than Sai.
