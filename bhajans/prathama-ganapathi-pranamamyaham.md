## Lyrics

Prathama Ganapathi Pranamamyaham
Parvathi Priya Sutha Bhajamyaham
Ashvatha Moola Vasa Devadi Deva
Parthi Vinayaka Paahi Gajanana
Bhaktha Priyam Bhava Bhaya Nashanam
Vande Aham Gana Nayakam

## Meaning

Let us offer our primary worship and sing the praises of Lord Ganesha, the beloved son of Mother Parvathi. He is the source of all and the Lord of Lords. He is verily Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi who protects His devotees. He protects and removes their worldly fears and obstacles. Salutations to the Lord of the Ganas.
