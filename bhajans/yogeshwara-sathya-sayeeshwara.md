## Lyrics

Yogeshwara Sathya Sayeeshwara
Parthipurisha Pavana
Patitodhara Sai Ram
Amrutha Roopa Ananda Daata
Eshwari Nandana Sai Ram
Jagadodhara Sai Ram
Sri Sathya Sai Swagatham

## Meaning

Welcome Lord Sai, master of the Saints, holy Lord of the Parthi and son of the Mother Eswaramma. You are the uplifter of the weak, personification of the Eternal who grants bliss and protects the Universe.
