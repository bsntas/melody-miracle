## Lyrics

Parthi Pureeshwari Ma Jaya Sai Maheshwari Ma
Dukha Nivarani Bhava Bhaya Harini
Abhaya Pradayini Ma ( Sai )
Jaya Ma Sai Ma (3)
Omkara Roopini Ma (2)

## Meaning

Victory to the mother of Parthi, who is the incarnation of Mother Eshwari. She removes the afflictions of worldly sorrow and fear; And grants us fearlessness. Victory to that Mother Sai, who is the embodiment of Omkara.
