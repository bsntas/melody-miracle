## Meaning

O Lord Sai, You are the blemishless Lord in human form. O Lord Sai, You are beyond attributes, as well as the source of all attributes. You are the master of the entire universe and beyond the universe. You are the refuge of downtrodden and the entire creation. We bow to Thee O Lord Sai.
