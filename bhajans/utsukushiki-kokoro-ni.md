## Lyrics

Utsukushiki Kokoro ni
Sumita mo Yasashiki Kami Itooshii Krishna
Sekai Chuu no Hito ni
Ai wo Ataeta mo
Sai Krishna Anata no Fue no Ne ni Nosete

## Meaning

O Lord Krishna, You dwell in our hearts with Your kindness and beauty. We sing along with the sound of Your beautiful flute. Glory to Lord Sai who is verily Lord Krishna.
