## Lyrics

Shiva Shiva Sai Shankara Roopa
Shiva Parameshwara Samba Shiva
Hara Hara Hara Hara Bhola Shankara
Shirdi (Parthi) Pureeshwara Sai Shiva
Samba Shiva Sai Shiva (2)

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Sai who is the embodiment of Lord Shankara (Shiva). He is worshipped by many as Lord Parameshwara, Samba Shiva and Bhola Shankara. The One who took the form of the Lord of Shirdi is now with us as the Lord of Parthi.
