## Lyrics

Govardhana Giridhara Gopika Manohara
Mohana Muralidhara Sathya Sai Natavara [ Govardhana Giridhara ... ]
Vasudeva Giridhara Brindavana Sanchara
Keshava Damodara Sathya Sai Natavara

## Meaning

Glory to Thee, O Lord Krishna, You lifted the mountain to save people of Gokula, You are the beloved of Gopikas, You are the enchanting flute player and a delightful dancer. You are the one wandering in Brindavan. You are also known as Keshava (destroyer of demon Keshi) and Damodara. Lord Sathya Sai of Parthi is none other than You.
