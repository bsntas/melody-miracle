## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Ganesha always and constantly remember the Lord of all, the one of valor and the ocean of compassion who removes the fear of birth and death. Glory to the brother of Lord Guha (Lord Subrahmanya), who is an ocean of compassion to His devotees.
