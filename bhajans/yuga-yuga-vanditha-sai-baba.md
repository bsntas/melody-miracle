## Lyrics

Yuga Yuga Vanditha Sai Baba
Sharanam Sharanam Shata Koti Sharanam

Sai Ram Sai Ram (3)

## Meaning

Oh Baba, Millions surrendered at your feet during the past several eons. In the age of TRETA you were Rama, In the age of DWAPARA you were Krishna and in this age of KALI you are Sathya Sai Baba Oh my Sai Ram.
