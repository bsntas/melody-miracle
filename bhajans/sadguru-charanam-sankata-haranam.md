## Meaning

Meditate on the Holy Feet of the Universal Preceptor, Lord Sai, they will protect you from harm. They are the Feet of the True eternal charioteer. Always chant and sing of them. Sweet is the remembrance of Thy name - Sai. Thy Lotus Feet are the abode of peace.
