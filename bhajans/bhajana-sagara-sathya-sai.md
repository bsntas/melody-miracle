## Meaning

The name of Lord Sathya Sai itself gives rise to an entire ocean of Bhajans. In the refuge of that Name, we surrender ourselves completely. O Lord Sai, Your name dwells in everything in this universe, bringing sweetness to all. In the temple of love, the music of Your name resounds, evoking supreme joy, bliss, and happiness. We surrender to the one with the Holy Name, Lord Sai.
