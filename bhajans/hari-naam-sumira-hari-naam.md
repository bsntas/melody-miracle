## Lyrics

Hari Naam Sumira Hari Naam Sumira Hari Naam Sumira Bhajorey
Tribhuvana Nayaka Sab Sukha Dayaka
Vinati Sahayaka Naam Japorey

## Meaning

Chant and remember the name of Lord Hari (Narayana), the Lord of the Universe who bestows goodness and happiness. Chant the name of the Lord who helps the devotees who surrender to Him.
