## Lyrics

Sai Krishna Sai Krishna Sai Gopala
Seguire tu Luz me hundire en el Ti.
Reunire las fuerzas para poderte seguir.
Guia mi camino. Alumbra mi vivir.
No permitas nunca que me aleje de ti.
Conduceme Senor Sai hacia Ti.
Sai Krishna Sai Krishna Sai Gopala.

## Meaning

O Sai Krishna, Sai Gopala, I will follow Your light; I will meditate on You; I will gather all my strength to follow You.  Guide my path and enlighten my life. Don't let me be far from You. O Lord, guide me towards You.
