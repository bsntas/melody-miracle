## Meaning

Lord Ranga, reclining on the bed of snake has now incarnated at Parthi as Lord Sai. He is the same as the one who scaled the Universe in three steps (during the incarnation as Lord Vamana), and the dark-hued Krishna. Glory to the one who is the source of everything.
