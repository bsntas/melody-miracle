## Lyrics

Naman Karoon Mein Guru Charanam

## Meaning

I bow down to the feet of the Divine Preceptor Sai. O Lord Sai, the eternal Guru, I surrender to Thy feet. I surrender to the feet of Lord Vishnu and to the feet of Lord Shiva.
