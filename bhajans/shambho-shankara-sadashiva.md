## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Sai of Parthi who is none other than Lord Shiva (Shambho, Shankara, Sadashiva). Salutations to the one who holds the crescent Moon on His head and the mighty Ganges in His matted Locks, to the consort of mother Parvathi and to the Lord of the Universe.
