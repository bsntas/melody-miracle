## Lyrics

Raja Rajeshwari Rajya Pradayini
Raakshasa Nashini Palaya Maam
Sri Bhuvaneshwari Tripura Sundari
Shiva Shakthi Roopini Palaya Maam

## Meaning

Save us, O Mother, the Goddess of emperors, donor of kingdoms, destroyer of the demon race, the Goddess of the entire world, the most beautiful of all the three worlds. Protect us, the one who is the embodiment of Shiva, the male aspect and the Shakthi, the female aspect of God.
