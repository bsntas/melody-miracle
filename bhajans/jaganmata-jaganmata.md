## Lyrics

Jaganmata Jaganmata
Devi Bhavani Hey Sai Mata [ Jaganmata Jaganmata ]
Mata Maheshwari Girija Mata
Parthi Pureeshwari Parama Dayakari
Devi Bhavani Jai Sai Mata

## Meaning

The Universal Mother is known by several names as Devi Bhavani, Maheshwari and Girija. She is the extremely compassionate Mother Sai who dwells at Parthi.
