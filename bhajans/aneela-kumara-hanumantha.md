## Meaning

Glory to Hanuman, the son of the wind God, Vayu and Anjani. He is the most powerful leader of the monkey army with innumerable qualities. He is the foremost devotee and messenger of Lord Rama who protects the pious and the holy people. Glory to the most powerful protector.
