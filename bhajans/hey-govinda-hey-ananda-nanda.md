## Lyrics

Hey Govinda Hey Ananda Nanda Gopala
Mohana Muralidhara Shyama Gopala [ Hey Govinda ... ]
Mandara Giridhari Harey Natavara Lala
Madhava Keshava Madana Gopala

## Meaning

Glory to the Lord Gopala (Krishna), son of Nanda. He is ever blissful, having an enchanting form. Glory be to the one who holds the Flute in His hands, absolute pretender, who lifted the Mandara mountain in order to save the Yadavas from the wrath of the Rain God.
