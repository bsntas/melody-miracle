Shankari Putra Shubhanana (Shiva) (2)

## Meaning

Glory to the Lord of the Ganaas (attendants of lord Shiva), the elephant-faced Ganesha, the one who is full of compassion, beautiful and who is the remover of obstacles. He is the son of Lord Shiva and Shankari (Parvathi) and the one with an auspicious face.
