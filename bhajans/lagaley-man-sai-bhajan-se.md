## Lyrics

Lagaley Man Sai Bhajan Se Preet
Mata Pitha Sutha Bandhava Naari
Sab Do Din Ke Meet Samajh Man
Sai Bhajan Se Preet
Sai Prabho Nija Bandhu Tumharo
Sada Rahey Tero Meet Samajh Man
Bolo Ram Ghana Shyam
Sai Ram Sai Ram

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Sai Ram, who is the real friend and kin, who will always be with you. Hook your mind to the loving Sai Bhajans. All your kith and kin, like, mother, father, children and spouse are just friends for few moments only.
