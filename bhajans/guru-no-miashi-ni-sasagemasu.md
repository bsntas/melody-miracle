## Meaning

We devote ourselves and sacrifice everything at Your lotus feet my Lord. O Lord Sathya Sai, Prema Sai, please grant our prayers. No matter what, we always pray to our Loving Guru who is Baba, Buddha, Jesus, and Sri Sai Rama. We offer everything to You.
