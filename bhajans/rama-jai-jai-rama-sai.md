## Meaning

Glory be to Rama, the lotus eyed One, the handsome King, Glory be to Sai Rama. Rama is the personification of Truth; grants Peace and freedom from fear. Glory to the One of nectarine words. Rama, the refuge of the forlorn, Lord Sai Rama.
