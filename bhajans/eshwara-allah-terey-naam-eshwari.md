## Lyrics

Eshwara Allah Terey Naam Eshwari Nandana Sai Ram
Krishna Christhu Terey Naam Karuna Sagara Sai Ram
Rama Raghava Terey Naam Shirdi Pureeshwara Sai Ram
Krishna Keshava Terey Naam Karuna Sagara Sai Shyam

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, You are adored as Lord Shiva and Allah. You are Sai Ram, the son of mother Eshwaramba. You are none other than Lord Krishna, Jesus Christ, Lord Rama and Lord Sai of Shirdi. You are also known by Keshava and Shyama (names of Lord Krishna) and You are an ocean of compassion.
