## Lyrics

Rama Bhaktha Hanumantha

Sri Rama Daasa (Dootha) Namo Namo (2)

## Meaning

Hanumantha, the dear devotee and servant of Lord Rama. He whose Mother is Anjana and grants freedom from fear to His devotees. He is the son of the Wind god, bestower of boons. Let us offer our obeisance to the servant and messenger of Sri Rama.
