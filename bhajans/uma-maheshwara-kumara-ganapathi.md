## Lyrics

Uma Maheshwara Kumara Ganapathi
Namami Gajamukha Daya Nidhey
Bhaktha Jana Priya Sai Ganesha (2)
Bala Gajanana Krupa Nidhey (2)

## Meaning

I bow to the elephant-faced Lord Ganesha and His entire family, parents Uma (Mother Parvathi) and Maheshwara (Lord Shiva) and brother Kumara (Lord Muruga). The Lord of ganaas (attendants of Lord Shiva) is dear to His devotees and is the repository of benevolence.
