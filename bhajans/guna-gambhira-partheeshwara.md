## Meaning

Lord Sai, the Lord of Puttaparthi, is the salient embodiment of all good qualities. Lord Sathya Sai resides in hearts filled with Love. He is the beloved of devotional singing. He is the personification of bliss. He is the giver of auspiciousness. He resides in Prasanthi Nilayam.
