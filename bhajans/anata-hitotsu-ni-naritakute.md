## Lyrics

Anata to Hitotsu ni Naritakute O Sai
Afureru Omoi wa Sai, Sai, Sai
Inori no Kotoba mo Yume Miru Sugata mo

## Meaning

O Lord, my only wish is to become one with You. You fill my every thought, prayer, and dream with Your beautiful form. Lord Sai Baba, You are Rama, Krishna, Buddha, Jesus.
