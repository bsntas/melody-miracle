## Lyrics

Rama Govinda Harey
Bhajorey Rama Govinda Harey [ Rama Govinda ... ]
Dasharatha Nandana Rama Harey

Gokula Nandana Shyama Harey
Parthi Pureeshwara Sai Harey

## Meaning

Sing in praise of Lord Rama and Lord Govinda (Krishna). Rama, the darling son of Dasharatha, is the the one who annihilated the ten-headed Ravana. Shyama (Krishna) is the dear son of Gokula. Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi is none other than Rama and Krishna.
