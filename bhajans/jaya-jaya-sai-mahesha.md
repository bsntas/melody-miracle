## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Sai Mahesha
Jaya Jaya Gowri Gangadhara
Jaya Jaya Samba Sadashiva
Jaya Jaya Parthi Lingeshwara

## Meaning

Victory to Sai Mahesha, Gangadhara consort of Mother Gowri, Sadashiva the consort of Mother Amba and the Lord of Linga whose abode is Parthi.
