## Lyrics

Radha Madhava Gopalana
Govinda Gopala Narayana
Narayana Hari Narayana (2)
Narayana Veda Parayana

## Meaning

Chant the many names of the Lord Krishna - Madhava, Gopala and Govinda, the protector and sustainer of all beings and the Lord of Radha. Chant the name of Hari and Narayana, the preacher of the Vedas.
