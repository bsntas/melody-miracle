## Lyrics

Mana Mohana Ghana Shyama Gopala
Mathura Natha Venu Gopala [ Mana Mohana Ghana ... ]
Nanda Kishora Navaneetha Chora

## Meaning

Oh the Lord of Mathura, Gopala you lure the minds of human beings, the son of Nanda, you steal the butter of devotion from everybody's heart, Oh Sai Gopala the one dwelling at Parthi.
