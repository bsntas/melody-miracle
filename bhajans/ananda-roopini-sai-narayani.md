| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Devi |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Ananda Roopini Sai Narayani
Himagiri Nandini Ma [ Ananda Roopini ... ]
Triloka Palini Mata Tri Dayini
Tri Jagata Vandini Parthi Nivasini
Sai Bhavani Ma

## Meaning

Glory to Mother Narayani (Parvathi), daughter of the king of Himalayas. She is the protector of the three worlds who is bliss personified and who bestows peace on the three states of existence - body, mind and soul. She is none other than Mother Sai of Parthi who is worshiped by the three worlds.
