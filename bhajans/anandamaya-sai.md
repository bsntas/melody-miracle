## Meaning

Sai, You are verily the embodiment of Bliss, Compassion and Love. Your divine effulgence is equivalent to the splendour of crores of sun. You are pleasantly disposed towards the underprivileged, storehouse of eternal Peace, all pervasive divinity. You are Omnipotent, blemishless and the embodiment of Existence, Awareness and Bliss.
