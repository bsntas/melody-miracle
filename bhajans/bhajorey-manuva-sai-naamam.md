## Lyrics

Bhajorey Manuva Sai Naamam
Parthi Nivasam Pankaja Netram [ Bhajorey ... ]
Prema Swaroopam Sayeeshwaram
Hrudaya Nivasam Sayeeshwaram
Shirdi Pureesham Sayeeshwaram
Poornavataram Sayeeshwaram (Pari)

## Meaning

Oh mind! chant the divine name Sai having eyes like lotus residing in Parthi. He is Sayeeshwara, the embodiment of love reveling in our hearts and resident of Shirdi. He has descended in Parthi with full power of Godhead.
