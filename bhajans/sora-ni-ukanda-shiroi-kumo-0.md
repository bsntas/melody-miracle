## Meaning

White clouds float in the sky. The evening sun comes and dyes the sky red. It becomes like the color of Mother Sai's clothes. My tears flow unceasingly at this sight. We become one with You again. You embrace us tightly in Your divine chest. O Mother Sai, we call out Your beautiful name. Sai Rama, Your kindness echoes throughout the land. I stand and bask in the evening sun, as I become a child of the light. I return to my place alongside my beloved mother, as my love for You rides through the wind.
