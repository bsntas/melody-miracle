Naga Bharana Parvathi Ramana Sai Mahadeva

## Meaning

Praise Lord Sai Shiva, the auspicious one who is called as Mahadeva (the greatest of all the Lords). Lotus-eyed, and merciful, He is adorned with the holy river Ganga and the crescent moon in His matted hair, and serpents around His neck. Parvathi’s Lord residing in the lofty peaks of Himalaya, He is worshipped by sage Narada, Tumburu and Nandi.
