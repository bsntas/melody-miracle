Parthi Pureeshwara Sai Maheshwara

## Meaning

Lord Sai is the Lord in human form. He is Lord Shiva who swallowed the poison (to protect the devotees), the compassionate one with the third eye on His forehead, who performs the cosmic dance to the rhythm of the musical instruments. Lord Sai residing at Parthi is the embodiment of Lord Shiva.
