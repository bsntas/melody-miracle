## Lyrics

Gopala Brindavana Pala
Gopi Lola Gokula Bala
Murali Lola Munijana Pala
Suramuni Vanditha Gopala Bala

## Meaning

Sing the glory of Gopala of Brindavan beloved of Gopis (cow herd women) you are little Krishna of Gokula the enchanting flute player. Worshipped by saints and gods and you are the protector of them.
