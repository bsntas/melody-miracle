## Meaning

Glory to the elephant-faced Lord Ganesha, the Lord of the Ganaas, the son of Lord Shiva. He is none other than Sai, the resident of Parthi, who is compassionate. The pot bellied son of Mother Girija, removes obstacles and grief, confers boons and uses a mouse as a conveyance.
