## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Jaya Ma Jaya Sai Ma
Janana Marana Bhaya Harini Ma [ Jaya Jaya ... ]
Sundara Vadani Suhaasini Ma
Mangala Karini Maheshwari Ma
Mahadeva Mana Mohini Ma
Maya Nashini Sri Sai Ma

## Meaning

Let us sing in Glory of Mother Sai, who negates the fear of the cycle of birth and death. The consort of Maheshwara, has a Beautiful smiling countenance, the One who charms Mahadeva and offsets the effects of Illusion.
