## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Rama, Lord Sai Rama and Lord Raghava. Rama, also known as Raghava, is the Scion of Raghu dynasty, of Solar lineage. Rama who vanquished the demon Ravana, has now incarnated as Sai Rama.
