## Meaning

O God of Gods, Sai Narayana, You are the protector and refuge of the destitute. You are the eternal charioteer. Your supreme state is eternal. You are spotless and unaffected by the world. You are the upholder and sustainer of the universe, the embodiment of effulgence, love and Supreme Peace.
