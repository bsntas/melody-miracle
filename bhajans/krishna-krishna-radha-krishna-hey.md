|---|---|

## Lyrics

Krishna Krishna Radha Krishna Hey Gokula Bala
Hey Gopi Lola
Nanda Kumara Navanita Chora Hey Nandalala
Rama Rama Dasharatha Rama Hey Raja Rama
Hey Sai Rama
Raghukula Bhooshana Rajeeva Nayana Hey Sai Rama

## Meaning

Worship Lord Krishna, the beloved of the Gopis of Gokula, the son of Nanda, and the stealer of butter (our hearts). Worship the lotus-eyed Lord Rama, the jewel of the Raghu dynasty, and the son of King Dasharatha. He is none other than Lord Sai.
