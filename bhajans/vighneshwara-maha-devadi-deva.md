## Lyrics

Jaya Mangala Kara Deva (3)

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Vighneshwara (Lord Ganesha), the Lord of Lords, the destroyer of obstacles in life, the personification of the primordial sound "Om" and the very embodiment of divine light.
