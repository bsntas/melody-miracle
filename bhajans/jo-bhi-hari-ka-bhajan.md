## Lyrics

Jo Bhi Hari Ka Bhajan Karey
Woh Hi Brahma Hari Pad Paavey
Sai Nath Jo Bhajana Karey
Bhava Sagar Se Paar Karey

## Meaning

Those who chant the name of Hari alone reach the position of Brahman (Lord of Universe). Those who chant the name of Sai is sure to cross the cycle of birth and death.
