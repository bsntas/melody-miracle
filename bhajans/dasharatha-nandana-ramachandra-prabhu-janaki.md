## Lyrics

Dasharatha Nandana Ramachandra Prabhu Janaki Jeevana Jaya Jaya Ram
Raghukula Bhooshana Deena Janavana
Patitha Pavana Sita Ram
Raghupathi Raghava Raja Ram
Patitha Pavana Sita Ram

## Meaning

Oh Rama, You are the darling son of King Dasharatha, You are life to Janaki, Janaka's daughter (Goddess Sita), You are the crown jewel of the Raghu Clan and the saviour of the destitute. Oh Lord of Sita, You are the uplifter of the fallen (destitute) and the Lord of the Raghu clan.
