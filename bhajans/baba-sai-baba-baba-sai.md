## Lyrics

Baba Sai Baba, Baba Sai Baba,
Ta voix réchauffe notre coeur,
Ton Amour nous fait danser dans la joie,
Chante, Oh Sai Baba, chante,
Danse, danse, danse, danse, danse dans nos coeurs,
Chante, Oh Sai Baba Chante.

## Meaning

Oh Sai Baba, Your voice warms our hearts. Your love makes us dance in joy. Sing, Oh Sai Baba, sing. Dance, dance, dance, dance, dance in our hearts. Sing, Oh Sai Baba, sing.
