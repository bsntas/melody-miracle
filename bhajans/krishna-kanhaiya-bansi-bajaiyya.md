## Lyrics

Krishna Kanhaiya Bansi Bajaiyya
Paar Karo Merey Jeevana Naiya
Nanda Ji Ke Bala Murali Gopala
Meera Ke Prabhu Giridhara Bala [ Paar Karo ... ]

## Meaning

O Krishna, play the divine Flute, carry the boat of my life across to the other side. You are the son of Nanda, You are the one who played the Flute, lifted the Mountain and the Lord of Meera.
