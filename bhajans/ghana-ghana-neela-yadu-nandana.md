## Lyrics

Ghana Ghana Neela Yadu Nandana
Ghana Shyama Sundara Mana Mohana
Natavara Giridhara Gopalana
Murali Manohara Madhusudhana

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Krishna of the Yadu dynasty, the beautiful dark blue colored one who enchants the mind, the one who lifted the Govardhana mountain, the supreme dancer who fosters cows, the one holding the flute and the one who slayed the demon Madhu.
