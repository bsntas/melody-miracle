## Meaning

The lotus Feet of Mother Sai, who sits on the soft petaled lotus flower, are as holy as the confluence of the three holy rivers (Ganga, Yamuna, Saraswathi) and bestow good fortune to all the three worlds. Total surrender at those Feet will protect us and destroy all of our sins.
