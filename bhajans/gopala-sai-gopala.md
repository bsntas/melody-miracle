# Gopala Sai Gopala

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/gopala-sai-gopala)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Krishna |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Gopala Sai Gopala
Parthi Pureeshwara Gopala
Govinda Govinda Gopala Sai Gopala [ Gopala Sai ... ]
Rhuma Jhuma Rhuma Jhuma Nacho Gopala [ Govinda Govinda ... ]

## Meaning

Lord Sai is a protector of cows (Gopala), the Lord who lives in Puttaparthi, O protector of cows, Lord Govinda, please dance to the tune of rhuma jhuma like little Lord Gopala.
