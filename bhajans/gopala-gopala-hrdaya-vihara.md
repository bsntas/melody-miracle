Shyama Gopala Sai Gopala Gopala Gopala Gopala Gopala (2)

## Meaning

Lord Sai is a protector of cows (Gopala), the one who lives in the hearts of devotees, the dark hued one (Shyama), the attractive one (Krishna), the expert dancer ( Natavara ).
