## Meaning

Praise be to Lord Ganesha, the one who grants spiritual prowess. He is the son of Mother Parvathi. He relishes Modaks, and is adorned with vermillion. He is worshipped primordially. He is the son of Lord Shiva, who holds the crescent moon in His hair locks. Victory to Lord Ganesha, the bestower of auspiciousness.
