## Lyrics

Jagadeeshwari Amba Shiva (Sai) Shankari

## Meaning

Victory to the Divine Mother Shankari (Parvathi), the consort of Shiva, who protects the entire Universe. The manifestation of Shakthi (cosmic energy), she is the resident of the Sri Chakra (divine symbol). With Her beautiful lotus-shaped eyes, Her glance itself showers compassion on Her devotees.
