Nachey Gangadhara Nachey Bimbadhara [ Nachey Sai ... ]

## Meaning

Chant the name of Sai, the Lord of Lords who is verily Lord Shiva adorned with the sacred Ganges on His hair and who wears the crescent moon. He delights in His cosmic dance to the beat of the drums while the sound of His anklets reverberates in tune.
