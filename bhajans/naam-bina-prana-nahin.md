## Lyrics

Naam Bina Prana Nahin
Nanda Nanda Mukunda Sai [ Naam Bina ... ]
Gao Rey Guna Gao Rey
Ganga Yamuna Teera Chalo Rey

## Meaning

There is no life without chanting the name of adorable son of Nanda, of Lord Sai Mukunda. Sing His glory and His attributes, Go to the banks of the rivers Ganga and Yamuna.
