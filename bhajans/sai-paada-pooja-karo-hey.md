## Meaning

O Mind - Chant the Holy Name of Sai and offer it at His Lotus Feet. By chanting thus, redeem all your sins and fears across all births. His feet are those of Nataraja (Lord Shiva), they are the holy feet, which were worshipped by Lord Brahma the creator and they are the feet, which are soft and delicate.
