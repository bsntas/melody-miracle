## Lyrics

Karuna Sagara Krishna Murari Radhey Govinda
Madhava Mohana Mangala Roopa Radhey Govinda Jaya Radhey Gopala [ Karuna ]

Jaya Sai Govinda

## Meaning

Chant the name of Radhey Govinda who is also Krishna, Murari the ocean of compassion. Victory to Radhey Govinda who has an Auspicious and captivating form. He has a blissful form, who holds the conch and wheel in His Hands. You are the Beloved of devotees and you are Sai the resident of Parthi.
