## Lyrics

Chandrashekara Indushekara
Sathya Dharma Shanthi Prema Sai Shankara
Hara Hara Mahadeva Hara Hara Mahadeva (2)
Janana Marana Shoka Bhava Klesha Vidhura
Chandra Chooda Chandra Vadana Chandrashekara [ Hara Hara ... ]

## Meaning

Chant the name the Lord of the Lords Mahadeva (Shiva), the one who frees us from the cycle of birth and death, the one who adorns the moon on His head and whose face is serene like the moon rays. He is verily Lord Sai who fosters truth, righteousness, peace and love.
