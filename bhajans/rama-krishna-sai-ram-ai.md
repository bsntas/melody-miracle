## Lyrics

Rama Krishna Sai Ram Ai no Melody
Sai Prema, Sai Ram Kokoro no Kotoba wa Inori
Iesu, Sai Buddha, Omoi Todoke
Idai Naru Kami yo, Sathya Sai Baba
Towa ni Kagayaku, Sai Ram Sai Ram

## Meaning

Magnificent Sai, You are Rama, Krishna, Jesus, and Buddha. You shine with Your eternal form. The words of my heart are prayers for the melody of love.
