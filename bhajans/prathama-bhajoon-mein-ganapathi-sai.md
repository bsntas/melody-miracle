# Prathama Bhajoon Mein Ganapathi Sai

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/prathama-bhajoon-mein-ganapathi-sai)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Ganesha |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Prathama Bhajoon Mein Ganapathi Sai
Jai Jai Jai Jai Sai
Buddhi Pradayaka Hey Gana Nayaka
Ganapathi Sai Hey Sukha Dayi [ Jai Jai ... ]

## Meaning

Let us offer our primary worship to Lord Ganesha, the Lord of the demi-gods, the bestower of intellect and goodness. Salutations to Lord Sai who is verily Lord Ganesha.
