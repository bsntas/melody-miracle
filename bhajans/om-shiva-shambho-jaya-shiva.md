## Lyrics

Om Shiva Shambho Jaya Shiva Shambho
Bhava Bhaya Hari Hey Tripurari [ Om Shiva ... ]
Hey Girija Pathi Gangadhari
Hara Hara Shambho Hey Shiva Shambho
Samba Sadashiva Sai Maheshwara

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Shiva who is known by many names: Shambho, Tripurari (destroyer of three cities), Girija pathi (the Lord of Girija), Ganga Dhari (bearer of the holy Ganges), Samba Sada Shiva and Maheshwara (the supreme Lord). He is verily Lord Sai.
