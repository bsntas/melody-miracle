## Meaning

Lord Narayana, resting on cobra, the Lotus-eyed One, worshipped and adored by the devotees. You are the Lord of Pandharpur. You are the preceptor of the Vedas, Narayana. Dressed in yellow garments, holding the conch and the Disc, You are the dweller of Vaikunta, abode of Narayana. Ocean of mercy, deliverer from pitfalls of materialistic life. You are none other than the Lord of Parthi, Lord Sai.
