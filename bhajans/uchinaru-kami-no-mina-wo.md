## Meaning

If you call out the beautiful name of God who resides within us, He will touch with His kind, sweet love. Whether you are happy or sad, you can pray to the beautiful name of Sai who resides in your heart. Chant the names of Rama, Krishna, Shirdi Sai, Sathya Sai and Prema Sai.
