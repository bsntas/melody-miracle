## Lyrics

Yuga Avatara Sayeeshwara
Yadava Yadu Kula Nandana
Sundara Shubha Kara Sridhara
Madhava Keshava
Mangala Sai Gopala

## Meaning

You are the incarnation of this Kali age Oh Lord Sai, You bestow auspiciousness, You are verily Krishna, the beautiful and sweet son of Yadu clan and  Lord Vishnu the consort of Goddess Lakshmi.
