## Meaning

Salutations to mother of the Universe, Mother Bhavani, who is the remover of distress and bestower of auspiciousness, enchanter of Lord Shiva, with beauty in speech. Victory to the one who showers bliss and nectar, to the one who resides in Parthi, the destroyer of sins, the liberator of the downtrodden.
