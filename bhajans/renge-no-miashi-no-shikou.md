## Lyrics

Renge no Miashi no Shikou no Kami yo

## Meaning

Let us bow to the beautiful lotus feet of our Supreme Lord and Magnificent Avatar (Incarnation), Sathya Sai Baba, who breaks the chains of our rebirth.
