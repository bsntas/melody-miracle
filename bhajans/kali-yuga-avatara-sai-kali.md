## Meaning

O Lord Sai, You are the incarnation of the Kali age. You are none other than the beautiful Lord Muruga, wielder of the radiant spear, Vel. You are worshipped as Skanda, Guha, and Kadirvela with the shining spear. You are also known by Kartikeya and Shanmukha. Glory be Thee, O Lord Sai.
