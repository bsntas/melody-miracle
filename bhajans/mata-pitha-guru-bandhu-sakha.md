## Lyrics

Mata Pitha Guru Bandhu Sakha Hari
Narayana Hari (Sai) Narayana
Janaki Jeevana Sita Mohana
Lakshmi Ramana Narayana

## Meaning

O Lord Narayana, You are my mother, father, preceptor, kith and kin. You are Lord Rama, the very life of Mother Janaki. You are the consort of Lakshmi and is none other than Lord Sai.
