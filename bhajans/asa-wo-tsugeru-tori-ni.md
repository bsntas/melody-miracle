## Lyrics

Asa wo Tsugeru Tori ni, Kyou no Ichinichi wo Inori

## Meaning

Today, I pray to the birds that call out in the morning. Look at the rainbow that covers the sky. This is the divine work of God. O Lord Sathya Sai, You are the wind, the ocean, and the stars. Sai Ram, I offer You my gratitude again today.
