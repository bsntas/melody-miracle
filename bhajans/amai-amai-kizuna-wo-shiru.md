## Lyrics

Amai, Amai, Kizuna wo Shiru

## Meaning

How sweet and joyful it is to know our bond with our Lord. In silence and love, we pray for tranquility. O Lord Sai, we follow Your beautiful smile, feeling Your love inside of us. Your love spreads to everything making everything one.
