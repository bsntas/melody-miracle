## Lyrics

Allah Ho Akbar Sai Ram Maulah Ho Akbar Sai Ram
Rama Rama Jai Sai Ram Bolo Sundara Naam

## Meaning

Chant the divine names Allah, Maulah, Rama, Mahavir and Yesu. Victory unto them and Gurubani (divine verses of Gurugranth scripture of the Sikhs).
