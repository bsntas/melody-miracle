## Meaning

Victory to Mother Durga, the daughter of the King of mountains (Himalayas). O Mother, You are the destroyer of misfortune. You are the most effulgent one and the embodiment of Shiva and Shakthi. You are the destroyer of demons (all evils). Victory to Thee.
