## Lyrics

Baba Merey Parthi Vihari
Hum Sab Aayey Hain Sharana Tumhari [ Baba Merey ... ]
Dukh Bhanjana Mera Saath Na Chodo
Dedo Darshana Asha Na Todo
Sunalo Vinati Hamari

## Meaning

Baba, Lord of Parthi, please listen to our prayers. We all have taken refuge in You. Please destroy our sorrows, do not leave us or take away our hopes to Your darshan.
