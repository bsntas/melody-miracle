## Lyrics

Sharanam Sharanam Gananatha (2) [ Sri Gananatha ... ]

## Meaning

I surrender to You, O Lord Ganesha the leader of ganaas (attendants of Lord Shiva). O son of Parvathi and the dear son of Lord Shiva, You are none other than Sai Gajanana residing in Parthi. I surrender to You.
