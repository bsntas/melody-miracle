## Meaning

The bells in the Temple and the anklets on Lord Shiva's feet resound as He dances. All musical instruments such as the Mridangam, Veena and Damaru resound in the glory of His name. O Lord of dance, dance the Tandava (cosmic dance). Lord Sai is verily Lord Shiva, the Lord of the innocent.
