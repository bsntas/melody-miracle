## Meaning

Salutations to the enchanting flute player from Yadava dynasty, Lord Krishna, the slayer of demon Mura. Lord Sai who is verily Lord Krishna, the Lord of Mathura, has an auspicious and a sweet name. His soft spoken speech is ever sweet and He is the very personification of sweeetness.
