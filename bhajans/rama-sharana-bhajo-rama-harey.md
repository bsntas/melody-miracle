## Meaning

Sing of glorious surrender to Lord Ram, the one who liberated the chaste devotee - Ahalya. He is the one who is beyond attributes, the spotlessly pure Rama. Glory to the son of Dasharatha. He is none other than Sai, adored by the whole world. Glory to Sai Rama.
