## Meaning

This song is in praise of Lord Shanmukha (also called Muruga, Kumara), the son of Lord Shiva and Parvathi. He is extolled as the one who is armed with divine Spear and accompanied by His consort Valli. We seek the blessings of the compassionate Lord Muruga riding on the peacock. Our salutations to Lord Sai Natha who is none other than Shanmukha.
