## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Krishna, the dark hued Lord of Radha. He is the son of Mother Yashoda and an enchanter with the flute. With ornaments on His ears and vermillion mark of the musk on His forehead, He is adorned with the jewel, Koustubha. Glory to the handsome, cloud complexioned son of the Gokula chief, Nanda. Glory to Lord Krishna of Gokula, the Lord of wealth and the darling of Yadavas.
