## Lyrics

Jaya Meera Prabho Giridhari
Jaya Govinda Gopala Krishna Murari
Madhura Madhura Muralidhari
Ananda Ananda Sai Murari

## Meaning

Victory to the Lord of Meera, Lord Govinda, Gopala (names of Krishna), who held the Govardhana mountain high and the one who plays the Flute. He is verily sweetness and Bliss personified and is none other than Lord Sai.
