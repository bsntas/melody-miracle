## Meaning

Glory to the blue-throated and beautiful Lord Shiva, the beloved of Goddess Parvati. He has incarnated as our Lord Sathya Sai, and is the greatest of the Lords, residing atop mount Kailash. Lord Shiva is the source of all the emerging and merging processes (Linga).
