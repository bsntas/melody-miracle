## Lyrics

Chandramouli Shankara Chandrashekhara
Neelakanta Shooladhari Ambikeshwara
Hey Vibhuti Sundara Tribhuvaneshwara
Sai Natha Sai Natha Jai Maheshwara
Jai Mahesha Jai Umesha Jai Sureshwara

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Sai, Lord of the Lords, and the Lord of Eternity. He is verily Lord Shiva, Lord of Uma and the blue-necked bearer of the trident. His body is decorated with holy ash and He wears the crescent moon on His forehead.
