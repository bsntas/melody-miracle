## Lyrics

Sarva Dharma Roopa Parama Gatheesha
Maha Shakthi Hey Mahaan Teja
Vishwadhara Poorana Brahma Sathya Sayeesha
Prema Roopa Dhara Kali Avatara Sai Mahesha
Shirdi Sai Mahesha Sathya Sai Mahesha

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Sathya Sai who is the embodiment of all religions, who is extolled as the most effulgent, powerful, personification of Love and the incarnation of this Kali age.
