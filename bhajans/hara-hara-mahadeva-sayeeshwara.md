# Hara Hara Mahadeva Sayeeshwara

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Shiva, Sai |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Hara Hara Mahadeva Sayeeshwara
Namo Namami Shirdeeshwara [ Hara Hara ... ]
Vishweshwara Vishwambara
Hara Hara Gangey Mahadeva (2)

## Meaning

Worship and chant the names of Lord Mahadeva (Shiva) who bears the Holy Ganges, of Lord Sai of Shirdi and Parthi.
