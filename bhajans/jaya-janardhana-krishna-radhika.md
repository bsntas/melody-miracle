## Meaning

Victory be to the Krishna, who is the consort of Radhika. Krishna is also called Janardhana, son of Nanda, consort of Rukmini. As Lord Narayana, His vehicle is Garuda. Protect us oh Krishna who is also known by various names such as Keshava, Mukunda, Suresha, Mahesha, Sridhara, and Govinda. Master of Guruvayur, and Vaikunta, please protect us.
