## Meaning

Pray to Sai, who is Rama, Krishna, Vasudeva, son of Nanda. Glory to Rama, Krishna and Sai. Sai is the son of Nanda, born again. Refuge of the meek and the forlorn. He is Sai of Shirdi, Lord Srinivasa of Tirupati (Vishnu).
