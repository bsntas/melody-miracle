## Lyrics

Chandra Chooda Mahadeva Shiva Shiva Shambho Deva
Hara Hara Mahadeva Shiva Shiva Shiva Sai Deva
Sathya Naam Sathya Sai Deva Mruthyunjaya Mahadeva
Om Namah Shivaya Hari Om Namah Shivaya

## Meaning

Salutations to the Lord of the Lords, Shiva, who wears the moon on His head and who has conquered death. Lord Sathya Sai is none other than Lord Shiva.
