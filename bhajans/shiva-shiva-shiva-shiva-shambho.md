## Lyrics

Shiva Shiva Shiva Shiva Shambho Shiva
Hara Hara Hara Hara Sadashiva [ Shiva Shiva ]
Tandava Priyakara Shambho Sabesha
Parthi Mahesha Sai Shiva

## Meaning

Chant the holy name of Shiva, Shambho and Sadashiva, Hara who is fond of celestial dance Tandava. He is also the Lord of Parthi, Sai Shiva.
