## Lyrics

Aao Aao Shyama Gopal
Madhura Manohara Sai Gopal Aao [ Aao Aao ... ]
Dharma Uddharana Bhaktha Parayana
Aao Narayana Krishna Gopal (Aao)

## Meaning

Welcome O Shyam Gopala, the one who has a charming and a captivating form. You are the upholder of righteousness and very dear to the devotees who treads the right path. We welcome You as Narayana, Krishna and Gopala.
