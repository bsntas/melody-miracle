## Meaning

O Lord Sai, You are the destroyer of miseries of weak and meek. You are pure and spotless with full of kindness. You are Rama, the consort of Janaki (Sita). You are the resident of Ayodhya and the darling son of Dasharatha. You are the abode of the supreme bliss, the Emperor of Raghu clan who bestows joy and happiness to devotees and You are loved by the people of Ayodhya.
