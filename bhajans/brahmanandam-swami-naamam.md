## Meaning

We derive Supreme Bliss by chanting the name of Lord Sai. His Form is delightful to the eyes. Singing His glory gives us everlasting peace. His Form is charming to our hearts. One gets Atmic bliss by meditating on Him and being in the service of Lord Sai gives us Supreme Bliss.
