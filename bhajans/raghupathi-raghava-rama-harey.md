## Lyrics

## Meaning

Pray to Lord Rama who is the doyen of Raghu lineage who has a dark-hued lovely form. He is the cosmic consciousness, the ultimate abode, the most exalted of humans. The destroyer of desires and attachment has come down as Lord Sai Rama.
