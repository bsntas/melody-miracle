## Meaning

Oh Gananatha, You are the destroyer of all obstacles, protector and sustainer of Your devotees and the bestower of boons. Oh compassionate one, You are the bestower of auspiciousness. You are the almighty with supreme powers adorned with a symbol made of sandal paste. We bow to You, O protector of devotees.
