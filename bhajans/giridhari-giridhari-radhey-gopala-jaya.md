## Lyrics

Giridhari Giridhari Radhey Gopala Jaya Giridhari
(Aa)Shritha Jana Vihari Giridhari
Meera Ke Nath Prabho Giridhari (Mayi)

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Krishna, also known as Giridhari (who lifted the mountain), the Lord of Radha. You are the refuge to Your devotees. You are the Lord of  Meera.
