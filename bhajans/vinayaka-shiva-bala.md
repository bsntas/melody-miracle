|---|---|

## Lyrics

Vinayaka Shiva Bala
Sura Muni Vanditha Guna Sheela
Vakratunda Dhara Pranavakara
Lambodhara Gananatha

## Meaning

Lord Ganesha, son of Lord Shiva, is worshipped by sages and saints. The pot-belled Ganesha with the curved trunk is full of noble qualities, and is the source of all life.
