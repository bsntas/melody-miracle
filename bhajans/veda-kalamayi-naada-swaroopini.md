## Lyrics

Veda Kalamayi Naada Swaroopini
Kadamba Priya Karuna Sagari
Veena Venu Mrudanga Vinodini
Nanabharana Bhooshani Ranjani

## Meaning

Salutations to Mother Saraswathi, the Mother of Vedas and arts. She is the very personification of sound, the one who adorned with various jewels and flowers, the one who loves music, and the one who rejoices in Veena, Flute and Mrudangam (Indian percussion instrument).
