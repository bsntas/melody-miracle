## Lyrics

I Will Come to You

I look upon the mountains, I look into the sky
I look into the rivers flowing sweetly by
You are all I see

Sathya Sai Baba, You are all I see
I will come to You, You will come to me
We will merge as one for eternity

I find You in the stillness, I find You in my heart
I love to hear Your guidance so I can play my part
Sathya Sai Baba

Sathya Sai Baba, Oh Sathya Sai Baba
I will come to You, You will come to me
We will merge as one for eternity

I open up my heart, in love I pass the time
I let Your gentle wisdom purify my mind
Sathya Sai Baba

You are in my heart, Sathya Sai Baba
I will come to You, You will come to me
We will merge as one for eternity
