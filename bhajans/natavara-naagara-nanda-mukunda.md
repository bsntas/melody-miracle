## Lyrics

Natavara Naagara Nanda Mukunda
Brindavana Ghana Shyama Govinda
Radha Madhava Ananda Chanda
Sai Murari Satchidananda

## Meaning

Krishna the embodiment of dance, is the darling son of Nanda, who grants liberation. The dark cloud hued protector of all beings, who lived in Brindavan. He shared the divine happiness with Radha. He is our Sai, bestowing truth, awareness and bliss and destroys any evil in us just as he annihilated Mura, the demon.
