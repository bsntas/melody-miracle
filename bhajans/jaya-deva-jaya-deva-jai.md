## Lyrics

Jaya Deva Jaya Deva Jai Jai Ganapathi Deva
Vidya Dayaka Buddhi Pradayaka
Siddhi Siddhi Kara Deva ( Jaya Deva ... )
Girija Nandana Bala Gajanana
Jaya Lambodhara Mooshika  Vahana
Vighna Vinashaka Deva

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Ganesha, the leader of "Ganas"(volunteers of Lord Shiva), who gives knowledge, promotes intellect, and bestows success. Salutations to the young son of Mother Parvathi.
