## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Gananatha Deva Shambho Kumara
Jaya Jaya Partheesha Prabho Sai Ganesha
Jaya Jaya Hey Adi Deva Deena Sharanya
Jaya Jaya Hey Abhaya Daata Parthi Ganesha

## Meaning

Glory to the son of Shambho, leader of Shiva's troops and the Lord of Parthi. Glory to the refuge of the Forlorn, the First Lord, who grants protection from fear.
