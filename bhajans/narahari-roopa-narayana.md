## Meaning

Oh Lord Narayana! You are the abode of all beings; the form of Lion-faced Lord Narasimha! Oh Lord! You are the embodiment of divine music! Oh Lord of Puttaparthi! You are the Lord Who washes away our sins! Oh Lord Narayana, You are the ultimate refuge of all beings!
