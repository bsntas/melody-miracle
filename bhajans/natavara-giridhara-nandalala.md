# Natavara Giridhara Nandalala

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Krishna |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Natavara Giridhara Nandalala
Gopala Shyama Sundara [ Natavara Giridhara ... ]
Meera Ke Prabhu Giridhara Bala
Surdas Ke Sada Krupala
Murali Manohara Radhey Gopala

## Meaning

The dark-hued and beautiful Lord Krishna (Gopala), son of Nanda is the Lord of Meera and Surdas. Ever merciful, He likes to dance and play the flute.
