## Lyrics

Ganesha Bhaja Mana Ganesha Bhaja Mana Ganesha Bhaja Manarey
Parvathi Nandana Pashupathi Ranjana
Deva Ganesha Bhaja Manarey (2)

## Meaning

O mind, pray to the name of Lord Ganesha. He is the son of Goddess Parvathi and the one who pleases Lord Shiva, the lord of all beings.
