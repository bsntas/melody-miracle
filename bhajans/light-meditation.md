## Lyrics

Let the lamp within my heart be bright and shining,
So the light shines throughout the universe,
Spreading rays of love and peace and understanding,
That will help unite the brotherhood of man.
I am in the light, the light is in me, I am the light. (x2)
And these ears listen only to what is true.
May this tongue of mine speak in moderation,
Words of wisdom that are full of sweetness too.
I am in the light, the light is in me, I am the light. (x2)
And these feet should help me do what is right.
With a heart full of love and compassion,
See the world and all its beings bathed in light.
I am in the light, the light is in me, I am the light. (x2)
