## Lyrics

Gana Natha Gana Natha
Mooshika  Vahana Jaya Gana Natha
Mangala Dayaka Gana Natha (Shubha) [ Gana Natha ... ]
Parvathi Putra Sri Gana Natha
Jagadodhara Jaya Gana Natha
Sharanam Sharanam Gana Natha

## Meaning

Surrender to Lord Ganesha, Lord of the Ganaas, the one who rides the Mooshika (mouse) and who gives us all types of auspiciousness. He is the son of goddess Parvathi and He uplifts the whole world.
