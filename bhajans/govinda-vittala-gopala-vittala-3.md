## Lyrics

Govinda Vittala Gopala Vittala (3)

Sri Ranga Shirdi Ranga Parthi Ranga Vittala (2)

## Meaning

Chant the glorious names, of Govinda, Vittala, Gopala (names of Lord Vishnu) who protects cowherds and all beings. He is the Lord of Shirdi, Parthi and Pandharpur.
