| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## Lyrics

Himagiri Nandini Hey Jagat Janani
Sab Dukha Bhanjani Loka Niranjani [ Himagiri Nandini ... ]
Surya Chandra Ke Koti Prakashini
Jagat Janani Mahishasura Mardhini

## Meaning

Glory to Mother Parvathi who has the effulgence of a million suns and moons and who is the destroyer of demon, Mahishasura. She is the daughter of the king of mountains. She is the remover of our worldly sorrows.
