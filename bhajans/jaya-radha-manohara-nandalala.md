## Lyrics

Jaya Radha Manohara Nandalala
Jaya Geeta Vinodana Shubha Lola
Jaya Shyama Murari Goparipala (3)
Jaya Sai Murari Parthi Gopala

## Meaning

Victory to the son of Nanda, Lord Krishna, the charmer of Radha, who delights in sacred music. Gopala the dark colored, slayer of demon Mura, takes care of Gopis and is verily Sai of Parthi.
