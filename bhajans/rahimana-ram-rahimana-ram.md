## Lyrics

Rahimana Ram Rahimana Ram
Allah Eshwar Ek Hi Naam
Panduranga Vittala Vittala Naam
Bhajorey Manuva Hari Ka Naam ( Sai Ram )
Ek Omkara Bolo Jai Sathya Naam
Vahey Guru Vahey Guru Nanak Naam
Prem Hi Eshwar Prem Hi Ram [ Bhajorey Manuva ... ]

## Meaning

O mind, chant the various names of the Lords Ram and Rahim, the gracious One. Allah and Eshwar are one and the same. Chant the names of Panduranga Vittala and Hari, O mind; Omkara is the one true reality as preached by the great Guru Nanak. Love is God. O mind, chant the name of Hari and Sai Ram.
