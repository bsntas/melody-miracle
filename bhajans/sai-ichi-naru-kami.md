## Meaning

Sai, You are the one God of the Universe. You embody Truth, Goodness, and Beauty. Awaken within me the Wisdom of Buddha. Through Dharma, envelope me with love. Take me into the Holy community. O Sai, God of Love, grant Your blessings upon this sacred world.
