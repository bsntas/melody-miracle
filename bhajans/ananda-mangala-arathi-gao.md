## Lyrics

Ananda Mangala Arathi Gao
Veena Venu Mrudanga Bajao [ Ananda Mangala ... ]
Madhava Madhava Hari Narayana

Madhura Madhura Sangeeta Sunao

## Meaning

Sing the blissful and auspicious Arathi of Lord Sathya Sai to the accompaniment of musical instruments such as Veena, Flute and Mrudunga set in tune to sweet melodious music. Victory to Lord Sai is verily Lord Madhava (Krishna) and Lord Narayana.
