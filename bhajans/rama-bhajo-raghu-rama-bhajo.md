# Rama Bhajo Raghu Rama Bhajo

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/rama-bhajo-raghu-rama-bhajo)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Rama, Krishna, Shiva |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Rama Bhajo Raghu Rama Bhajo
Sita Manohara Rama Bhajo
Shyama Bhajo Ghana Shyama Bhajo
Radha Manohara Shyama Bhajo
Shambho Bhajo Shiva Shambho Bhajo
Gowri Manohara Shambho Bhajo

## Meaning

Chant the names of Lord Rama, consort of Sita, of blue complexioned Lord Krishna, beloved of Radha, and of Lord Shiva, the consort of Gowri.
