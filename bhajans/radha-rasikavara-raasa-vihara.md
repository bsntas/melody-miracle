## Meaning

Praise be to Lord Krishna and His Divine Consort, Radha, who are engrossed in the Raasa Dance. Glory to the one who is the embodiment of virtuous character and the one who plays enchanting music on His flute. Victory to Lord Madhava and Lord Mukunda who grants liberation.
