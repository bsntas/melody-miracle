## Lyrics

Jai Jai Jai Yadu Vamsha Dulari
Leela Lalita Manohara Pyaari [ Jai Jai ... ]
Bhuvana Mohana Prabhu Sant Sahayaka
Gaavata Veda Purana Pukari
Nanda Ke Lal Yashoda Ke Bal
Gopi Gopal Bala Jaya Vraja Lal

## Meaning

Sing in praise of the dear one of Yadu lineage, Lord Krishna, whose acrobats are lovely and enticing. Saints and Sages have sung in adoration of the Lord, who is the dear child of Nanda and Yashoda and the one whose name is chanted by the pundits who chant the Vedas and scriptures.
