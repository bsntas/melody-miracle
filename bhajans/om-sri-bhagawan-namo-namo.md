## Lyrics

Om Sri Bhagawan Namo Namo
Deena Dayala Parama Krupala [ Om Sri ... ]
Namo Narayana Prashanthi Vasa
Mahaanubhava Devadi Deva

## Meaning

We offer prayerful salutations to Lord Sathya Sai, who is compassionate to the downtrodden and who showers His grace on us. He is greatest of great celestial beings, verily the Lord Narayana, who has come as the Lord of Parthi.
