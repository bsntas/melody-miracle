## Lyrics

Pathri ni Umareta Shirdi Sai

## Meaning

Glory to Thee Lord Shirdi Sai born in Pathri. You are the reincarnation of Lord Shiva. Lord Shiva and Mother Parvathi, please bestow us with Your Divine Vision as you did with Devagiri Amma.
