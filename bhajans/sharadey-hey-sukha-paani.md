## Lyrics

Sharadey Hey Sukha Paani
Bharati Janani Bhagya Vidayini [ Sharadey Hey ... ]
Mangala Dayini Madhura Vani

## Meaning

Sing the praises of Mother Saraswati, the Goddess of knowledge, whose speech is sweet, auspicious, who bestows fortune and who is the creator of enchanting music. Mother Sai is none other than Goddess Saraswati.
