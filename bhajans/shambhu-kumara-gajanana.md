## Lyrics

Shambhu Kumara Gajanana
Shankari Tanaya Gajanana
Ambika Tanaya Gajanana
Ambuja Lochana Gajanana

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Gajanana son of Lord Shiva (Shambho) and Mother Parvathi (Shankari, Ambika). His eyes are of the shape of Lotus.
