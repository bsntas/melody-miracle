## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Ananda Amrutha Varshini
Raja Rajeshwari Rakshaya Maam
Durga Saraswathi Lakshmi Narayani
Brahma Sanathani Palaya Maam
Parthi Nivasini Rakshaya Maam

## Meaning

Sing the Glory of the Divine Mother who sheds Love in the form of a downpour. Protect me the Queen of Goddesses. You are the incarnation of the three energy principles, the Eternal and is verily Mother Sai who dwells in Parthi.
