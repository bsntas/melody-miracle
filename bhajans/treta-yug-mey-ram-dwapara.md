## Lyrics

Treta Yug Mey Ram Dwapara Yug Mey Shyam
Kaliyug Ke Sai Ram Parthipuri Sai Shyam
Sri Ram Ghana Shyam Sai Ram Sai Shyam [ Treta Yug ... ]
Ayodhya Vasi Ram Mathura Vasi Shyam
Prashanthi Vasi Ram Parthipuri Sai Shyam [ Sri Ram ... ]

## Meaning

Sai is the incarnation of this Kali age residing in Parthi. In the Treta age He was Rama, resident of Ayodhya and in the Dwapara age, He incarnated as Krishna in Mathura.
