## Lyrics

Govinda Govinda Govinda Bolo
Shyama Sundara Venu Gopala Bolo [ Govinda Govinda ... ]
Parthasarathi Keshava Bolo
Sanathana Sarathi Partheesha Bolo

## Meaning

Sing the name of Govinda, the handsome bluish boy Gopala with the flute. Sing the name of Keshava, the charioteer of Partha (Arjuna). Sing the name of the eternal charioteer, the Lord of Parthi - Sai - who guides us all in the right path towards liberation.
