## Meaning

The name of Lord Sathya Sai is Holy, the beautiful name which the sages worshipped, the most auspicious and nectarine name, the name that exhilarates the body and mind, the name which destroys the fears of the materialistic world and the name which bestows Truth, Righteousness, Peace and Love.
