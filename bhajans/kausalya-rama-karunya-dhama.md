## Lyrics

Kausalya Rama Karunya Dhama
Ravana Mardhana Rama Sri Rama [ Kausalya ... ]
Ahalyodharaka Rama Sri Rama

Sri Rama Jaya Rama Jaya Jaya Rama (3)

## Meaning

Oh Rama! the abode of compassion, You are the beloved of Kausalya, slayer of Ravana, redeemer of Ahalya from her curse. Victory to You Rama, who is served by Maruti (monkey God).
