Bhava Priya Sai Bhava Bhaya Bhanjana

Shirdeeshwara Sai Deva Deva
Partheeshwara Sai Deva Deva

## Meaning

The holy feet of Sai destroys woes. Incarnations of this age and worshipped by all, both Parthi Sai and Shirdi Sai who are fond of their devotees' reverential feelings, remove their fears of existence. Both the avatars are delightful and beautiful to behold.
