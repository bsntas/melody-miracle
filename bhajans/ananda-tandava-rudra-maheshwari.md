## Lyrics

## Meaning

O Goddess Maheshwari (Parvathi), You are so blissful during the cosmic dance (creation), You are the protector of distressed devotees. You are the blissful and beautiful universal Goddess Bhairavi who bestows knowledge. You are verily Mother Sai of Parthi.
