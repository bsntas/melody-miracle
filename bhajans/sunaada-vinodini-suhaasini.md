## Lyrics

Sunaada Vinodini Suhaasini
Anatha Rakshaki Sri Bhuvaneshwari
Abhaya Pradayini Ananda Roopini

## Meaning

The Divine Mother revels in sacred music, is bliss personified, protector of helpless and needy and the one who bestows solace to all.
