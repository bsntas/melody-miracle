## Lyrics

Permiteme poner a tus Pies
Mi amor, mi amor y devocion, Swami
Tu sabes que eres Todo para mi
Y mi existir te lo debo solo a Ti
Oh Swami!
Sosten mi barca al sucumbir
en el mar de la Ilusion
Quiero ver en todo solo a Ti
y amar sin distinguir
Oh Swami!

## Meaning

Allow me to surrender my love and devotion at Your Feet. You know that You are everything for me and I owe my whole existence only to You. Oh Swami! Hold my boat so it doesn't sink in the Sea of Illusion. I want to see only You in everything and love all without distinction. Oh Swami!
