Jai Jai Panduranga (3)

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Panduranga Vittal, the Lord of Pandharpur. He is Lord Krishna of Radha. He is Giridhar, the one who lifted the mountain. He who revels in mischief and is the stealer of hearts is none other than the Lord of Parthi, Sai Ranga.
