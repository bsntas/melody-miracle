## Lyrics

Akhila Loka Nayaki Devi Tripura Sundari
Ananda Roopini Sai Jagadeeshwari
Adi Mahakaali Lakshmi Vaagdevi Roopini [ Ananda Roopini ... ]

## Meaning

O Supreme Mother, You are the Master of the universe, the most beautiful one in three worlds. You are the embodiment of bliss and is verily Mother Kaali, Lakshmi and Saraswathi personified.
