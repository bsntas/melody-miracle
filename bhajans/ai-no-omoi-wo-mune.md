## Meaning

The thoughts of love fill my chest (heart). I speak only the words of love. Let us follow the path of love to our Reverent Sai Baba. Chant the beautiful name of God. Embrace the blessings of our Lord. Let us follow the path of God to our Reverent Sai Baba.
