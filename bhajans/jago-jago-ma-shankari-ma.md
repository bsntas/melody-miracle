## Lyrics

Jago Jago Ma Shankari Ma
Shankari Ma Abhayankari Ma [ Jago Jago ... ]
Mata Maheshwari Parvathi Ma
Devi Daya Karo Shiva Ramana
Janani Triloka Palini
Jai Jai Bhavani Ma

## Meaning

Oh Mother Shankari, wake us up to the reality of the soul, the one who assures total protection. Victory be to you Maheshwari, Bhavani, Janani, the delight of Lord Shiva and sustainer of the three worlds.
