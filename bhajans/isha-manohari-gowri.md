## Lyrics

Isha Manohari Gowri
Kailasadhipa Ranjani Gowri
Uma Maheshwari Mangala Gowri
Sai Janani Kedara Gowri

## Meaning

Mother Gowri enchants and delights Lord Shiva, Lord of Kailas. She is Mother Uma, and Mother Sai of Kedara mountain, who grants auspiciousness.
