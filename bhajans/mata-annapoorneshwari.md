## Meaning

Pray to Mother Annapoorneshwari, who is none other than Sathya Sayeeshwari, who is also Mother Saraswathi (who bestows knowledge) and Mother Lakshmi (who bestows wealth). Mother Sai is also the embodiment of Shiva Shakti.
