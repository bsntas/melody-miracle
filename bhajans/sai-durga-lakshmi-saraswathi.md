## Lyrics

Sai Durga Lakshmi Saraswathi
Hey Mahimatmayi Tejomayi [ Sai Durga ... ]
Manda Hasita Mayi Prana Vahini
Atma Jyothi Anandamayi (2)

## Meaning

Mother Sai, the life-force in all, is Durga, Lakshmi and Saraswathi. She is the indwelling soul, with a gentle smile, engulfed in divine effulgence and full of bliss.
