| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Krishna |

## Lyrics

Gokula Bala Gopala Krishna
Govardhanodhara Govinda Krishna
Meera Ke Prabhu Mohana Krishna
Parthipureeshwara Sri Sai Krishna

## Meaning

In Gokula, you were Gopala Krishna. For Mira, you were Lord Mohana Krishna, the captivating Lord playing your flute. You lifted the Govardhana mountain. In Puttaparthi, you are Lord Sai Krishna.
