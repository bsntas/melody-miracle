## Lyrics

## Meaning

Let us worship and surrender at the Lotus feet of our Guru Sai. Lord Sai grants us Devotion, Knowledge, Love and Liberation. O Lord Sai, You are the Lord of the Universe. Our prostrations at Thy lotus feet.
