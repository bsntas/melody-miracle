| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## Lyrics

Shyama Manohara He Giridhari
Radha Madhava Krishna Murari [ Shyama Manohara ... ]
Meera Ke Prabhu Natavara Lala
Narada Sangeeta Lola Gopala

## Meaning

The dark-hued and beautiful Lord Krishna, who held the mountain up, is Lord of Radha. He is the Lord of Meera and destroyer of Mura. Sage Narada sang His glory.
