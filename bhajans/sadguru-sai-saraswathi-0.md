## Meaning

Worship the supreme teacher, Sai, the embodiment of Saraswathi with all knowledge and wisdom. A lover of music, She is also full of peace. She plays beautiful melodies on the Veena and She is the embodiment of eternal sound of creation. As Sai of Parthi, wearing a white robe, She personifies love.
