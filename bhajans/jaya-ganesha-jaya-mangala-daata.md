## Lyrics

Jaya Ganesha Jaya Mangala Daata
Vande Saraswathi Sai Mata [ Jaya Ganesha ]
Mooshika Vahana Sri Gananatha
Vighna Vinashaka Bhagya Vidhata
Vande Saraswathi Sai Mata

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Ganesha who confers prosperity and welfare, the one who has a Mouse as His Vehicle, the one who is the leader of Shiva's army of Ganaas and destroys impediments. Salutations to Mother Saraswathi.
