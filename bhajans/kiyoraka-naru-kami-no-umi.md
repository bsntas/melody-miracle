## Lyrics

Kiyoraka Naru Kami no Umi
Toki wo Koe Yami wo Nukete
Kono Mune wo Mitashi Tamae
Warera no Sai Ganapathi
Ganapathi Jai Jai Sai Ram

## Meaning

O Lord, You sparkle as bright as the ocean. You cross over time itself to clear away the darkness. You do all of this to gratify our hearts. You are our Lord Ganapathi (Ganesha).
