## Lyrics

Navaneetha Chora Bala Gopal
Yashoda Nandana Krishna Gopal
Sai Gopal Sathya Sai Gopal
Sai Gopal Bhajo Murali Gopal

## Meaning

Salutations to The Cowherd who stole butter and butter-like hearts of people of Gokul. Known as Krishna, a darling of mother Yashoda. Sing the glory of our Sai Krishna, who manifests everywhere through the music of HIS divine flute.
