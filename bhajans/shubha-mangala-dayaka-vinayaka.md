## Lyrics

Shubha Mangala Dayaka Vinayaka
Gajavadana Gananatha Gajanana [ Shubha Mangala ... ]
Mooshika Vahana Modaka Hasta
Jaya Lambodara Vighna Vinasha
Pashankusha Dhara Paapa Vinasha
Parthi Pureeshwara Sai Gajanana

## Meaning

Praise be to Lord Ganesha, the bestower of auspiciousness. His vehicle is the mouse and He enjoys the sweet Modaks. He is the remover of all obstacles and the destroyer of sin. Residing in Puttaparthi, He is none other than our Lord Sai.
