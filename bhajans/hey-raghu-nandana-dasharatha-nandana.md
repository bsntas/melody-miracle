## Lyrics

Hey Raghu Nandana Dasharatha Nandana
Sita Pathey Ramachandra Parthi Pathey Ramachandra
Parama Pavitra Varija Netra [ Sita Pathey ... ]
Bhakthoddhara Sabari Raghuraya
Raghukula Bhooshana Rajeeva Lochana [ Sita Pathey ... ]

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Rama of the Raghu dynasty, the son of Dasharatha and the consort of Mother Sita. He is ever pure, has lotus-shaped eyes and protects His devotees. He is none other than Lord Sai of Parthi.
