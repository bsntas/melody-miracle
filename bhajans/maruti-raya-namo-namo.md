## Meaning

Salutations to Hanuman who is also known as Maruti Raya (King of the monkey clan), and is the mighty son of Vayu (God of wind). Victory to the valorous one who adores Lord Rama, and assisted the Lord in the annihilation of Ravana. Salutations to the most beautiful Lord Sai Rama.
