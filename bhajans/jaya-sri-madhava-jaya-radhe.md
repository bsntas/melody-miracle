# Jaya Sri Madhava Jaya Radhe

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/jaya-sri-madhava-jaya-radhe)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Krishna |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Jaya Sri Madhava Jaya Radhey Govinda
Mukunda Murahara Murali Govinda
Aao Jaya Murali Govinda
Deena Bandhu Deena Nath Radhey Govinda
Prema Sindhu Prema Natha Sai Govinda

## Meaning

Salutations to Radha's Lord, Govinda(Krishna), the charming flute-player and slayer of demon Mura. He is an ocean of love and the kinsman and Lord of those who are less fortunate.
