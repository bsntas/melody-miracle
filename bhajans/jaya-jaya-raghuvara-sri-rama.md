## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Raghuvara Sri Rama
Hara Brahmarchitha Prarthitha Rama
Kaalaathmaka Parameshwara Rama
Janani Janaki Manobhi Rama (2)

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Rama who belongs to the Raghu dynasty, who is adored by both Lord Shiva and Brahma (the God of Creation). Glory to the Supreme Lord Rama who is beyond Time and who is the beloved of Mother Janaki.
