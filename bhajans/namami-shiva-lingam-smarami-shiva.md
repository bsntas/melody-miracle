## Meaning

I worship the Linga, the oval symbol of Lord Shiva, I meditate upon the Linga. I worship constantly, I meditate constantly and salute  the Linga that emanates from the lotus of the navel, that is the cause for the grace/wisdom in the world, that emerges on the holy night of Shivarathri, that bestows everlasting bliss.
