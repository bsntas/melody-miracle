## Lyrics

Hari Om Hari Om Hari Om Namah Shivaya
Sai Om Sai Om Sai Om Namah Shivaya
Dama Dama Damaru Mahadev Ka

## Meaning

Chant the divine mantra of Shiva " Namah Shivaya". All the Gods Pay their Obeisance to Lord Mahadev who holds the Damaru in his Hands.
