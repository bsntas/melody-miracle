## Lyrics

Nayana Sunayana Sundara Shyama
Madana Mohana Prabhu Hey Vraja Bala
Kamala Kantha Karuna Daata
Raja Gambhira Raja Gopala
Manasa Ranjita Shyama [ Madana Mohana ... ]

## Meaning

The beautiful eyes of Lord Krishna have entranced us. This child of Brindavan has enraptured our hearts. He is soft like the lotus, showering compassion on all, yet He is the supreme King of Kings.
