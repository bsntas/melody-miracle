## Lyrics

Sai Ke Darbar Mey Aao
Madhura Madhura Sangeeta Sunao [ Sai Ke... ]
Veena Venu Mrudanga Bajao
Raga Sudha Ras Sabko Pilavo [ Madhura Madhura... ]

## Meaning

Let us all gather in the Divine presence of Lord Sai, glorify Him by singing sweet music and playing the instruments Veena, Flute and Mrudanga and may everyone drink the sweet nectar of devotion.
