## Lyrics

Pibare Rama Rasam Rasane Pibare Rama Rasam
Janana Marana Bhaya Shoka Viduram
Sakala Shastra Nigamagama Saram(2)
Bhajarey Rama Rasam Rasane Pibare Rama Rasam

## Meaning

Drinking in the nectar of Lord Rama's name by chanting His name helps us escape from the sorrowful cycle of birth and death. His name is the essence of all religious texts.
