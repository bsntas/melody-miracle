## Lyrics

Vighna Vinashaka Vinayaka (3)
Amba Bhavani Shiva Shambhu Kumara
Karunya Lavanya Akhilandeshwara

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Vinayaka (Lord Ganesha), the destroyer of obstacles in life, who is the son of Lord Shiva and Parvathi (Amba Bhavani), who is compassionate and beautiful and who is the Lord of the Universe.
