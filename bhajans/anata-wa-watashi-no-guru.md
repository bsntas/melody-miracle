## Lyrics

Anata wa Watashi no Guru Baba, Guru Baba, O Ai no Kami

## Meaning

You are my Guru Baba, God of Love. You show us the truth of 'Aham Brahmasmi' (Thou Art That). The flower of Wisdom glows at Your feet. Let it bloom in the light within me.
