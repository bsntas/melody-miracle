## Lyrics

Om Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Babaya Namah (7)

## Meaning

Let us bow and chant the glory of Lord Sathya Sai Baba in all aspects. Chant the mantra - Om, which is the primordial sound, Bhagawan - the one who possesses the sixteen attributes in full splendor (only Lord Krishna in Dwapara Age was supposed to have had all sixteen), Shri, which represents prosperity, Sathya the embodiment of truth, Sai and Baba - divine Mother and Father.
