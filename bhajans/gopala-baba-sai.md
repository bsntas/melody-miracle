## Meaning

O Lord Sai, you are Lord Gopala. In the town of Gokula, you gave protection to the cows and their calves. O Lord, you saved the mountain Govardhana, as per your promise and the devotees from the anger of Lord Indra. Lord Sai, as Gopala, was also the beloved of the Gopikas.
