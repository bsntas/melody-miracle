## Lyrics

Parthi Puri Nayaki Paripalinchu Kamakshi
Nithya Buddhi Dayaki Neerajakshi Sundari
Bhaktha Loka Palaki Kamitaartha Dayaki

## Meaning

O Sai, Goddess of Parthi, rule over our desires. O the bestower of boons, grant us the eternal wisdom. O the beautiful lotus-eyed one, the queen of the devotee's worldly lives, You bestow desired affluence on Your devotees.
