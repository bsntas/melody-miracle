## Lyrics

Jagadeeshwara Paahi Parameshwara Shiva Gangadhari

Shambho Shankara Parthi Vihari

## Meaning

Protect us O Lord Shiva, the Sovereign Lord of the Universe. You hold the river Ganges in Your matted locks and are blue-throated. You wear the Crescent moon on Your head and the skin of the tiger round your body (which indicates the sublimation of animal qualities). You are verily Lord Sai who dwells in Parthi.
