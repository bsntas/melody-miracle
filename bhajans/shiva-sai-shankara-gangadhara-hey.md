## Lyrics

Shiva Sai Shankara Gangadhara (Hey)
Parthi Vihara Sayeeshwara [ Shiva Sai ... ]
Trishoola Dhari Hey Shiva Shankara
Deena Dayala Sayeeshwara
Lingodhbhavakara Lingeshwara [ Parthi Vihara ... ]

## Meaning

This song extols the glory of Lord Sai who is none other than Lord Shiva with attributes like, Gangadhara (the one who holds mighty Ganges in His matted locks), Trishoola Dhari (armed with Trident) Deena Dayala (compassionate to downtrodden), Lingodhbhavakara (the one who manifests the sacred Lingam).
