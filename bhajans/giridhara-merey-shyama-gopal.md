## Meaning

Chant the various names of My Lord Gopal, the Giridhara, The one who lifted Govardhana mountain, the cowherd of Gokula The enchanting Lord Mukunda, Madhava, with the flute, the slayer of Mura, the Lord Sai Gopal.
