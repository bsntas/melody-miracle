Sai Natha Haro Hara (3)

## Meaning

This song is in praise of Lord Shanmukha (also called Muruga, Kumara), the son of Lord Shiva and Parvathi. His abode is on top of the Pazhani Hills. Lord Sai of Puttaparthi is verily Lord Shanmukha. Our humble obeisance to Him.
