## Meaning

O Lord Sai, You are always dwelling in my heart with Your beautiful form. You peacefully guide us with Your beautiful name. Sai Baba, I love You. Sai Baba, I sing to You. Sai Baba, I pray to You. I devote myself to You in prayer. I will live with love and light.
