## Meaning

Glory to Thee, O Lord Shiva, the auspicious one who resides on Mount Kailasa. You are the Greatest God, the protector of the innocent. You are beyond attributes, one who is free of all blemishes. You are adorned with sacred ash and the one who loves the Tandava dance. You are none other than Lord Sai.
