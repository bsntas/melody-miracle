## Lyrics

Jaya Ma Jaya Ma Jaya Jaya Jaya Ma
Prema Sai Ma Ananda Sai Ma
Krupa Sai Ma Tum Hi Daya Sai Ma

## Meaning

Praises be to Mother Sai who is the very personification of Supreme Love and Bliss, the One of Compassion and Mercy.
