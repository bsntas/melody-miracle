## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Sai of Parthi, the very embodiment of Lord Ganesha, who is the source of the primordial sound of creation, Om. He is the remover of all obstacles and sustainer of the universe. His vehicle is the mouse (which symbolizes darkness) and He holds the sweet 'Modaka' in His hands. He embodies the diminutive as well as the expansive form of the universe.
