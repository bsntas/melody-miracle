## Lyrics

Yuga Avatara Sayeeshwara
Yadava Yadu Nandana
Hrudaya Vihari Hey Deena Bandho
Hari Hari Hari Om Jagadeka Bandho
Hey Deena Bandho Karuna Sindho

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, You are the incarnation of this Kali age. You are verily Lord Krishna of Yadava dynasty. You are the kin of the downtrodden and also the whole world, You wander in people's hearts and You are the ocean of compassion.
