## Lyrics

Yadu Kula Nandana Sri Hari Madhava Govinda Gopala Krishna Krishna
Krishna Krishna Radha Krishna

## Meaning

O Lord Krishna of the Yadava clan, You are also known by different names as Madhava, Govinda, Gopala, Madhusudhana and Mohana. You are adored by Radha.
