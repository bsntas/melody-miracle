## Lyrics

Shankara Abhayankara
Daya Nidhey Manohara [ Shankara ... ]
Saama Gaana Priyakara
Sai Jaga Vandana
Parameshwara Maheshwara
Sabha Pathey (Krupa Nidhey) Manohara

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Shankara who dispels all fears, the one who is the ocean of mercy, the one who is most beautiful and the one who loves music. We offer our salutations to the Lord of the Universe, Lord Sai.
