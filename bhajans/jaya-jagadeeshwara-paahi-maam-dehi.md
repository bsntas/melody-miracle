## Lyrics

Jaya Jagadeeshwara Paahi Maam Dehi Maam
Anga Vibhuti Harey Shiva Shankara
Amaradeeshwara Hara Hara Shankara

## Meaning

Protect me Oh Lord of the Universe, Shankara, who has smeared Vibhuti (Sacred ash) on His Body and who is the Lord of the immortals.
