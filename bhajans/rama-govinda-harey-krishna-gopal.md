## Meaning

Glory to Lord Rama and Lord Govinda (sustainer of all beings - Lord Krishna). He is the slayer of demon Madhu. He is the darling son of Devaki and beloved Yadava child, the ruler of all beings. He is the ruler of all worlds, benefactor of the meek. Glory to that Lord of Mathura, the Lord of Lakshmi. Glory to the Liberator of the chaste. Glory to Lord Sai, Lord of Parthi, who is verily Lord Krishna.
