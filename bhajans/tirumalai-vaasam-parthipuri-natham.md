## Meaning

Chant the name of the Lord of seven hills, Narayana, who is bliss incarnate. He holds the conch (symbolic of the sound Om which signifies creation), Sudarshana Chakra (wheel of time bringing about transformation), and mace (signifies mental and physical strength). He is called by several names, including Venkata (destroyer of sins) and Sai Narayana.
