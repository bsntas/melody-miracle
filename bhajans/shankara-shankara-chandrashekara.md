Parthi Puri Shankara Shambho Shankara (2)

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Shankara (also known as Chandramouli and Shambho) the one who holds the Ganges in His Locks, the one who is worshipped as the consort of Mother Gowri, the one who holds the crescent Moon on His head and to the Sai Shankara who creates the sacred Lingam.
