## Lyrics

Simhasaneshwari Sayeeshwari
Shiva Vama Bhage Sarveshwari [ Simhasaneshwari ... ]
Vande Vande Jagadeeshwari

## Meaning

Glory to Mother Parvathi who sits on the throne on the left side of Lord Shiva. Pay obeisance to the Mother of the Universe, Mother Sai, who is none other than Mother Parvathi.
