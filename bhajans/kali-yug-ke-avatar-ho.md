## Meaning

Blessed be Mother Eswaramma, the mother of Lord Sai. O Lord of this Kali age, You are none other than Brahma, Vishnu and Parameshwara (Shiva).  O Lord, drive away the miseries of the downtrodden. Chant the name Sai Ram.
