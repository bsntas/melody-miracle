|---|---|

## Lyrics

Radhey Krishna Radhey Krishna Bhajorey Bhajo
Giridhara Naagara Bhajorey Bhajo [ Radhey Krishna ... ]
Murali Manohara Bhajorey Bhajo
(Putta) Parthipuri Sai Bhajorey Bhajo

## Meaning

Sing the name of Lord Krishna, Lord of Radha, who plays the enchanting flute and who resides in Puttaparthi  as Lord Sai.
