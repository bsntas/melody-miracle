## Lyrics

Sai Madre compasiva, Madre universal, Madre mia
Ven guilla mi manana, muestra (m)el camino que debo seguir
Se que eres divina, se que eres amor
O Sai mia, da me tu vision

## Meaning

Sai, compassionate Mother, universal Mother, O Mother of mine! Come, guide my future and show me the path that I should follow. I know You are Divine, and You are Love Incarnate. O my Sai, grant me Your vision.
