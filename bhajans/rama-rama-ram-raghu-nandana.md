## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Rama of Raghu clan, the indweller of hearts. He was the son of Dasharatha and Kausalya to whom He was the very life. Lord Rama, the consort of Sita was the resident of Ayodhya. Glory to Lord Rama who destroyed Ravana with His famous bow, Kodanda. He is served by Hanuman (Maruti). Sing the name of the son of Eshwaramba, the Lord of Puttaparthi, Lord Sai who is the incarnation of Lord Rama. Victory to Lord Rama and Lord Sai.
