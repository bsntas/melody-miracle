## Meaning

Love is God, God is Love. Sai, the God of Love, is our very breath. Hail to Lord Jesus. O Lord Sai, You are Amaterasu (Japanese Sun God) and Kannon (the Bodhisattva). At every moment, I recite Your beautiful name for You are the embodiment of Love.
