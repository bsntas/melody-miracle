## Lyrics

Madhava Mohana Muralidhara

Navaneetha Chora Narayana [ Nanda Mukunda ]

## Meaning

Oh Lord Narayana you have taken birth in the Yadava clan as the son of Nanda, one who steals the butter (of worldly desires) from the hearts of your devotees, holds the flute and charms everyone around.
