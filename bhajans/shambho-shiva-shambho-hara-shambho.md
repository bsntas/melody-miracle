## Lyrics

Shambho Shiva Shambho Hara Shambho Namah Om
Arunachala Vihara Karuna Dhara Namah Om
Shambho Natana Shambho Tripura Shambho Namah Om
Bhava Hara Shiva Nataraja Shekhara
Shaileshwara Mahadeva Shankara
Bhaja Mana Shiva Shambho

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Shiva, who is also known as Shambho, Shekhara, and Hara. Dancing in the Arunachala hill, Shiva is the Lord of the mountains and all three worlds, and He bestows compassion upon all. O Mind, chant the name of Shiva, the Lord of all Lords.
