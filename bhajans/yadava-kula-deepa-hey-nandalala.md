## Lyrics

Yadava Kula Deepa Hey Nandalala
Hey Nandalala Sai Gopala [ Yadava Kula ... ]

Jaya Muralidhara Hey Vraja Bala

## Meaning

O Lord Gopala (Krishna), You are the light of Yadu Dynasty, You are the one who annihilated the demon Madhu. With the Flute, You played about in Brindavana. You are verily Lord Sai of Trayee Brindavan. Victory to Thee, O Lord.
