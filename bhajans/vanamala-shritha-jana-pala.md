## Lyrics

Vanamala Shritha Jana Pala
Darshanam Ivvayya Gopala (Sai)
Goparipala Gopi Lola
Radha Manasa Giridhara Bala
Giridhara Bala Raasa Vilola (Radha Lola) (2)

## Meaning

O Lord Gopala (Krishna), the protector of the good and the pious, the one who adorns the garland of forest flowers, grant us Your vision. You are the protector of the cows. You occupy the thoughts of Radha, You are the one who protected the devotees by lifting the Govardhana mountain and the one who performed the celestial dance.
