## Meaning

Singing Sai Bhajans grants us supreme bliss. Let us sing the glory of the effulgent Lord Sai who resides at Puttaparthi on the banks of the Chitravati river. Let us sing the glory of the one who is the protector of Truth, Right Conduct, and Peace. Lord Sathya Sai is the protector of all.
