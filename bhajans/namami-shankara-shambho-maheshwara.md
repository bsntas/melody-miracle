## Lyrics

Namami Shankara Shambho Maheshwara
Namami Sadguru Mahadeva [ Namami Shankara ... ]
Namami Brahma Namami Vishnu
Namami Sadguru Sai Deva

## Meaning

I worship Lord Shiva, the bestower of good, the great Lord. I prostrate to Shiva who is the true preceptor. I bow down to Brahma, the Lord of creation, I salute Vishnu, the Lord of sustenance. I prostrate to the Divine Preceptor, Lord Sai.
