## Meaning

Lord Sai, our guru, is the Hindu trinity of the creator (Brahma), protector (Vishnu) and destroyer (Shiva), and the basis of the universe. Sai, in His Shiva form swallowed the poison to save humanity. O Sai, kindly protect us, help us cross the ocean of life and bestow on us the nectar of eternal life.
