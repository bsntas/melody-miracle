## Lyrics

God is dwelling in the beauty in the temple of my heart
Of my heart, of my heart, of my heart, of my heart
Soham, Soham, You and I are one (2)
Soham, Soham, kia kotahi e (2)

## Meaning

"Te Matua kei roto i toku manawa nui" - The Lord is in my beloved heart; "Kei roto-kei roto-kei roto taku manawa nui" - Within, within, within, my beloved heart; "Kia ko tahi e" - Yes, all is one.
