## Meaning

Glory to Lord Ganesha, the elephant-faced son of Mother Parvathi. He is the bestower of all powers and has the mouse Mooshika as His vehicle. He is worshipped and served by the pious and the righteous. We bow at Thy lotus feet, O Lord Ganesha.
