## Lyrics

Ganesha, Ganesha Yami wo Furi Harau
Ganesha, Ganesha Seigi no Kami
Ooinaru Umi wo Wataru Fune to Naru
Yuuki wo Atae Chikara wo Sazuketa mo
Katari Tsugou, Ima Utaou

## Meaning

O Lord Ganesha, You clear away the darkness. You are righteous and just. You are the ship that allows us to cross the vast ocean. You grant us courage and power. We sing to You.
