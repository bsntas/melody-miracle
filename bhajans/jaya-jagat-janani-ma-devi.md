## Lyrics

Jaya Jagat Janani Ma Devi Sai Ma Sathya Sai Ma
Shiva Shakthi Roopini Ma Sai Ma

## Meaning

Glory to the Mother of Universe Sathya Sai, who is the Personification of Lord Shiva and Shakthi, who sustains the Three worlds and confers Liberation.
