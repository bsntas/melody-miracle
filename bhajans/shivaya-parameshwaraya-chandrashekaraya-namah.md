# Shivaya Parameshwaraya Chandrashekaraya Namah

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Shiva |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Shivaya Parameshwaraya Chandrashekaraya Namah Om
Haraya Haraya Haraya Namah Om
Shankaraya Namah Om
Bhavaya Guna Sambhavaya Shiva Tandavaya Namah Om
Bhavaya Bhavaya Bhavaya Namah Om
Guna Sambhavaya Namah Om

## Meaning

Bow to Lord Shiva, who is the Lord of the Universe with a crescent moon on His hair, who loves the Tandava (cosmic) dance, who gives us virtues to go through our cycle of birth and death.
