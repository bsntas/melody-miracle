## Lyrics

Namah Om Namah Om Hari Om Hara Om
Shiva Shiva Shivaya Shivaya Namah Om [ Namah Om ]
Shirdipureesha Sai Shakthi Maheshwara
Parthipureesha Sai Omkareshwara

## Meaning

Bow to Lord Shiva, Lord of Shirdi and Parthi, the remover of worldly fears, and the Lord of Om.
