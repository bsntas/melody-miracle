## Meaning

Glory to Divine Mother Bhairavi (Parvathi), the protector of the weak, the embodiment of music (sound) and the lotus-eyed one who supports the Universe. Glory to the author of the Vedas, the one who is verily Lord Sai, the Supreme Soul.
