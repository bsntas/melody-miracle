## Lyrics

Narayana Hari Naam Bhajorey (3)
Krishna Janardhana Jaya Panduranga
Radha Ramana Hari Narayana
Ayodhya Vasi Sri Raghu Rama
Dasharatha Nandana Janaki Rama
Parthi (Shirdi) Pureeshwara Sri Sai Rama

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Narayana. Sing the glory of Lord Krishna, the protector, also hailed as the loving deity Panduranga. Honor Radha's great God, Hari Narayana. Acknowledge Sri Rama, the prince of Ayodhya from the Raghu clan. He is son of the great King Dasharatha, and the beloved of Mother Sita. Pray to Lord Sai, the Lord of Shirdi and Parthi who is none other than Lord Rama.
