## Lyrics

Rama Harey Raghu Rama Harey
Shyama Harey Ghana Shyama Harey
Janaki Jeevana Rama Harey
Radha Madhava Shyama Harey

## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Rama of Raghu clan, the very life of Mother Janaki and the glory of Lord Shyama (Krishna), the dark complexioned one who is dear to Radha.
