## Meaning

O Lord Sai, dweller of Prashanthi Nilayam of Parthi, You are eternally blissful, beauty personified. You are embodiment of existence, awareness and bliss. You are Govinda (Krishna), the one who carried the Mandara mountain, who bestows Mukti (liberation) to devotees.
