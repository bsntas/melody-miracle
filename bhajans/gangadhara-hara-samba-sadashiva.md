## Meaning

Welcome O Lord Sai, whose Holy name bestows happiness on all. You are the Eternal Lord Shiva who holds the river Ganges on His matted locks. O Lord, You appeared in Shirdi and now You have appeared in Parthi. O divinely beautiful Lord Sai, please come and grant us the bliss of Your presence.
