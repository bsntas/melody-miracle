## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Shiva, the one who is synonymous with the primordial and cosmically resonant sound of “Om.” Bearing the Ganges in His matted locks, He is the destroyer of desires, the embodiment of compassion, and the Lord of the entire Universe.
