## Lyrics

## Meaning

The journey towards Sai is a long one that continues every day for the purpose of opening my heart to the truth. I wish for the grace of Sai. I wish that I may become one with Him. I wish for the end of my rebirth cycle and for endless joy. Lord Sai, You have brought to me love, peace, and freedom.
