## Lyrics

Gopala Gopala Radhey Gopala
Radhey Shyam Sri Ranga Sai Gopala
Madhava Hey Yadava Pandari Natha Vittala
Hey Deena Natha Vittala
Hey Sai Natha Vittala
Radhey Shyam Sri Ranga Sai Gopala

## Meaning

Oh Gopala, the beloved of Radha, also you are Shyama and Sai Gopala the resident of Srirangam. You are the star of Yadu dynasty and you are the Lord Vittala of Pandari, who gives refuge to meek and weak devotees. Also you are Sai Natha Vittala.
