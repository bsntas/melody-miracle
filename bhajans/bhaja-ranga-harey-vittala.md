## Lyrics

Bhaja Ranga Harey Vittala
Panduranga Harey Vittala
Sai Ranga Harey Panduranga Harey
Bhaja Rakumayi Ranga Vittala [ Panduranga Harey ... ]

## Meaning

Chant the Holy name of Lord Vittala. Glory to Lord Panduranga, the consort of Rakumayi. Sing the glory of Lord Sai who is none other than Lord Vittala.
