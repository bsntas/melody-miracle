## Meaning

O Sai who is the Lord of the destitute, the very form of Brahman, the supreme Lord, who is all knowing, please accept us at Thy Holy Feet and grant us liberation. O Lord Sai, You are pleased by truth, right conduct, peace, love and non-violence. Give us strength, devotion, bliss, peace and liberation from birth.
