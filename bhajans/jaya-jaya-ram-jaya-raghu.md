## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Ram Jaya Raghu Ram Ravikulabdhi Soma
Sita Ram Sri Raghu Ram Sathya Sai Bhagawan [ Jaya Jaya Ram ... ]
Sita Kantha Ram Jaya Janaki Jeevana Ram

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Rama, Scion of the Solar race, who is the consort and the very life of Mother Sita, who has come presently in the form of Lord Sathya Sai.
