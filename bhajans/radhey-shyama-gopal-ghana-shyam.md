## Lyrics

Radhey Shyama Gopal Ghana Shyam Gopal
Ghana Shyam Gopal Giridhari Gopal [ Radhey Shyama ... ]

Hum Bhakthon Ke Sathya Sai Gopal

## Meaning

Praises be to Lord Gopal, beloved of Radha, Of the color of dark blue clouds, To the Lord of Brindavan, Krishna Gopal, To the Lord of Meera, son of Nanda and To our dear Sathya Sai Gopal.
