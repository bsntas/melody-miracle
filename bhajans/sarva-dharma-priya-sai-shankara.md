## Meaning

Glory to Lord Shiva (Shankara), the one who encompasses all religions, the one who is worshipped by all, the one who is the Lord of the Universe, the one who destroys all sins and the one who is always in bliss. Bow to Lord Sai of Parthi who is the very embodiment of Lord Shankara.
