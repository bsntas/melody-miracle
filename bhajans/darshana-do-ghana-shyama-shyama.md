## Lyrics

Darshana Do Ghana Shyama Shyama
Radhey Govinda Radhey Govinda [ Darshana Do ... ]
Mathura Natha Deena Dayala
Parthipuri Ghana Shyama Shyama

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai who is verily the dark-complexioned Lord Krishna, the Lord of Radha. O Krishna, You are the Lord of Mathura and the most compassionate one. O Lord Sai, the resident of Parthi, please grant us Your divine vision.
