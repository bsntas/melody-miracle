## Lyrics

Anjaneya Paahi Maam Hanumantha Paahi Maam
Vayu Nandana Paahi Maam Lanka Dahana Paahi Maam
Aprameya Paahi Maam Suprakasha Paahi Maam
Rama Dootha Paahi Maam Sathya Sai Paahi Maam

## Meaning

O Lord Anjaneya, son of Vayu (Wind God) protect us. O Lord Hanumantha who set Lanka on fire, protect us. Protect us, O Rama's messenger, the effulgent one endowed with immeasurable qualities. O Lord Sathya Sai, protect us.
