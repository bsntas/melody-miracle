## Lyrics

Natavara Naagara Hey Nandalala
Radha Lola Raasa Vilola [ Natavara Naagara ... ]
Murali Lola Munijana Pala
Meera Ke Prabhu Giridhara Bala
Giridhara Bala Sai Gopala

## Meaning

The darling son of Nanda, You are the one who enchants with Your mischief. O the soul of Radha, You are the one who enticed all Gopis with Your dance. You are the one who enthralls with Your flute, the saviour of the Sages. You are the Lord of Meera. You are the child who lifted the mountain. You are Sai Gopala.
