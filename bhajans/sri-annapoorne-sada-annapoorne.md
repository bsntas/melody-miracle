## Meaning

Praise be to the Divine Mother, the one who provides us with spiritual nourishment and sustenance. She rules over the entire universe, and grants knowledge, peace, and auspiciousness. Bearing the crescent moon, she is verily our Lord Sai residing in Puttaparthi.
