## Lyrics

Shirdi Sai Vittala Shesha Sai Vittala
Sri Ranga Vittala Rakumayi Vittala
Jaya Jaya Vittala Jaya Sai Vittala
Hey Ranga Vittala Panduranga Vittala

## Meaning

This song extols the Glory and the attributes of Lord Panduranga Vittala (another name for Lord Krishna) of Pandharpur. He is none other than the Shirdi Sai who as Vishnu was reclining on the celestial Serpent Adi Sesha. Lord Vittala's consort is Rakumayi.
