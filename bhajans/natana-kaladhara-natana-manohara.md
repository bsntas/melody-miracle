## Lyrics

Natana Kaladhara Natana Manohara
Narthana Sundara Nataraja
Shivakami Sundara Shiva Raja
Shiva Sai Shankara Shiva Raja

## Meaning

We pray to Shiva who is the source of all dance forms, heartthrob of Parvathi. His dance form enchants one and all. Beautiful Shiva is none other than Sai who also used to dance charmingly giving joy to his devotees.
