## Meaning

Glory to Lord Shiva, the Lord of all who dances the beautiful Tandava dance. He holds the river Ganges in His matted locks and has three eyes (the third eye is that of wisdom). He is the Lord of Nandi, the Bull and is the very personification of Supreme Power.
