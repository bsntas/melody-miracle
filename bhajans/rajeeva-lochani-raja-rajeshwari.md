## Meaning

The Goddess of the Emperors, the Lotus eyed, Bestower of auspiciousness, Mother Sai Narayani, the sovereign ruler of this entire creation. She is the remover of sorrows, Destroyer of sins, who lives in Prashanthi Nilayam, The very embodiment of Love, Sai Bhavani.
