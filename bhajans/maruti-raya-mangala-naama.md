## Lyrics

Maruti Raya Mangala Naama
Pavana Putra Paavana Charitha
Anjani Putra Anjaneya
Ati Balavanta Jaya Hanumantha

## Meaning

Chant the auspicious name of Lord Maruti (Hanuman), the Son of Wind God, the one with blemishless character. Salutations to the Son of Anjani, the mighty Hanumantha.
