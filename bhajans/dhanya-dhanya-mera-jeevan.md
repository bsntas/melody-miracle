## Lyrics

Dhanya Dhanya Mera Jeevan Prabhuji
Tera Darshan Paaya Maine
Teri Charanon Mey Aaya Prabhuji [ Dhanya Dhanya ... ]
Teri Leela Sabse Nyaari
Teri Mahima Jeevan Daatri

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, my life is blessed as I have received Your Divine Darshan and have offered myself at Thy Lotus Feet. O Lord, Your Divine Miracles are unique and life bestowing. Glory to Thee O Lord Sai.
