## Meaning

Listen and meditate on the glory of the Avatar of this Age, Lord Sai, residing in Parthi, who is none other than Lord Krishna in human form. The three worlds dance to His melody. (As per Indian Mythology the three worlds are Sky, Earth and the sub-terrain below the ocean).
