## Lyrics

Tirumalesha Ramana Govinda

Gangadhara Hara Pannaga Bhooshana

Hari Hara Roopa Sri Venkatesha

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi. O Sai, You are verily Lord Govinda, the beloved Lord of Tirumala and Lord Shankara (Shiva) who holds the river Ganges on His matted hair and who adorns the serpent as an ornament. You are the one who wears the Kaustubha jewel and You are verily Lord Venkatesha who is the embodiment of Hari (Vishnu) and Hara (Shiva).
