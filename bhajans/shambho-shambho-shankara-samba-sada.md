## Lyrics

Shambho Shambho Shankara Samba Sada Shiva
Ganga Jatadhara Kanaka Sabesha [ Shambho Shambho ... ]
Kailasa Vasa Hey Paramesha
Shiva Shakthi Roopa Sai Sarvesha (2)

## Meaning

Glory to Lord of the Lords, Sai who is verily Lord Shiva, who wears the Ganges in His matted hair and who resides at Mount Kailasa. Lord Sai is the embodiment of both Lord Shiva and Shakthi (Mother Parvathi). He is the Lord of all.
