# Nanda Kumara Jaya Gopal

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/nanda-kumara-jaya-gopal)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Krishna |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Nanda Kumara Jaya Gopal
Kanhaiya Lal Jaya Gopal
Yashoda Ke Bal Jaya Gopal
Radha Ke Nath Jaya Gopal

## Meaning

Victory to Gopal (Lord Krishna), son of Nanda and Yashoda and the Lord of Radha.
