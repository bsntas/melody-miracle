## Lyrics

Chitha Chora Nandalala
Nanda Gopala Govinda Lala
Devaki Nandana Gopala
Murali Manohara Nandalala
Madhusudhana Yashoda Bala
Madhava Mohana Shyama Gopal

## Meaning

Chant the many names of Lord Krishna, the captivator of the hearts and mind, the charming cowherd boy who plays the flute and the one who destroyed the demon Madhu. He is the child of Devaki fostered by Nanda and Yashoda. He is the ever sweet and enchanting dark-complexioned Gopal.
