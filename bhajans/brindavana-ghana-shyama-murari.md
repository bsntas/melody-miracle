## Lyrics

Brindavana Ghana Shyama Murari
Madhura Manohara Muralidhari [ Brindavana Ghana ... ]
Radha Madhava Kunja Vihari
Raasa Vilola Hey Giridhari
Rajeeva Lochana Parthi Vihari

## Meaning

Lord Krishna, slayer of the demon Mura, is Radha's Lord and moves about in the gardens of Brindavan. The lotus-eyed Lord who lifted the mountain Govardhan to protect His devotees is Lord Sai of Parthi.
