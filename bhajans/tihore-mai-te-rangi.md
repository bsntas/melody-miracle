## Meaning

Clear up Sky clear up; stop stop stop Rain come out Sun; Fly Kingfisher up onto the clump of astelia. Ruffle the rain-drops from your wings in case you catch a chill. Escape Earthworm out of your burrow in case it fills with water and you drown. Let the Sun shine.
