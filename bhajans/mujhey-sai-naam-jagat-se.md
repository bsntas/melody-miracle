## Lyrics

Mujhey Sai Naam Jagat Se Pyaara

## Meaning

What I love most in this world is the name of Lord Sai. He is ever blissful. He is none other than Lord Krishna, son of Nanda. Lord Sai is the very life breath of His devotees.
