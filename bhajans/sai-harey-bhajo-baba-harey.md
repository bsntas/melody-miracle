## Meaning

Sing the Glory of Lord Sai Baba Sing the Glory of Lord Allah, Yesu and Guru Nanak Sing the Glory of Lord Buddha, Mahavira and Zohrashtra Praise be to Lord Sai, who is the embodiment of all religions.
