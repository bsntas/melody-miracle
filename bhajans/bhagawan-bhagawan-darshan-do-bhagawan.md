## Lyrics

Bhagawan Bhagawan Darshan Do Bhagawan
Allah Sai Buddha Zohrashtra
Nanak Sai Yeshu Mahavir
Sabka Malik Ek Hai Bhagawan
Parthi Pureeshwara Ram

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, please grant us Your Divine Vision. Bless us to realize that Allah, Buddha, Zohrashtra, Guru Nanak, Jesus Christ, Mahavira, and Rama are all incarnations of You, O Lord of Puttaparthi.
