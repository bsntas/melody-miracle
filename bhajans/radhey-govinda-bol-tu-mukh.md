## Meaning

Repeat the names of Lord Govinda and Sai Ram with your tongue. Our life span ebbs away with every passing second. Hence, chant the name of Hari and Sai before it is too late. Let every hair on our body vibrate with the name of Sai and our breath with the name of Ram.
