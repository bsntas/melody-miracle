## Lyrics

Madhava Mohana Kunja Vihari
Mathuranatha Muralidhari [ Madhava Mohana ... ]
Meera Ke Prabhu He Giridhari
He Giridhari Shyama Murari [ Meera Ke ... ]
Parthi Pureeshwara Sai Murari

## Meaning

Lord Sai of Parthi is the enchanting Krishna roaming in the gardens. He is the Lord of Mathura, the flute-player and the destroyer of demon Mura. Lord of Meera lifted the Govardhana mountain (to protect His devotees)
