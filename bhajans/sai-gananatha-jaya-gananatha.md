## Meaning

Victory to Thee, O Lord Sai, You are verily Lord Ganesha, Lord of the Ganas (Shiva's army). You are the embodiment of Truth, Righteousness, Peace, and Love. You are the God of supreme wisdom and courage. Your resolute light guides us.
