## Meaning

Sing the glory of the elephant-faced Lord Ganesha, the young child who is the bestower of auspiciousness. Protect us, O beloved son of Lord Shiva and Mother Parvathi. You are the pleasant-faced one worshipped by the sages. We bow to Thee, O Lord Sai, You are verily Lord Ganesha.
