## Lyrics

Shyama Murari Bansidhari
Jagadoddharana Leela Dhari [ Shyama Murari ... ]
Radha Meera Raasa Vihari
Krishna Ranga Ananda Dayi
Yaduvara Shyama Shyama Ghana Shyama

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Krishna of Yadu dynasty, who has a darkened complexion and who enchants all with the music of His flute. He is the redeemer of the world who enjoys playing divine sports. Radha and Meera join Him in Raasa Leela. Glory to the bestower of bliss.
