## Lyrics

Gajananam Girija Tanayam
Gangadhara Shiva Kumaram Devam [ Gajananam ... ]
Suramuni Sevitha Charanam Sharanam
Sada Smarami Sai Sharanam

## Meaning

We adore Lord Gajanana (elephant headed god), the loving son of Girija (Parvathi) and Shiva (who adorns the Ganges). We bow to Your feet, which is worshipped by Gods and saints. We dwell on Your divine form always.
