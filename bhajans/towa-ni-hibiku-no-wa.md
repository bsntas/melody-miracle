## Lyrics

Towa ni Hibiku no wa Kami wo Tataeru Uta
Subete wa Kami no Ai no Yorokobi no Mama ni
O Harey Ram Harey Ram Sai Ram

## Meaning

O Lord, our song of praise upon You resounds eternally. Everything is made of Your love and joy. O Lord Sai Ram, I exist as one with You. Baba, I am Your eternal love.
