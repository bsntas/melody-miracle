## Meaning

Victory to Shiva Shankara who has His Abode at Kailasa, Ramachandra at Ayodhya, Sri Krishna at Dwaraka, Ramakrishna at Dakshineshwar and to the One who dwells at Shirdi and Puttaparthi.
