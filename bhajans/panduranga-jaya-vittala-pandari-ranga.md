# Panduranga Jaya Vittala Pandari Ranga

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Vittala |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Panduranga Jaya Vittala Pandari Ranga Hari Vittala
Bhaktha Rakshaka Mukthi Pradayaka Nithya Niranjana Hari ...
... Vittala [ Panduranga Jaya ... ]
Anatha Natha Deena Dayalo Gaana Vilola Hari Vittala

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Panduranga (Lord Vittala) of Pandharpur! The ever pure Lord protects and gives liberation to the destitute and friendless.
