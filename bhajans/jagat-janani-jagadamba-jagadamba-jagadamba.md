## Meaning

Mother Bhavani is the Universal Mother who vanquished the demon Mahishasura as well as demons Shumbha and Nishumbha to promote the welfare of the world. Mother Sharada plays sublime notes on Her Veena and Mother Durga assumes nine different forms. Glory to the Divine Mother.
