## Meaning

Glory be to Lord Krishna, also known as Govinda, the consort of Radha and the son of Nanda. He is the one who steals butter (hearts of devotees). Chant the name of Lord Narayana, who installed Himself as Vittala Lord of Pandharpur. He has incarnated as Lord Sai at Parthi.
