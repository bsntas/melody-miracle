## Lyrics

Tirumalesha Prabhu Govinda
Venkata Ramana Govinda [ (Hey) Tirumalesha Prabhu ... ]
Sankata Harana Parthi Vihara
Govinda Sai Govinda (Hey)

## Meaning

O Lord Govinda, You are the beloved Lord of the mountains of Tirumala. O beloved Lord Venkata, You remove all our distress. You are verily Lord Sathya Sai residing in Puttaparthi.
