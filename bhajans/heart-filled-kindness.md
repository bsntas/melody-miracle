## Lyrics

You may be rich and wealthy.
Your coffers may be filled with gold,
But unless your heart is filled with kindness,
You'll be lonely when you grow old.
You may not have much money,
Your cupboards may be rather bare;
But if your heart is filled with kindness,
You'll be happy beyond compare.
