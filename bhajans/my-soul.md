## Lyrics

In my soul
A lotus grows
The gift of love
That Sai bestows
It is only for You that I sing
For the voice I raise is a gift from You
And the words and the melody too [ In my soul ... ]
It is only for You I perform
And the one I do is a gift for You
And the fruit of my labor is true [ In my soul ... ]
For Your love is the gift You gave me
And the flame that burns in the heart of hearts
Is the same flame that burns within me [ In my soul ... ]
