## Meaning

Glory to the Divine Mother who embodies the Supreme power and who is the source of creation, preservation, and destruction. She is Bhuvaneshwari, the Queen of the Universe, shining with divine light and dispelling darkness. Hail Durga, the protector and destroyer of evil. Victory to Divine Mother Sai, the compassionate energy that nurtures and blesses all.
