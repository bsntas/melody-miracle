## Meaning

Meditate on Shankara, the supreme deity. He is the Lord of Srisailam and Kailasa, whose consort is Mother Gowri. He saved the universe by swallowing the 'haalaa-hala' poison (which rested in his throat turning it blue), Who grants us the power to overcome the fear of death. He manifests in the Linga form. Oh Sai Shankara! Protect us and bless us.
