## Meaning

Salutations to the most auspicious Form of Lord Shiva, the Shiva Linga. Glory to the Linga that embodies Mother Gowri (Parvathi) and Ganga. Glory to the Form adorned with a garland made of Mandara flowers and that bears the river Ganga, the Form that was worshipped by Lord Rama and Nandi, the Form that is enshrined in the hearts of devotees and is worshipped on the Holy occassion of Shivarathri.
