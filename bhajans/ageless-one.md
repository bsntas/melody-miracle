## Lyrics

Ageless One,
Sacred Spirit,
We praise Your name
For all to hear it.
Free my soul,
Help me see,
Let Your love ...
wash over me.
(Sweet, sweet love, wash over me)
All souls shall know,
Through Your grace All blessings flow,
As we serve
With heart and mind,
Thy will be done
On earth this time.
(Sweet, sweet Lord)
