## Lyrics

Jaya Jagadeeshwari Jaya Parameshwari
Jaya Partheeshwari Jaya Bhuvaneshwari
Jaya Shaktheeshwari Raja Rajeshwari
Jaya Ma Jaya Ma Vijaya Sayeeshwari

## Meaning

Victory to the Mother of the Universe, Lord Sai, the resident of Puttaparthi who is supremely powerful.
