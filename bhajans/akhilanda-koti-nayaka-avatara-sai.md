## Meaning

O Sai, the supreme leader of all gods and the worlds; who descended on earth and who is the indweller of hearts; who is the reserve of immeasurable good qualities and bliss personified. You are the one who reclines on the Adi Sesha (multi-headed serpent), You are the essence of the Vedas and who has incarnated for the sake of His devotees.
