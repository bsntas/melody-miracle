## Lyrics

Madhuvan Mey Sai Gopala Harey
Mathura Ke Krishna Gopala Harey
Shirdi (Parthi) Ke Sai Baba Harey [ Madhuvan Mey ... ]
Madhuram Charitham Sai Charitham
Madhuram Vachanam Sai Vachanam
Mathuradipathey Akhilam Madhuram

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai who is verily Lord Krishna, the Lord of Mathura who moves about in the forest inhabited by demon Madhu. Glory to Lord Sai Baba of Shirdi and Parthi. Lord Sai's character and speech are nectarine. Glory to the one who is the very personification of sweetness.
