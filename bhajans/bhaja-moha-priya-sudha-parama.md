## Lyrics

Bhaja Moha Priya Sudha Parama Pavitra Parthi Pureesha
(Hey) Sada Niranjana Bhava Bhaya Bhanjana Shiva Shiva Sayeesha [ Bhaja Moha ... ]
Dama Dama Dama Dama Damaru Mahesha
Dhimithaka Dhimithaka Digambaresha

## Meaning

Let the praises flow passionately to Sai Shiva, the Lord of Puttaparthi, who is the supremely sacred one. Always without blemishes, Lord Sai Shiva is the remover of fear and ignorance in this world. He dances rhythmically to the beat of the drum. Lord Sai Shiva is the embodiment of peace, happiness, and all the good qualities in this world.
