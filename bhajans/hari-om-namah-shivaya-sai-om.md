## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Sai who is verily Lord Shiva, the one who holds the Holy river Ganges in His matted locks and who pleases Mother Gowri (Parvathi). He wields the Trident and is the destroyer of the three demons of Tripura. Chant the name of Lord Sathya Sai.
