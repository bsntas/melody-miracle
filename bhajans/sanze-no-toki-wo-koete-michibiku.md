## Lyrics

Sanze no Toki wo Koete Michibiku Yuga no Kami Sai Baba

## Meaning

You transcend the time of our three lives to guide us, the Lord of the Yuga, Sai Baba. The infinite light that pervades the cosmos is Your grace, Sai Shiva. Salutations to Lord Shiva who is none other than Lord Sathya Sai.
