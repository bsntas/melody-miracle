## Lyrics

Rudra Swaroopa Omkara Natha
Neelakantha Sai Deena Natha
Tandava Priyakara Girija Ramana
Vibhuti Sundara Kailasa Rana
Lingodhbhava Kara Shiva Yogeshwara

## Meaning

Lord Shiva, with the blue throat, is the protector of the meek. He is the cosmic dancer, the delight of Mother Parvathi and the embodiment of Om. Lord of Kailasa, with the eye of wisdom and adorned with holy ash is the source of creative energy. He is Lord Sai.
