## Lyrics

Chandrashekara Sai Sundara
Devadi Deva Maheshwara
Vyagrambaradhara Hey Shiva Shankara
Vibhuti Sundara Amaradeeshwara
Chidambaresha Sayeeshwara [ Devadi Deva ]

## Meaning

Glory be to the beautiful Sai Shiva, who adorns the moon on His head, who is the God of Gods, Maheshwara. You are Lord Shankara who is clothed in tiger skin and besmeared with sacred ash. You are Lord Sayeeshwara who resides in Amarnath and Chidambaram.
