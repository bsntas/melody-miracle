## Lyrics

Kanarazu Yukute wo Hagemashi Mimamoru Ganapathi
Miashi wo Agameyo (Minna Agameyo)
Chikara ni Michita Ganapathi

## Meaning

Lord Ganapathi (Ganesha), with Your infinite power, You watch over and guide us along the right path. Let us all worship His divine feet.
