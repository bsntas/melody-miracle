## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai Kanhaiya (Krishna)Glory to the merry mischief maker Lord Krishna Kanhaiya Glory to Lord Kanhaiya who lifted the mountain Govardhana to protect his Gopis and Gopikas (cowherds) Glory to Lord Sai who is the very incarnation of Lord Krishna.
