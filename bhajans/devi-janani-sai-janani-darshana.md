## Lyrics

Devi Janani Sai Janani Darshana Do Mata
Danava Mardhini Dharma Swaroopini Darshan Do Mata
Sathya Sanathani Shanthi Pradayini Darshan Do Mata
Parthi Pureeshwari Prema Pradayini Darshan Do Mata
Darshan Do Mata (4)

## Meaning

O Universal Mother Sai, bestow on us the vision of Your divine form. You are the destroyer of demons and the embodiment of righteousness. You are the eternal truth and the goddess of Parthi who bestows peace and love.
