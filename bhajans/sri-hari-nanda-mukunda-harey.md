## Meaning

Chant the names Lord Hari (Krishna), Nanda's son and the name of Dasaratha's son, Lord Rama, in whose heart Goddess Lakshmi resides. O Rama, destroyer of many demons like Ravana, You are purity personified. Chant the name of Lord Sai who is verily Lord Rama.
