## Meaning

Oh Beautiful and Virtuous Rama, Your name is sweeter than honey. Oh Rama, with the Kodanda (the Sacred Bow) in hand You are the personification of Valor in the battle. You are the temple of Bliss. Victory to You, Oh Rama.
