## Lyrics

Brindavan Ke Bansiwala Radha Manohara Sai Shyama [ Sai Murari ... ]

Sundara Shyama Sai Gopala
Brindavan Ke Krishna (Radha)

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai who is the same as Lord Krishna, the one who slayed the demon Mura and the one who is bewitchingly dark as the rain cloud. Glory to Lord Sai, who is none other than Gopala who played in Brindavan and who dwells in the mind of Radha.
