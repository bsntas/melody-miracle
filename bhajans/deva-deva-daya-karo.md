## Lyrics

Deva Deva
Daya Karo Sai Deva
Krupa Karo Sai Deva
Antaryami Sai Natha
Parthipurisha Hey Paramesha
Deena Dayala Sai Natha
Prema Swaroopa Paramananda
Jai Jai Jai Jai Sadguru Deva
Karuna Sagara Sathya Sai Deva

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, the indweller of heart, have mercy and shower Thy grace. O Embodiment of Love and Bliss who resides at Parthi, Thou art extremely kind to the miserable, afflicted and dejected.
