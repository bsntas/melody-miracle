## Meaning

Let us sing the song of the Lord with our hearts filled with love. The beautiful name of the Lord is the fountain of our lives. Always live with love in your heart. Respecting all things is the way of God. Always live in prayer and keep the Lord's beautiful form in mind. You are our Lord, the God of Love. Please guide us with Your love, for the love of the Lord is everything there is in this world.
