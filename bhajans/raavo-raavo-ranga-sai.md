## Lyrics

Raavo Raavo Ranga Sai

Venu Vilola Vijaya Gopala

## Meaning

Come and give us your Darshan Oh Ranga Sai (Krishna - Sai). One who has a lovely form, dear to Radha. One who revels in playing on the Flute, the victorious protector of all beings, You are none other than Sai Rama, who has taken the vow to redeem the Vedas (spiritual way of life).
