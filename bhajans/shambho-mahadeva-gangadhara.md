## Meaning

Chant the names Lord Shiva (Also called Shambho, Mahadeva, Shankara and Sadashiva) - the One who adorns the crescent moon on His head and the sacred river Ganges in His Matted Locks. He is none other than the Lord Sai of Parthi.
