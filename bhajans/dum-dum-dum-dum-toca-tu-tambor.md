## Lyrics

Dum Dum Dum Dum toca tu tambor
Porque ha nacido en Puttaparthi el señor
Sathya Sai Baba se llama el avatar
Y ha venido al mundo a enseñarnos a amar
Y ha venido al mundo a traernos la paz

## Meaning

"Dum Dum Dum Dum" play your drum, because the Lord has been born in Puttaparthi. The Avatar is called Sathya Sai Baba, and He has come to the world to teach us to love and to bring us peace.
