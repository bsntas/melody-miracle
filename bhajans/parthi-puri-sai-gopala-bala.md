## Lyrics

Parthi Puri Sai Gopala Bala
Shirdi Puri Sai Gopala Bala [ Parthi Puri Sai ... ]
Meera Ke Prabhu Giridhara Gopala
Saama (Bhajana) Gaana Priya Sai Gopala

## Meaning

Sing the glory of the protector of all beings, Lord Sai of Parthi, who is also Sai of Shirdi and Krishna, the Lord of Meera. Glory to the one who loves songs and Saama Veda.
