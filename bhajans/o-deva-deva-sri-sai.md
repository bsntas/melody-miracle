## Lyrics

O Deva Deva Sri Sai Baba
Karunintsu Mammu Kapada Vayya [ O Deva ... ]
Adharamaina Ni Pada Seva Kaligintsu Maaku O Deva Deva
Sathya Swaroopa Sri Sai Baba
Karunalavala Brovanga Rava

## Meaning

O Lord Sai Baba, shower grace upon us and protect us. Give us opportunity to pray at your feet. Embodiment of Truth and Grace please come and take care of us.
