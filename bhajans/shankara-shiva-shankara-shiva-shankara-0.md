## Lyrics

Shankara Shiva Shankara Shiva Shankara Sai Shankar
Parthi Ke Tum Ho Nivasi

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, Your Divine abodes are Puttaparthi and Kailasa, You are the Form of Lord Shiva (Shankara) who holds the river Ganges on Your head. Glory be to Thee O Lord Sai.
