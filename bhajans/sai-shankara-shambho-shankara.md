## Lyrics

Sai Shankara Shambho Shankara
Natana Manohara Jaya Abhayankara
Chandrakaladhara Gowri Shankara
Parama Dayaghana Sai Shankara
Eshwaramba Nandana Parthi Shankara
Paramananda Prabhu Parameshwara

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Sai of Parthi, the Son of Eshwaramba. O Lord Sai, You are none other than Lord Shankara (Shiva) who is fond of dance, who bestows victory, who wears the crescent-shaped moon on the head and the consort of Gowri (Parvathi). You are the most compassionate one who is always in bliss.
