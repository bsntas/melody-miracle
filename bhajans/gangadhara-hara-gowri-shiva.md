## Meaning

Glory to the Lord Sathya Sai, who is the very form and embodiment of the Trinity - Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva. He is verily Lord Shiva who bears the Ganges on His head and the consort of Mother Parvathi. Lord Sai is the most compassionate one who confers happiness and prosperity and is very dear to all His devotees.
