## Lyrics

Natavara Giridhara Nandalala

Nandalala Sai Nandalala

## Meaning

The Lord of dance, the one who raised the mountain to protect his devotees, The darling son of Nanda, He is the protector of all beings, Gopala, also known as Govinda. Sing praises of the darling son of Nanda, who is none other than our beloved Sai.
