## Meaning

Beloved Lord Sai, please come and give Your Darshan. Help me cross this ocean of life's miseries. Hear my plea, O Sai Ram. Lead me from untruth to truth, from darkness to light, from death to immortality. Help me overcome this cycle of life and death.
