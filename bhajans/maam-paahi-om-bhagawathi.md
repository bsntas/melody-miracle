## Lyrics

Maam Paahi Om Bhagawathi
Sharada Ma Saraswathi [ Maam Paahi ... ]

Bhava Bhaya Bhanjani Sai Maheshwari (2)

## Meaning

O Divine Mother, please protect us. You are Mother Saraswathi, the mother of learning and art. You are the embodiment of sound. You are the eternally Supreme Mother Sai, the remover of all worldly fears.
