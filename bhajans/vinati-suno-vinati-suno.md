## Meaning

O Mother Parvathi, You are very dear to Lord Shiva, You bestow happiness to all, You are very dear to all the Saints. O Mother Santoshi, You are the annihilator of all sufferings. O Mother Sai, who is none other than Mother Parvathi, please listen to my pleadings.
