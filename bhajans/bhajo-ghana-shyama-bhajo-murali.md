## Lyrics

Bhajo Ghana Shyama Bhajo Murali Gopala Bhajo
Nanda Ke Lal Bhajo (Radhey) (2)
Giridhara Gopala Mathura Natha
Kamala Nayana Hey Kaivalya Dhama [ Nanda Ke ... ]

## Meaning

Worship the dark-hued Lord Krishna, the enchanting Divine Flute Player, the son of Nanda. Worship the lotus-eyed Lord who lifted the mountain and who is the Lord of Mathura.
