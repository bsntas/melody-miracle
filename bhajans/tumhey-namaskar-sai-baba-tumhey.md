## Meaning

Accept our prayerful obeisance O Lord Sai Baba, the Lord of Parthi. You are the one who bears the Shiva Linga and the bestower of supreme bliss. You smear Yourself with Vibhuti (Holy Ash) and You are the most beautiful Sai. O Lord, please accept our offering of love.
