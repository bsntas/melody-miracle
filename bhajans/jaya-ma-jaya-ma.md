## Lyrics

Jaya Ma Jaya Ma
Jaya Shirdi Shankari Ma
Jaya Parthi Eshwari Ma
Shiva Shakthi Sai Ma
Jagat Janani Jaya Jaya Ma

## Meaning

Hail Praises on Mother who is Shankari at Shirdi and Eshwari at Parthi. Victory to the Universal Mother who is Shiva and Shakthi in One.
