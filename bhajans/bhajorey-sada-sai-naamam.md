## Lyrics

Bhajorey Sada Sai Naamam
Pavana Naamam Partheeshwara Naamam
Ishta Pradayaka Naamam
Mruthyu Sanjeevana Naamam
Sathya Sayeeshwara Naamam (Bhajorey)

## Meaning

Chant incessantly the name of Sai, the Lord of Parthi who sanctifies us and whose name works as medicine to cross the cycle of birth the death. Chant the name Sathya Sayeeshwara.
