# Madhava Jaya Keshava Jaya Govinda

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/madhava-jaya-keshava-jaya-govinda)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Narayana, Krishna |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Madhava Jaya Keshava Jaya Govinda Sai Narayana
Govinda Sai Narayana
Gopala Sai Narayana [ Madhava Jaya ... ]
Kalimala Hari Sai Murari
Jaya Baba Jaya Narayana
Parthipurisha Pavana Purusha

## Meaning

Victory to Sai Narayana, the incarnation of Madhava and Gopala. Glory to Sai, the Lord of Parthi and the Supreme being, the destroyer of evil and of the demon Mura.
