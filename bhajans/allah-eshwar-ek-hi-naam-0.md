# Allah Eshwar Ek Hi Naam

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Sarva Dharma, Guru |

## Lyrics

Allah Eshwar Ek Hi Naam
Sab Mil Bolo Hari Ka Naam
Ram Rahim Yeshu Sai Naam
Buddha Zohrashtra Mahavir Naam
Ek Omkar Bolo Jai Sathya Naam
Vahey Guru Vahey Guru Nanak Naam

## Meaning

Let us all sing the name of the Lord who is our Preceptor, Sathya Sai, who has many names: Allah, Hari, Buddha, Zoroaster, Yeshu (Jesus), Mahavir, and Nanak.
