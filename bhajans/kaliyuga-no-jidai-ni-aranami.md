## Lyrics

Kaliyuga no Jidai ni Aranami Koete
Izaya Susumeyo Kami no Mina Tonaete
Om Sai Ram Kono Tamashii ni

## Meaning

Cross through the raging waters of this Kali age. Chant the beautiful name of the Lord and advance forward. My soul resounds with the name Sai Ram and spreads from earth to heaven.
