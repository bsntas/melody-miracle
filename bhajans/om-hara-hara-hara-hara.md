## Lyrics

Om Hara Hara Hara Hara Uma Maheshwara
Gowri Shankara Namah Om
Ksheerabhisheka Priya Shiva Shiva Shambho
Sayeeshwara Namah Om
Shiva Shaktheeshwaraya Namah Om
Om Namah Shivaya Shivaya Namah Om (4)

## Meaning

I pray to the Lord Shiva, Lord of Uma (Parvathi). He is the bestower of auspiciousness and good, the Lord of Gowri. The Lord Shiva who loves to be bathed in milk (abhisheka), Who has now taken the form of Lord Sai, the embodiment of Shiva and Shakthi.
