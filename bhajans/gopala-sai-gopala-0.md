## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Gopala and Govinda (names of Lord Krishna). O Lord Krishna, You fill our hearts to the brim with Your love. You watch over us with Your loving gaze. O Divine Flutist, You are none other than Lord Sathya Sai.
