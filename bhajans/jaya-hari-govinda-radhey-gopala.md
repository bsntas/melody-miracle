## Lyrics

Jaya Hari Govinda Radhey Gopala
Madhura Madhura Muralidhara [ Jaya Hari ... ]
Brinda Vihari Krishna Murari
Meera Ke Prabhu Hey Giridhari
Radha Madhava Damodara

## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Krishna known by many names - Govinda, Gopala, Madhava and Damodara. Glory to the one playing lilting tunes on His Flute, who moved about in Brindavan and who is the dearest Lord of Radha and Meera.
