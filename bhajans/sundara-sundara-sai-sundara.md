## Lyrics

Sundara Sundara Sai Sundara
Sundara Nanda Gopala
Gopala Sai Gopala
Murali Madhava Mohana Roopa
Munijana Manasa Chora
Chitha Chora Maakhan Chora

## Meaning

Salutations to the enchanting son of Nanda, Lord Gopala (Krishna), the one who loves the music of the flute, the one who steals butter as well as the hearts of sages and the one who is verily Lord Sai.
