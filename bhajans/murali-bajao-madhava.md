## Lyrics

Murali Bajao Madhava
Giridhara Bala Gopala [ Murali Bajao ... ]
Madhura Madhura Hari Naam Bajao
Mathura Natha Sai Natha

## Meaning

O Lord Madhava (Krishna), play the melodious flute. O Lord Gopala (Krishna), You are the one who held the Govardhana mountain high to protect the cows. Sing the sweet name of Hari (Lord Vishnu) and Krishna, the Lord of Mathura who is none other than our Lord Sai.
