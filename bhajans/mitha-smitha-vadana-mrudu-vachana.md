## Lyrics

Mitha Smitha Vadana Mrudu Vachana
Mangala Charana Madhusudhana
Ravi Shata Koti Prakashana
Sai Ramana Sanathana

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Krishna who has a smiling face and a soft spoken tongue. We bow to His auspicious Feet. He is also called Madhusudhana (slayer of demon Madhu). He is effulgent as a million suns. He is the eternal Lord Sai.
