## Lyrics

Ooinaru Mezame no Shifuku no Hana wo
Kie Suru Kokoro ni Sakase tamae

## Meaning

O Magnificent Lord, You bloom the flowers of supreme bliss within my heart. You hold our hands and walk together with us on this long journey towards enlightenment. O Buddha, You are the light of Love.
