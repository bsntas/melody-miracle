## Lyrics

Tu eres el Amor de este corazon
Y eres esa Luz que brilla en mi interior
Tu eres la dulzura que embriaga el alma
Y eres la ternura que me da calma
Tu eres la sonrisa en labios infantiles
Y eres el suspiro en los an'os seniles
T eres la esperanza en la juventud
Y eres la Conciencia en la Plenitud
Tu eres Esto y eres Aquello
Y eres Todo a mi alrededor
Tu eres el Padre y eres la Madre
Y eres el duen'o de mi corazon

## Meaning

You Are the Love of this heart and You are the bright light that is inside me. You are the sweetness that engrosses the soul and You are the tenderness that calms me. You are the smile in the infant's lips and You are the sigh of relief to the old. You are the hope for the young and You are the Consciousness for the Plentiful (liberated soul). You are This, You are That and You are everything around me. You are the Father and You are the Mother and You are the Owner of my heart.
