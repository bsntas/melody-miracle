## Lyrics

Satchidananda Paramananda
Devaki Nanda Mukunda Radhey Govinda (Vasu)
Anatha Natha Deena Natha
Prashanthi Vasa Shanthi Pradatha
Sai Ananda Mukunda Radhey Govinda

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Sai, the very embodiment of Truth, Consiousness and Bliss, who resides in Prashanthi Nilayam. He is none other than Lord Krishna, the son of Devaki and the beloved of Radha. Glory to Lord Sai, the saviour of needy and downtrodden and the bestower of peace.
