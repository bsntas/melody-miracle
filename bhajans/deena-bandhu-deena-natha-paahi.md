| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Sai |

## Lyrics

Deena Bandhu Deena Natha Paahi Sai Shankara
Hey Trinetra Hey Pavitra Vimala Charitha Raksha Maam
Parama Purusha Parthi Nivasa Sathya Sai Shankara

## Meaning

O Supreme Lord Sai, You are the Lord and kinsman of the downtrodden, You are verily the three-eyed Lord Shankara (Shiva). Kindly protect me, the purest and spotless Lord of Parthi.
