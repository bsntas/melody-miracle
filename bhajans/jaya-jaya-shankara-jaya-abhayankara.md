## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Shankara Jaya Abhayankara
Parvathi Shankara Sri Sai Shankara
Srishti Sthithi Laya Karana Karana
Mruthyunjaya Gananayaka Nayaka
Uma Pathey Shiva Shankara Shankara
Gangadhara Jagadeeshwara Eshwara

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Shankara (Shiva), the consort of Parvathi and the one who protects us from fear. He is responsible for creation, sustenance and destruction of the Universe, the one who has won over death, the Lord of the Ganaas, the Lord of Uma, the one who holds Mother Ganges on His head and the Lord of the Universe. He is none other than Lord Sai.
