## Lyrics

Jaya Gowri Vara Sugunalaya
Jaya Jagadeeshwara Niramaya [ Jaya Gowri ... ]
Jaya Partheeshwara Premalaya (2)
Karunalaya Sathya Dharmalaya
Prashanthi Nilaya Dayamaya

## Meaning

Victory to the Lord of the Universe, Lord Shiva, the beloved of Mother Gowri, is a paragon of virtues and is blemishless. He is none of than Lord Sai, compassionate one, the embodiment of Truth and Righteousness who has Prashanthi Nilayam as His Abode.
