## Lyrics

Hrudayanta Ranga Sai Nandana Vana Vihari
Jaya Yadu Nandana Jaya Ghana Shyam (2)

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Sai, the indweller of the heart, who is verily Lord Krishna, the deep blue complexioned son of Yadu lineage, the one who dwells in the Nandana Forest.
