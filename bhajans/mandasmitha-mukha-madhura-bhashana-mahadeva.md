## Lyrics

Mandasmitha Mukha Madhura Bhashana Mahadeva Shambho

Linga Manohara Nandi Vahana Mahadeva Shambho
Mahadeva Shambho Hara Hara Mahadeva Shambho (2)

## Meaning

Glory to the Lord of Lords, Lord Shiva who has a happy and smiling face, who speaks sweet words and bestows good fortune. He uses the bull for a conveyance and is portrayed and worshipped in the form of a graceful Linga. He is the one who burnt Manmata (God of Love) to ashes (for disturbing Lord Shiva during His penance).
