## Meaning

Salutations to the holy feet of Lord Sai residing in Puttaparthi who is verily Lord Subrahmanya. Also known as Karthikeya, He is the son of Shiva (who resides in the Arunchala hill) and the Lord of Valli. He is the ocean of mercy, sustainer of His devotees, and the refuge of the impoverished. Surrender to Lord Subrahmanya.
