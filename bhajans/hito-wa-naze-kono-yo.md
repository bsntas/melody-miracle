## Lyrics

Hito wa Naze Kono Yo ni Umare Nani wo Sureba Iito Iuno
Watashi wa Dare Doko kara Kitano Doko he Yukeba Ito Iuno
Yama wa Takaku, Umi wa Aoku, Tsuki wa Teru Nami no Ue
Shiawase Motome, Ai wo Motome, Shinri no Tobira Akeru noyo

## Meaning

For what purpose are people born into this world? Who am I? Where have I come from? Where do I go in this life? This world is beautiful with the tall mountains, the blue oceans, and the moon shining above the waves. Lord, we have come to seek Your happiness and love and to open the door of our hearts to truth.
