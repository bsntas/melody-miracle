## Lyrics

Kaliyuga no Jidai ni Korareta Kami no Keshin
Shinri no Gonge Shiawase no Kami Ai no Keshin
Kurushimi Nayami Kanashimi Nageku Subete no Mono no tame
Shinri to Dharma wo Toki Gokourin Nasareta

## Meaning

You are the reincarnation that has come to us in the Kali age. You are the reincarnation of truth, happiness, and love. You have come to wipe away all our pain, worries and grief. Your descent to Earth has given us truth and righteousness.
