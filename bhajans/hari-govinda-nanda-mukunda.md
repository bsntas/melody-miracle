## Lyrics

Hari Govinda Nanda Mukunda
Keshava Narayana

## Meaning

Chant the different names of Lord Narayana, Hari, Govinda, Madhava, Sridhara. Glory to the one who is supreme Bliss, the one who dwells in Parthi as Lord Sai.
