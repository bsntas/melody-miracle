## Meaning

O Lord Sai, Your sacred name when chanted redeems one from fear of worldly miseries and destroys the sins. Your abode Puttaparthi is sacred. Your darshan, worship, bhajan everything is sacred. O Lord Sai Narayana, You are the embodiment of the primordial sound Om (Pranava). Let it be known that Sai is the Supreme Lord.
