## Lyrics

Govinda Rama Gopala Rama
Keshava Madhava Sai Rama
Janaki Jeevana Sita Rama
Sri Rama Jaya Rama Jaya Jaya Rama

## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Rama the consort of Sita. Chant the Holy names of Lord Krishna - Gopala, Keshava, Madhava and Lord Sathya Sai.
