## Lyrics

Anata to Ikiru, Tomo ni Ikiru, Baba is my life.

## Meaning

Divine Baba, I like with You for You are my life. I laugh with You, I walk with You because You are my very life. You fill me with tranquility and supreme bliss every single day.
