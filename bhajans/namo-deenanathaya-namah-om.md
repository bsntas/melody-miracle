# Namo Deenanathaya Namah Om

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Shiva |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Namo Deenanathaya Namah Om
Namo Devadi Devaya Namah Om
Namo Trishoola Dharaya Namah Om
Namo Trinetra Dharaya Namah Om
Namo Kailasa Vasaya Namah Om
Namo Shirdi Vasaya Namah Om
Namo Parthi Vasaya Namah Om [ Namo Devadi ... ]

## Meaning

Bow to Lord of Lords, Lord Shiva, the protector of the weak and forsaken who bears the trident and has three eyes (third eye wisdom). He resides in Kailasa, Shirdi, and Parthi.
