## Lyrics

Jai Jai Bhavani Durga Mangala Pradayini
Bhakthi Mukthi Dayini Phanindra Mani Bhooshani [ Jai Jai ... ]
Kaali Mahakaali Jai Jai Shiva Shakthi Jai (2)

## Meaning

Glory be to Mother Bhavani, bestower of Auspiciousness, Devotion and Liberation, adorned with precious Gems. Victory to the One who is personification of Shiva and Shakthi and Mahakaali.
