## Lyrics

Hey Ma Durga Bhavani Ma

## Meaning

Victory be to Mother Durga who removes the distress of Her devotees, who is the redeemer of the world, the consort of Lord Shiva. Mother Durga is verily Mother Sai who dwells in Parthi.
