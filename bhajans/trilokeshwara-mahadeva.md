## Meaning

I pray to Lord Shiva, who is the Lord of the three worlds. He is adorned with snakes and is the consort of Goddess Parvathi. He is the Lord of the Lords who as incarnated as Lord Sai. He dances to the rhythm of the drum. Glory to Lord Shiva.
