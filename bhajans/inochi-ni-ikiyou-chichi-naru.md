## Meaning

Let us live our lives within God. Fill your lives eternally with love and light. Let us live our lives with these thoughts. Let your heart be comforted by this voice inside of us. The universe is one, everything is God. All life is one, everything is God. Truth is one, everything is God. Heart is one, everything is God.
