## Lyrics

Shankara Shankara Samba Shiva
Samba Sadashiva Sai Shiva [ Shankara ... ]
Chandramouli Sadashiva
Sadguru Sathya Sai Shiva

## Meaning

Salutations to You Oh Lord Shankara (also known as Samba Shiva) who is worshipped as Sadashiva and Chandramouli we offer our salutations to you Oh Lord and universal Preceptor Sathya Sai Shiva.
