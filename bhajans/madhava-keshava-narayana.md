## Lyrics

Tribhuvana Bharana Narayana (Hey)

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Narayana, the one who reclines on the serpent Adi Sesha. Glory to the one who is also known as Madhava and Keshava. Glory to the remover of the fear of birth and death, the one who is wholly devoted to the promotion of Vedas and the Lord of the three worlds.
