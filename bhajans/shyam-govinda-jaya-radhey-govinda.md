## Lyrics

Shyam Govinda Jaya Radhey Govinda Jaya
Sai Govinda Jaya Madhava
Gopala Krishna Jaya Gopi Manohara Jaya
Madana Mohana Keshava
Bhajo Sadguru Naam
Harey Krishna Harey Ram
Paahi (Sai) Paada Pallava

## Meaning

Victory to the enchantingly beautiful Lord Govinda (Krishna) who is also glorified as Shyama, Madhava, Gopala and Keshava. He is Lord of Radha and the Gopikas. Sing the names of the Divine Preceptor Sai, Lord Krishna and Lord Rama. Surrender to the Lotus Feet of Lord Sai who is verily Lord Krishna.
