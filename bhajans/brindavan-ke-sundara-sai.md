## Lyrics

Brindavan Ke Sundara Sai
Parthi Puri Ke Pavana Sai
Bhaktha Janon Ke Leela Sai
Madhava Sai Mathura Sai
Mangala Dayi Baba Sai
Baba Sai Leela Sai (2)

## Meaning

O beautiful Sai of Brindavan, who is all sacredness, with Parthi as Your abode, You are the saviour of the devotees. O Lord Madhava, the dweller of Mathura (You are verily the embodiment of sweetness). Glory to Lord Sai, the bestower of auspiciousness.
