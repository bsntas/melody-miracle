## Lyrics

Sri Sai Natha Krishna Krishna (2)

## Meaning

Glory to the annihilator of demon Madhu, Lord Krishna, the son of Devaki and Vasudeva. Glory to the Lord of this Universe. You are also the charming lad of mother Yashoda and King Nanda. O Lord Sai, You are none other than Lord Krishna.
