## Lyrics

Brahmananda Guru Premananda Guru
Sai Guru Deva Sharanam
Bhaja Shankara Hari Hara Sharanam
Vishwanath Deva Gowri Manohara
Sai Natha Baba Parameshwara
(Bhajo) Shiva Shambho Shiva Sharanam

## Meaning

Surrender to our Divine Preceptor, Lord Sai, who is the embodiment of love and bliss. Chant the name of Lord Shiva, consort of Gowri and the lord of the universe, Sai Baba.
