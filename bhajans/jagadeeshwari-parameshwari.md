## Meaning

Sing the glory to Mother Sai who is the Supreme Mother residing at Puttaparthi. She is none other than Mother Annapoorna and the Mother of the Universe who uplifts Her devotees. She is verily Mothers Parvathi, Lakshmi, Saraswathi, Lalita, Gayathri and Kamakshi (Parvathi).
