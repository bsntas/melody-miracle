## Lyrics

Niji Iro no Hikari no Naka ni Kagayaku Kami Sai Baba
Ai ni Iyasare Yuruyaka ni Kokoro no Tsubomi wa Hana Hiraku
Kiyorakana Akari wo Tomasu, Guru Sathya Sai Baba
Ai no Hikari wo Hanatsu Guru Sathya Sai Baba

## Meaning

Sai Baba, Your rainbow light shines within me. Love gently heals me and blooms within my heart from a bud into a flower. You shine like a pure light, Guru Sathya Sai Baba. You release Your light of love to us, Guru Sathya Sai Baba.
