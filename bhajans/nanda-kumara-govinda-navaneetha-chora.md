## Meaning

I sing of the son of Nanda, the protector of cows; the One Who stole butter and who is the Lord of cows (all beings)! Oh Lord of Lakshmi! You are One that protects those that seek refuge in you. You are Sairam, you are Sai Krishna. You are the One that is compassionate towards the downtrodden and you are the form of Divine Love. You are the protector of cows (all beings), Lord Vishnu.
