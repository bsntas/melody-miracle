## Meaning

Sing the praises of Lord Madhava (Krishna) who regales the mind of devotees, the dark complexioned Lord of the Yadu clan, the slayer of demons Mura and Madhu. He is the protector who held aloft the Mandara mountain and moves about in the forests of Madhu (Madhuvan - Brindavan).
