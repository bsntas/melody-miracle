## Lyrics

Sathya Swaroopa Sai Rama
Sri Rama Jaya Rama Jaya Sai Rama [ Sathya Swaroopa ... ]

## Meaning

Lord Sai of Parthi is the embodiment of truth. He is Lord Rama, Vishnu and the eternal Brahman.
