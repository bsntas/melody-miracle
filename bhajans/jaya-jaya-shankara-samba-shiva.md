## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Shankara Samba Shiva
Tribhuvana Dharaka Maheshwara [ Jaya Jaya ... ]
Gangadhari Kailasa Vasi
Vibhuti Sundara Parthi Nivasi

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Shankara (Shiva), consort of Amba (Parvathi). Victory to the one who bears the three worlds, who holds the Holy Ganges on His head, who dwells in Kailasa and who has smeared Holy Ash all over His body. He is verily Lord Sai, the resident of Parthi.
