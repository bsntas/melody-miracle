## Lyrics

Sada Bhajorey Hari Naama Bhajo
Madhura Sudha Rasa Hari Naama Bhajo [ Sada Bhajo ... ]
Sri Raghu Rama Hey Ghana Shyama

## Meaning

Constantly sing the name of Hari, the name that confers the sweet nectar of immortality. Chant the names of Rama and Krishna. Chant the names of Parvathi and Shankara (Shiva).
