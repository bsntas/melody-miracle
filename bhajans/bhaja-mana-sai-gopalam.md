## Meaning

O mind, chant the name of Lord Gopala (Krishna) who fosters the cowherd, who grants boons and who delights the mind of the Gopis. Chant the name of the one who moves about in Brindavan and who loves art and music. He is none other than the Lord of Parthi, Lord Sai.
