## Lyrics

Adi Nama Sathyanarayana

Chandra Vadana Kamala Nayana

## Meaning

O mind, chant the primal name of Lord Sathya Narayana, and take refuge at the feet of our divine Preceptor Sai.. Lord Sai is the embodiment of Lord Vishnu who reclines on the serpent (Adi Sesha) and who has an effulgent face like the moon and eyes beautiful as the lotus.
