## Meaning

Glory to the resident of Parthi, Lord Sai, the one who conquers our hearts, the one who fills us with Love and who is the very source of Love. O Master of the divine song, I sing to You. Shower Peace on me with your Divine Voice. Shower Peace on me with Om.
