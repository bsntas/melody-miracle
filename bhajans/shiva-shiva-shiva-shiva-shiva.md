## Meaning

Worship Lord Shiva who is called by many names such as, Shankara, Gangadhara (the one who supports the sacred river Ganga on His head), Hara, Gowreeshwara (Consort of Mother Gowri), Chandrakaladhara (having crescent moon on the head), Haalaa-hala dhara (the one who swallowed the poison to save Gods), Yugadhara (the one who upholds all eons), Jatadhara (the one with matted hair). Glory to Lord Sai, Lord of Parthi who is none other than the Lord Shiva who adorns the serpent around His neck.
