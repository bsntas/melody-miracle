## Lyrics

Janardhana Jagannatha
Jaya Jaya Deva Jagadeesha
Sadguru Brahma Sanathana
Sadashiva Sai Narayana

## Meaning

Victory to the Lord of the Universe and loved by people. You are our sacred Preceptor who is Eternal. You are Shiva as well as Narayana.
