## Lyrics

Prathah Smaranam Surya Charanam
Bhanu Kiranam Sada Smaranam
Malinya Haranam Jyothi Bharanam
Aditya Madhuram Shanthi Sadanam

## Meaning

In the early morning, remember and worship the feet of Surya, the Sun God, who shines upon us His brilliant rays. He swiftly removes the blanket of darkness and sweetly provides light that illuminates our world, creating an abode of Divine Peace.
