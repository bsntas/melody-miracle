## Lyrics

Jaya Krishna Mukunda Murarey
Jaya Govinda Brinda Vihari [ Jaya Krishna ... ]
Mana Mohana Madhava Shourie

## Meaning

Victory to Krishna, Mukunda, Govinda, Madhava, Murarey, the One who moves about in Brindavan and entices the Mind, the Valorous, is the Lord of Meera's Devotion.
