## Lyrics

Jaya Hari Govinda Jaya Narayana
Vasudeva Nandana Sri Madhusudhana ( Jaya Hari ... )
Karunanidhe Hey Madhava Mohana
Parama Niranjana Yashoda Nandana
Jaya Narayana Jaya Narayana
Sai Narayana Sathya Narayana

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Govinda, Hari, Narayana (names of Lord Vishnu), and Krishna, the beloved son of Vasudeva and the one who slayed the demon Madhu. He is the treasure house of compassion, is supremely pure son of Yashoda and is ever pleasing to His devotees. Victory to Lord Sai who is verily Lord Krishna.
