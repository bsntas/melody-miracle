## Lyrics

Eres la luna que florece de luz
Supremo Señor de la Verdad
Eres el nombre que nos salvará
Señor de Parthi me inclino ante ti
Mi bienamado me rindo a tus pies
(Sai) Tu luz eterna enciende mi ser

## Meaning

O Lord Sathya Sai, You are the moon whose light shines brightly, You are the supreme being of truth, Your name is the one who will save us. I bow down before You, O Lord of Parthi. I surrender at Your feet, my beloved. Your light burns eternally within me.
