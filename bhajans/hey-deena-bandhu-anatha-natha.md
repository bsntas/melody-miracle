## Lyrics

Hey Deena Bandhu Anatha Natha

## Meaning

Our Lord Sai is that Supreme Consciousness, the uplifter of the downtrodden and refuge of the forlorn. Lord Sai is also our mentor and the source of succor to the orphaned.
