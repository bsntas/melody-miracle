## Meaning

Salutations to You, O Lord Sai who dwells at Parthi, who is verily Gopala, Govinda, Panduranga. You are the compassionate one who had earlier incarnated as Krishna in the Yadu lineage and moved about in Brindavan as the son of Nanda.
