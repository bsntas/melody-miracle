# Jaya Narayani Durga Lakshmi

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Devi |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Jaya Narayani Durga Lakshmi
Jaya Jagadambe Ambe Bhavani
Jaya Jaya Shirdi Sai Bhavani
Kamale Kamini Hari Narayani
Parthi Nivasini Sai Narayani

## Meaning

Victory to Mother Goddess Durga, mother of the Universe, and to Lord Sai of Shirdi and Parthi; to the consort of Vishnu who loves lotus flowers.
