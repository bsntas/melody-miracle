## Lyrics

Govinda Radhey, Govinda Radhey, Govinda Govinda Gopala Radhey
Venu Vilola, Vijaya Gopala, Govinda Govinda Gopala Radhey [ Govinda Radhey ... ]
Bhaktha Vatsala, Bhaagavatha Priya, Govinda Govinda Gopala Radhey

## Meaning

Chant the names of Lord Krishna - Govinda and Gopala, the beloved Radha. Glory to the enthralling Flutist. O victorious Lord Gopala, glory to Thee who is dear to Your devotees and who showers motherly love.
