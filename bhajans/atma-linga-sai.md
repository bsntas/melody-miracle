## Lyrics

Atma Linga Sai Sai Sathya Sai

Jyothir Linga Sai Sai Sathya Sai
Netra Linga Sai Sai Sathya Sai

## Meaning

O Lord Sathya Sai, You in the form of Atma Linga (created by You from within), in the form of Amrutha Linga (immortal symbol), Jyothir Linga (the effulgent one) and Netra Linga (the symbol with eyes).
