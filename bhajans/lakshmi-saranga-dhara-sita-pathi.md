## Lyrics

Lakshmi Saranga Dhara Sita Pathi Raghuveera Radhey Govinda
Rakumayi Panduranga
Sathya Sai Panduranga

## Meaning

The valiant one of Raghu dynasty, Lord Rama, who holds the bow, is the Lord of Sita, the incarnation of Goddess Lakshmi. Chant the names of Govinda, the Lord of Radha and Panduranga, the Lord of Rakumayi. Lord Sathya Sai is none other than Lord Panduranga.
