## Meaning

Lord Sai, You are the peace and tranquility within us and everywhere in this world. Every day we pray, love, and express our gratitude for You are the joyous and delightful Maitreya (the Buddha who is believed to incarnate in the future and who will enlighten everyone with His teachings). You shine Your light of love around the world so that everything can be one.
