Karunapaara Sai Avatara

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, You are the little Krishna who lifted the mountain, the enchanting flute player, the beloved of Gopis and Lord of Radha. You are the stealer of butter, the beloved of Nanda and the destroyer of demon Madhu. You have descended in the form of Sai, the most compassionate one.
