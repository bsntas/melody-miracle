## Lyrics

Eshwari Nandana Sai Narayana Narayana
Narayana Sathya Narayana
Kaliyug Mey Avatar Liye
Kan Kan Mey Tera Naam (3)

## Meaning

O Lord Sai Narayana, the son of Eshwaramba, You are the embodiment of Truth and the one who pervades this Universe. O Lord Sai, You are the Supreme One, who has donned a Human Frame in this age of Kali for all of us. O Lord Sai, every little particle of this creation reverberates with Your Divine Name.
