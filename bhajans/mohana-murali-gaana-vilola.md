## Lyrics

Mohana Murali Gaana Vilola
Jaya Brindavana Lola [ Mohana Murali ... ]
Yashoda Tanaya Nandalala
Gopi Vallabha Gopala
Gopala Gopala
Gopala Nanda Gopala

## Meaning

He who enchants with his songs on the flute, victory to the regaler of Brindavan, child of Yashoda and darling of Nanda, beloved of the Gopis, He is Gopala (Krishna).
