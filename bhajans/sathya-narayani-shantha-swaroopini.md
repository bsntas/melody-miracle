## Lyrics

Sathya Narayani Shantha Swaroopini
Mangala Vadani Malini
Maya Vilasini Maha Kamini
Mahishasura Mardhini Harini

## Meaning

Glory to you Oh mother Sathya Narayani who is the embodiment of eternal bliss and peace whose countenance is so enchanting and soft like a creeper and emanates radiance and auspiciousness.
