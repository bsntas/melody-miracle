## Meaning

Atma Rama (the Indweller), You are the light within me. Lord Sai Rama, You are the truth of my existence. You are Atma Rama, Sai Rama, Sai Krishna, and Sathya Sai Baba. Atma Rama, You dwell in everything. Lord Sai Rama, You fulfill everything in the universe. You are the fountain of supreme bliss.
