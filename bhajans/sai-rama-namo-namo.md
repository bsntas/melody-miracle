## Meaning

Chant the names of Sai Rama, Sai Krishna, the one residing in Shirdi and in Parthi, the one who is the upholder of this Universe, the one who is the personification of the Primordial sound "OM", the one who is always at Peace and who can appear before the devotee at will.
