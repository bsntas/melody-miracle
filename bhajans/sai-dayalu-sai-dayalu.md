## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai, the compassionate one who is the Lord of Parthi. He is the protector of devotees and who is also the Lord of Dwarakamayi at Shirdi. He is the most compassionate one who plays sweet music on the flute (Murali) in His incarnation as Lord Krishna. He has incarnated on this earth in this deluding Human Form.
