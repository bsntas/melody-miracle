# Narayani Namo Narayani Naada Svarupini

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/narayani-namo-narayani-naada-svarupini)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Devi |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Narayani Namo Narayani Naada Svarupini Narayani
Jagadodharini Narayani
Narayana Priya Manohari
Ambe Narayani Lakshmi Narayani
Sathya Narayani Sai Narayani

## Meaning

Pay obeisance to the Mother Goddess, embodiment of sound (vibration), sustainer of the Universe, beautiful and beloved consort of Vishnu. She has presently incarnated as Sai.
