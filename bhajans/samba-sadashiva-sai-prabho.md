## Lyrics

Samba Sadashiva Sai Prabho
Shambho Mahadeva Dehi Vibho
Jaya Ganga Jatadhari Gowri Pathey
Sathya Sai Mahadeva Parthi Pathey

## Meaning

O Lord Sathya Sai of Parthi, You are Lord Shiva, who supports the sacred river Ganga in Your matted hair. O Lord, we seek Your protection.
