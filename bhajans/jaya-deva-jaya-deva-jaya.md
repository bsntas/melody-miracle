## Lyrics

Jaya Deva Jaya Deva Jaya Guru Deva

## Meaning

Victory to the Sacred Preceptor Sai, who is verily of Truth, Awareness and Bliss. Sing the praises of Guru Nanak and Guru Govinda. Glory to Lord Sai, the embodiment of the Supreme Self.
