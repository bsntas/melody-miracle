## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai who is an infinite ocean of nectarine Love and who bestows bliss. He is the beautiful Sai of Shirdi and Parthi. He is the very embodiment of compassion. He is the Lord of the Universe, Lord of Parthi and our hearts. He is the Protector of the downtrodden and all those who surrender to Him. Glory to Lord Sai, the Supreme Lord who incarnated for the sake of His devotees.
