## Meaning

Prostrations to Shiva, One who has the moon on his crown of hair, One who is the great dancer of the Tandava (cosmic dance), who as Sathya Sai creates the divine Linga, Prostrations to that Sai Natha, Smeared in beautiful vibhuti, The one and only Lord, He is Sai of Shirdi.
