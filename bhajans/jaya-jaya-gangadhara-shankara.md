## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Gangadhara Shankara
Jaya Jaya Gowri Vara Shankara [ Jaya Jaya Gangadhara ... ]
Ardha Shareera Alankruta Girija
Parthi Pureeshwara Pavana Charitha
Palita Bhuvana Aashritha Phalada

## Meaning

Sing the Glory of the one who holds Mother Ganges on His head, Lord Shiva, the beloved of Mother Gowri. Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi is verily Lord Shiva whose half body is adorned by Mother Girija and who nourishes the world, grants welfare and has a sacred history.
