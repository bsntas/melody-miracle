## Meaning

O Lord Sai, You are beauty personified having captivating look, unstained from the world and attribute-less. You are verily Lord Narayana with Mother Lakshmi as consort who resides in Your heart. You are verily Lord Krishna who lifted the Govardhana mountain on Your finger tip. You are the most compassionate one who removes the miseries of Your devotees. You are the resident of Srirangam and the destroyer of demon Keshi.
