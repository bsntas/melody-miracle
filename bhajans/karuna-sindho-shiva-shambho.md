## Meaning

The eternally Auspicious Shiva with Mother Amba (Parvathi), is the same as Lord Sai, the lotus eyed, embodiment of compassion and is verily the Lord of the Kali age who destroys its evil effects. Chant 'Shiva Shambho' repeatedly.
