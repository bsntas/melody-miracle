## Lyrics

Rama Krishna Rama Krishna Rama Krishna Bhajorey
Sri Hari Madhava Govinda Keshava [ Rama Krishna ... ]
Nanda Nanda Shyama Govinda
Pandari Natha Panduranga Bhajorey

## Meaning

Sing the Glory of Rama, Krishna, Hari, Madhava, Govinda and Keshava. Sing the Glory of Shyama, the son of Nanda and Panduranga, the Lord of Pandharpur.
