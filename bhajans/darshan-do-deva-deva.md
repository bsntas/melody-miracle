## Lyrics

Darshan Do Deva Deva
Daasharathey Dayabhi Rama [ Darshan Do ... ]
Dharma Uddhara Daya Nidhey

## Meaning

O Lord Rama, bless us with Your Divine Vision. You are the son of Dasharatha. You are the compassionate one, upholder of righteousness and the reserve of kindness. You are protector and sustainer of Your devotees who take refuge in You.
