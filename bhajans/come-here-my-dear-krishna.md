## Meaning

Come dear Lord Krishna, I have made a building for You inside my heart. I have made food for You, dear Lord. You asked for butter, sweets and millk but why do You delay? I shed tears of yearning remembering You everyday. Please come to my house and I will wave You the Arathi. Why are You staying far away from me, O Lord, please come to my heart where I have made a building for You.
