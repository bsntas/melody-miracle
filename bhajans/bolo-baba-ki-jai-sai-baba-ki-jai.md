## Lyrics

Bolo Baba Ki Jai Sai Baba Ki Jai
O Shirdi Baba Ki Jai Parthi Baba Ki Jai [ Bolo Baba Ki ... ]
Treta Yug Mey Ram Roop Dwapar Mey Krishna Kanhaiyi
Kaliyug Mey Sai Shakthi Jad Chaitan Mey Hai Samayi
Har Avartaron Mey Roop Tera Sai Kan Kan Mey Tu Samaya
Prashanthi Vasi Sai Shankar Teri Adbhuth Maya

## Meaning

Victory to Thee, O Lord Sai of Shirdi and Parthi who incarnated as Lord Rama in Treta age, Lord Krishna in Dwapara age. In this Kali age, You are the Cosmic Energy that encompasses both the animate and inanimate objects. O Lord, every incarnation is Your divine Form. You are the Creator of this wonderful Illusion and You pervade every atom. Prashanthi Nilayam is Your divine abode.
