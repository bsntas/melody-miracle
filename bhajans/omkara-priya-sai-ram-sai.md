## Meaning

Sai Ram, the embodiment of Omkara is, the same Rama who was the jewel of the lineage of Raghu. He is the compassionate Lord of Sita, who liberated the devotee Ahalya, The indweller who is always in Divine Bliss. Victory to that Ram, who is our Sai Ram.
