## Lyrics

Rama Naam Smaranam Samastha Paapa Haranam
Kaama Krodha Haranam Raghuveera Naama Smaranam
Deena Janodharanam Bhavaabdhi Dukha Haranam

## Meaning

Contemplating on the Name of Rama destroys all sins. Remembering the Name of the Hero of Raghu Dynasty (Rama) destroys desire and anger. Serving the hapless and the forlorn destroys the sorrows of the cycle of birth and death.
