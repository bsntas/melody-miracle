## Meaning

I Give All My Love To You Baba; I Give To You What You have Given To Me. I Give All My efforts To You Baba; I Give To You What You have Given To Me. I Give All My Thoughts To You Baba. I Give All My Life To You Baba; I Give To You What You have Given To Me. I Give All My Life To You Baba. I dedicate myself and all that I do to You. I give my life, my work, and my mind to You. I am love because You loved me first. So I give everything to You for You have given everything to me.
