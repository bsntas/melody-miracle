## Meaning

O Lord Gajanana (the elephant headed god), the beloved son of Mother Girija (Parvathi) and Lord Shiva, You are the one who protects us from fear and liberates one from the cycle of birth and death. You are the lover of music and destroyer of demons and protector of weak and meek.
