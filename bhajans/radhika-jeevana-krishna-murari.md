### Raga Notes

```
Notes:Also known as Megh Malhar, this Raga is very similar to Madhmad Sarang. The difference lies in the rendering of the notes esp. R and N. R is rendered by touching M, and N by touching P (while descending).
```

## Lyrics

Radhika Jeevana Krishna Murari
Govardhana Dhari Hrudaya Vihari [ Radhika Jeevana ... ]
Meera Ke Prabhu Hey Giridhari
Parthi Vihari Sai Murari
Hey Mana Mohana Sharana Tihari

## Meaning

O Lord Sai residing in Parthi, You are Lord Krishna, the life of Radha, You are the resident in all hearts of devotees, You are the Lord of Meera, You are the one who lifted the hillock Govardhana aloft to save the cowherd community. We surrender to You, the most enchanting Lord.
