## Lyrics

Sri Hari Bol Sri Hari Bol Mukunda Madhava Hari Hari Bol
Ek Prabhu Ke Aneka Naam Prema Se Bolo Sai Bhagawan
Sai Bhagawan Bolo Sai Bhagawan

## Meaning

Chant Hari's name who is also called Mukunda and Madhava. There is one God, but He has many names. He is also known by the name Sai Baba. Chant Lord Sai's name with love.
