## Lyrics

Nanda Nandana Navaneetha Chora Brindavana Murari
Shyam Sundara Madana Mohana Brindavana Murari
Karuna Sagara Kamala Nayana Brindavana Murari  [ Shyama Sundara ... ]

## Meaning

Glory to the son of Nanda, Lord Krishna, the one who stole butter and who played in Brindavan. O Lord, You are the dark complexioned one with a beautiful and enchanting form and the one who resides in Brindavan. Glory to the the lotus-eyed one who is an ocean of compassion.

(Alt Lyrics: Lord Sai who resides in Puttaparthi is none other than Lord Vishnu, the one with a lotus in the navel and Lord Krishna, the one who adorns Brindavan).
