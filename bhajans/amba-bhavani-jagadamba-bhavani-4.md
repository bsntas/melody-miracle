## Lyrics

Amba Bhavani Jagadamba Bhavani (4)

Amba Bhavani Jagadamba Bhavani

## Meaning

O Universal Mother Bhavani (Parvathi), You are the Mother of the world, the resident of Sri Chakra (divine symbol), the bestower of bliss and knowledge and the most effulgent one. You are the embodiment of Omkara (primordial sound) who has taken the Form of Lord Sai.
