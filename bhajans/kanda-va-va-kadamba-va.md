## Meaning

Please come to us, O Lord Kanda, Kadamba, Muruga, Vela (various names of Lord Subrahmanya), Welcome O beautiful son of Lord Siva and Sakthi (Parvathi), who vanquished the demon Soorapadman. Welcome O Lord Shanmukha residing in the Holy abode of Thanigai (Thirutthani) and Thiruchendur. You are none other than Lord Sai residing in Puttaparthi. You are the one who emerged as six beautiful children from the Holy spark of Lord Siva's third eye. O Lord of Prashanthi, please protect us.
