## Lyrics

Radhey Radhey Radhey Shyam Rakumayi Vittala
Jaya Hari Sri Hari Sai Hari Vittala [ Radhey Radhey ... ]
Panduranga Vittala Pandari Natha Vittala
Purandara Vittala Pundarikaaksha Vittala

## Meaning

Sing the name of Vittala, beloved of Radha and Rukmini. Victory to Lord Hari, Lord Sai, Lord Panduranga Vittala of Pandharpur. He is the Lord of Saint Purandara Dasa and the liberator of devotee Pundarika.
