## Lyrics

Sai Ram Bolo Sathya Sai Ram Bolo
Sita Vallabha Tulasi Rama Lakshmana Sevitha Ram

## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Rama and Lord Sathya Sai who is verily Lord Rama. Glory be to Lord Rama, the consort of Mother Sita and the one served by Lakshmana. Sing the praises of the indweller, eternally blissful, compassionate Lord who liberated Ahalya. He is the one residing in our hearts, the one served by Hanuman.
