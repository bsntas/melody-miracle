## Lyrics

Ganesha Agameyo Inori no Kokoro wo Sathya Sai eto Todoke Tamou
Eichi to Yuuki no Chikara no Kami yo Hikari no Michi wo Terasu Kami
Subete no Inochi no Jiai no Kami yo Shinri to Seigi no Gajanana
Ai no Chikara de Michibiki tamae Sathya Sai eto Tsuzuku Michi

## Meaning

Worship our Lord Ganesha and pray with all your heart. O Lord, with Your wisdom and courage, You shine bright revealing our path. You are the Lord of truth, justice, and the love within everything. With the power of love, we advance forward on the path You have shown us.
