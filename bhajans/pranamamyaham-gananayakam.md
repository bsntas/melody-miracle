## Lyrics

Pranamamyaham Gananayakam (2)
Pranamamyaham Gowri Sutham
Pranamamyaham Shiva Nandanam
Pranamamyaham Sri Prathama Gurum
Pranamamyaham Karunakaram

## Meaning

We offer ourselves at the Lotus Feet of Lord Ganesha. Ganesha is the son of Mother Gowri (Parvathi) and Lord Shiva. We first worship Lord Ganesha, as He is the embodiment of compassion.
