## Lyrics

Krishnam Vande Nanda Kumaram
Radha Vallabha Navaneetha Choram
Ramam Vande Dasharatha Tanayam
Sita Vallabha Raghukula Tilakam
Muralidhara Sukumara Sharanam
Gopi Vallabha Nanda Kishoram

## Meaning

I bow to the little Krishna, the son of Nanda, consort of Radha, the one who stole butter, who plays the flute and had a tender body, was the beloved guardian of Gopikas. I bow to Rama, the son of Dasharatha, consort of Sita and the crown of the Raghu dynasty.
