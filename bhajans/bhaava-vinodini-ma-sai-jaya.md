## Lyrics

Bhaava Vinodini Ma Sai Jaya Jaya Ma Janani
Jaya Ma Jaya Ma Jaya Ma Janani
Prema Vilasini Ma Prana Nivasini Ma
Jaya Ma Jaya Ma Jaya Ma Janani (2)

## Meaning

Victory to our Divine Mother who rejoices in the devoted, heartfelt emotion of Her devotees. Glory to the Goddess who is the very basis of existence, the Cosmic Feminine Energy whose love is unconditional and ever-childlike.
