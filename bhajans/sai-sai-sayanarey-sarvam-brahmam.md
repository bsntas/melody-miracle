## Lyrics

Sai Sai Sayanarey Sarvam Brahmam Mayamanarey
Baba Baba Babanarey Bhakthito Bhajana Lo Palgonarey [ Sai Sai .. ]
Rama Rama Ramanarey Raakshasa Gunamulu Pommanarey
Krishna Krishna Krishnanarey Kashtamulanu Poguttunurey [ Sai Sai .. ]
Hari Hari Hari Hari Hari Anarey Atma Ramuni Madigonarey
Shiva Shiva Shiva Shiva Shiva Anarey Shiva Keshavulanu Samamanarey

## Meaning

Chant the name of Sai and realize that the universe is Brahman (the ultimate and unchanging reality); Chant the name of Baba and participate in group singing with devotion; Chant the name of Rama and drive away the demonic qualities within you. Chant the name of Krishna and it will eliminate your troubles; Chant the name of Hari and find the indweller in you; Chant the name of Shiva and realize that Shiva and Keshava (Krishna) are one and the same.
