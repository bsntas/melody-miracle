## Lyrics

Krupa Karo Mujhey Sai Murari
Dukhiyan Hoon Main Darsha Bhikhari [ Krupa Karo ... ]

## Meaning

Show mercy on me O Lord Sai Murari, I feel depressed without Your vision. Grant me Your Vision, O Lord, You are the one who lifted the Govardhana Mountain. You are the Star of my Eyes.
