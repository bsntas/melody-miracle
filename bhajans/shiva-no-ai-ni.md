## Lyrics

## Meaning

Let us surround ourselves with Lord Shiva's love to allow our lives to bloom. Let us praise Lord Shiva, pray to Lord Shiva and sing the glory of Lord Shiva. Please gift us with courage, please lead us to God. We revere You, Lord Sathya Sai Shiva.
