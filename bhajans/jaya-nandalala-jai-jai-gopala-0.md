## Lyrics

Jaya Nandalala Jai Jai Gopala
Raasa Vilola Radhey Gopala [ Jaya Nandalala ... ]
Murali Lola Munijana Pala
Gopi Lola Gopala Bala

## Meaning

Chant the name of Gopala, the son of Nanda, who performed the Celestial dance with Radha and other Gopis, delights in playing lilting tunes with the Flute and motivator of Sages and Saints.
