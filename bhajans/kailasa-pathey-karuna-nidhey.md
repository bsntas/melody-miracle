## Meaning

I surrender to the Lord of Kailasa (Lord Shiva) who is an Ocean of compassion. Glory to the one who holds the river Ganges on His matted locks and who is the consort of Mother Girija (Parvathi). Surrender to Lord Sai, who is verily Lord Shiva, the origin of all that is auspicious,
