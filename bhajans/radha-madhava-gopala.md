## Lyrics

Radha Madhava Gopala
Raasa Manohara Nandalala
Gopi Jana Mana Mohana Hey Krishna
Govardhana Dhara Nandalala
Parthi Vihara Nandalala (2)

## Meaning

Bow Down to the Lord of Lakshmi, who has taken birth as Krishna, who captivates our hearts and minds by His celestial dance with Radha (signifies the coming together of the individual and Cosmic soul). He is the son of Nanda and the one who held aloft the Govardhan mountain. Now strolls around delighting the devotees in Parthi.
