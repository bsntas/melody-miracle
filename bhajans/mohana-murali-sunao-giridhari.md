## Lyrics

Mohana Murali Sunao Giridhari
Aao Hrudaya Mey Prabhu Giridhari [ Mohana Murali ... ]
Hey Giridhari Parthi Vihari
Sri Madhusudhana Hrudaya Vihari
Meera Ke Prabhu Radha Pyaari

## Meaning

Play for us the enchanting music of the flute, O Lord Krishna. Welcome to my heart O Lord Giridhari, You are the one who resides in Parthi as Lord Sai. Be the resident of my heart, O Madhusudhana (slayer of demon, Madhu), Lord of Meera and beloved of Radha.
