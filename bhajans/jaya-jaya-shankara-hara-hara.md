## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Shankara Hara Hara Shankara
Jaya Jaya Shankara Mahadeva
Hara Om Hara Om Hara Om Hara Om
Hara Om Hara Om Sadashiva
Adi Kaarana Ananda Lola

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Shiva who is known by the names Shankara, Mahadeva and Sadashiva. Chant the mantra 'Hara Om'. Glory to the one who is the Primary Source of everything and who relishes to be in Bliss.
