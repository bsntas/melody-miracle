## Meaning

O Mother Saraswathi, the consort of the four-headed Lord Brahma, protect us. You are the Goddess of speech and sound. You are seated in the posture of lotus and is the one with extraordinary character. You are the embodiment of the highest knowledge, holding a book as its symbol and the Veena on the other hand. You are the Goddess of music and rhythm and Your arrow is speech (Geervani).
