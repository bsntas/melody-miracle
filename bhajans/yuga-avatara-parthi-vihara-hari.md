## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sathya Sai, the incarnation of Lord Vishnu in this Kali age who resides at Puttaparthi. He is Supreme Bliss, all merciful and the destroyer of sins. Shirdi Sai, Parthi Sai and Prema Sai are incarnations of Lord Shiva.
