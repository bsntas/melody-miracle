## Lyrics

Govinda Yadu Kula Madhava Ananda Paahi Keshava
Navarasa Yugadhara Muralidhara Nava Mohanaanga Hey Sridhara
Madhava Keshava
Sridhara Damodara
Gaana Lola Nija Bhaktha Paripala
Brindavana Sayeeshwara

## Meaning

Chant the names of Lord Krishna of Yadu dynasty - Govinda, Madhava and Keshava. O Lord, You exhibit the nine modes of melodies and postures while you play Your enchanting flute. Your other names are Sridhara and Damodara. You are none other than Lord Sai of Brindavan, who is the lover of music and the protector of His devotees.
