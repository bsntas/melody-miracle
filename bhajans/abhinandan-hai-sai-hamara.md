## Lyrics

Abhinandan Hai Sai Hamara
Shubh Janam Din Aaj Tumhara
Dhanya Dhanya Hai Sadguru Deva

Krupa Karo Ab Sab Bhakto Par

Ram Naam Dhan Dedo Baba
Sab Ko Shanthi Dedo Baba
Krupa Karo Ab Sab Bhakto Par

## Meaning

We celebrate this day, Your birthday, Lord Sai, our eternal teacher. Please shower Your blessings and wisdom, and teach us to meditate on You, and serve You. Bestow mercy and peace on us devotees, who are in Your Holy presence.
