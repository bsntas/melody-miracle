## Meaning

Glory to You, Oh Lord Sai Mahadeva To the One who is spotless in His virtues and who is the protector of all beings To the One who is beautifully adorned with the Vibhuti (Sacred Ash) We offer our prayers to you O Lord Sai Mahadeva.
