# Pavana Pavana Sai Naam

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/pavana-pavana-sai-naam)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Sai, Guru |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Pavana Pavana Sai Naam
Bhajo Nirantara Sai Ram
Bhakthi Bhaava Se Bhajana Sunao
Guru (Sai) Charanon Mey Dhyana Lagao

## Meaning

Always worship the pure and sacred name of Lord Sai. Chant the glory of the Lord with full devotion. Meditate on the Lotus feet of the divine master and guide, Lord Sai.
