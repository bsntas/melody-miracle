## Meaning

Let us chant the name of Lord Rama, the consort of Sita, of Shyam (Krishna), the savior of Radha. Chant the name of Shankar (Shiva), consort of Mother Gowri. The one Lord loves and fosters all faiths and religions. He is verily Sathya Sai, victory be to Him. Chant the name of Allah, Buddha, Guru Nanak, Zorashtra, Mahavir, and Jesus (Yesu). May victory be to all religions and faiths. May victory be to Lord Sathya Sai.
