## Lyrics

Srishti Sthithi Layakara Amrutotbhavakara
Guru Brahma Guru Vishnu Guru Devo Maheshwara [ Srishti Sthithi ... ]
Lingodhbhavakara Shiva Shiva Shiva Shankara
Shambho Mahadeva Sai Parameshwara

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Shiva who is responsible for creation, existence and destruction. He is the creator of Amrutha - the immortal nectar. He is the preceptor in the form of Trinity (Brahma, Vishnu and Maheshwara). Lord Sai who creates the of holy Shivalinga is none other than Lord Shiva.
