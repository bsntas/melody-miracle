## Lyrics

Jago Jago Giridhari Gopala
Jago Jago Hey Gokula Bala
Darshan Deejo Deena Dayala
Prabhu Paramatma Tribhuvana Pala
Sai Narayana Natavara Lala

## Meaning

O Lord Gopala (Krishna), grant us with the vision of Your Form. O Lord, awaken us from the slumber of Ignorance. You are the one who held the Govardhana mountain high, the one who is the Lord of the three worlds, the one who is an absolute pretender who moved about in Gokula and the one who is the refuge of the hapless.
