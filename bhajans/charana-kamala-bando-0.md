## Meaning

Hold on to the lotus feet of Lord Sai. Sai is the focus of our attraction. His feet are verily the ocean of blessing. He is the Divine Preceptor who grants peace. I am the servant of Sai, the essence of compassion. I pay homage to Lord Sai over and over again.
