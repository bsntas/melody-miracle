## Lyrics

Krishna Murari Muralidhari
Gopi Vallabha Giridhari
Hrudaya Vihari Leeladhari
Parthi Vihari Sai Murari

## Meaning

Lord Krishna who holds the flute in hand, held the mountain high, is dear to the Gopikas. Krishna who is ever playful, resides in the hearts of beings, is Sai Murari residing at Parthi.
