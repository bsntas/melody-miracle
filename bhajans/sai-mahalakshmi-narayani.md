## Lyrics

Sai Mahalakshmi Narayani

## Meaning

Salutations to the auspicious Mother Sai, who is the embodiment of bliss, who dispels darkness and fear, the one who is the very embodiment of auspiciousness and who bestows prosperity. Mother Sai is verily the embodiment of Shiva and Shakthi.
