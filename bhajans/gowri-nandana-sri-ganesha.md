## Lyrics

## Meaning

Oh Lord Ganesha (lord of demigods), You are the beloved son of Mother Gowri (Parvathi) and is verily Sai, the resident of Parthi. Victory unto You who is always invoked first, the one who holds the noose and hook, bestower of boons and who is worshipped from age to age. Protect us, Oh Lord.
