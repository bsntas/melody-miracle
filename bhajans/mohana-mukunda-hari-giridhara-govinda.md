## Meaning

Oh enchanting Lord Mukunda (Krishna) you are the one who lifted the mountain (Giridhara), you are Govinda Hari (Vishnu) you are none other than Narayana, Allah, Eshwar, Nanak, Jesus, Buddha, Mahavir, all are but emanations of the same Narayana, who is our beloved Sai Narayana.
