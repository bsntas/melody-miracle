## Meaning

Let Victory be on Your side Oh Lord Rama, the consort of Sita. Rama, the son of Raghu Dynasty, the redeemer of the curse on Ahalya, you are the crest jewel of the Raghu lineage. Chant the mantra Sita Ram, Sita Ram.
