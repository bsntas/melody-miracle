## Lyrics

Sharanagatha Sai Paapa Haro
Sadguru Eshwara Kalpataru [ Sharanagatha Sai ... ]
Deena Bandhava Tum Bhakton Ke
Abhaya Pradayaka Daasa Janon Ke (2)

## Meaning

Surrender to Lord Sai and be rid of your sins. He is the Supreme teacher and Lord who protects His devotees and fulfills their wishes.
