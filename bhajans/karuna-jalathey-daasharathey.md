## Lyrics

Karuna Jalathey Daasharathey
Kamaneeyanana Krupa Nidhey
Sri Raghu Nandana Sita Pathey
Shritha Jana Palana Suguna Nidhey
Daasharathey Daya Nidhey

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Rama, the son of Dasharatha and the scion of Raghu dynasty, has a pleasing form and is an ocean of compassion. He is the Lord of Mother Sita, the one who fulfills the desires of His devotees and is a paragon of virtues.
