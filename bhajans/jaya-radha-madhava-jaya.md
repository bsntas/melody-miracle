## Lyrics

Jaya Radha Madhava Jaya Kunja Vihari
Jaya Gopijana Vallabha Jaya Girivara Dhari [ Jaya Radha ... ]

Yamuna Teera Vihari
Hare Rama Hare Rama Rama Rama Hare Hare

## Meaning

Praise be to Lord Krishna and Radha, who reside in the gardens of Brindavana. Victory to the one who is adored by the Gopis. Victory to the one who granted a boon to the Govardhana mountain and held it on His little finger. Glory to the son of Mother Yashoda and who is endearing to the people of Brindavana. Victory to the one who resides on the banks of the river Yamuna. Chant the names of Rama and Krishna.
