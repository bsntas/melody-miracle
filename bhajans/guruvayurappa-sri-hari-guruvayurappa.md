## Meaning

Sing the names of the Lord of Guruvayur (Krishna): Govinda, Achyutha, Keshava, Madhava. Chant the name of Lord Rama and Hari. He is the one with lotus shaped eyes and dainty feet who enchants us with His flute. He is verily the supremely sacred and most compassionate Lord Sai of Puttaparthi.
