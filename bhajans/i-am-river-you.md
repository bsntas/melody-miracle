## Lyrics

I am the River, You are the Ocean, Nagare Iku Anata no Moto he
I am the Bird, You are the Sky, Anata no Ai wa Subete wo Tsutsumu.
I am the Leaf, You are the Tree, Ai no Naka de Ikite Yukou

## Meaning

I am the river, You are the ocean. I flow to You. I am the bird, You are the sky. Your love pervades everything. I am the leaf, You are the tree. I live within Your love.
