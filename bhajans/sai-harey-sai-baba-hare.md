Sai Harey Parthi Baba Harey Sarva Dharma Priya Baba Harey

## Meaning

Lord Sai, the destroyer of sins who upholds all faiths and religions, is worshipped as Allah, Guru Nanak, Yeshu, Rama, Krishna, Zohrashtra and Mahavir. He resides in Puttaparthi.
