## Lyrics

Deenodharana Rama Harey
Deena Dayala Krishna Harey
Raghukula Tilaka Rama Harey
Yadu Kula Nandana Krishna Harey

## Meaning

Oh Rama, you are the saviour of the meek and week. You are also Krishna who is compassionate to the meek and week. You are the star of the Raghu clan and as Krishna, the darling of Nanda and the star of the Yadu clan.
