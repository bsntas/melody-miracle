## Lyrics

Jaya Jagadeesha Harey Murarey
Jaya Jagannatha Janardhana [ Jaya Jagadeesha ... ]
Jaya Vanamali Jaya Giridhari
Jaya Jaya Sri Hari Sai Murari

## Meaning

Victory to the Lord of the Universe, Lord Krishna, who is the inner motivator of all, who wears a garland of jungle flowers and who annihilated demon Mura.
