## Meaning

O Lord Shiva, the God of Gods, the God of mountains who is worshipped with the petals of flowers and leaves, You are the protector of the Gods. We adore You, O Lord, the effulgent one who holds the Trident, adorned with the crescent moon on the head and a garland of skulls of evil-doers.
