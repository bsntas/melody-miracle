# Jagadodharini Mata Narayani Durga

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Devi |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Jagadodharini Mata Narayani Durga
Durgati Nashini Dukha Vinashini Durga
Jaya Mahishasura Durga
Abhaya Pradayini Shanthi Pradayini Durga
Jaya Durga Devi Durga Mata Durga

## Meaning

Salutations to Mother Durga, remover of misfortunes and sorrow, destroyer of the demon Mahishasura. She protects and bestows peace.
