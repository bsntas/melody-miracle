## Lyrics

Sathya Sai Rama Shiva Shakthi Roopa
Sathya Dharma Shanthi Prema Poorna Teja
Eshwaramba Nandana Sathya Narayana
Yuga Avatara Devadi Deva
Jaya Paramesha Parthi Pureesha
Hrudayeshwara Sayeeshwara

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai residing in Parthi. O Lord Sai, You are the personification of Lord Shiva and Goddess Shakthi. You are the one endowed with the luster of Truth, Righteousness, Peace and Love. You are the son of Eshwaramba and is verily Lord Sathya Narayana. You are the incarnation of the Kali Age, O Lord of Lords. Victory to Thee, O Lord Sai, the indweller of hearts.
