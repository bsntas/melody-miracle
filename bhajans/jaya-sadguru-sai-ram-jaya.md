## Lyrics

Jaya Sadguru Sai Ram Jaya Sai Mahadeva

## Meaning

Victory to the Sacred preceptor Sai Ram and Sai Mahadeva. Victory to Ram who has incarnated as the Lord of Parthi and Shyam who had incarnated as the Lord of Shirdi. Glory to the Sacred Preceptor Sai Ram and Sai Mahadeva who wears a red robe.
