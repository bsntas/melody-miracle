## Meaning

O Lord, You are verily Allah, Maulah, Jehovah, Mahavira, Ahura Mazda, Buddha, Guru Nanak, Krishna, and Narayana. You came whenever I called, and whatever name I call, You come to me! Your glory is sung in every heart.
