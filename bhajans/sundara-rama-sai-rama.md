## Lyrics

Sundara Rama Sai Rama
Sugunatheetha Megha Shyama
Srishti Sthithi Laya Karana Rama
Munijana Poojita Taraka Naama
Sath Chith Ananda Swaroopa Naam (2)
Sai Ram Sai Ram (4)

## Meaning

Oh Beautiful Rama, your virtues are of highest order, your color is that of dark clouds, you are responsible for Creation and existence, you are adored by sages, you are the liberator from the cycle of birth and death and you are always in bliss.
