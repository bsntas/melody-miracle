## Meaning

Glory to Lord Shiva, also hailed as Rudra Bhairava, who destroys one's evil tendencies and ignorance. Glory to Lord Shankara, the Consort of Mother Bhavani (Parvathi) who wards off the dangers of His devotees. Salutations to the Lord who is the very source and bestower of auspiciousness.
