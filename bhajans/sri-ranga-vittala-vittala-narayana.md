## Lyrics

Sri Ranga Vittala Vittala Narayana
Panduranga Vittala Vittala Narayana
Rakumayi Vittala Vittala Narayana
Dwarakamayi Vittala Vittala Narayana
Sri Sai Vittala Vittala Narayana

## Meaning

Pray to Lord Vittala who is none other than Lord Narayana. He is Lord Panduranga and the beloved of Rakumayi. He is also Lord Sai, the resident of Dwarakamayi in Shirdi.
