## Meaning

Salutations to You, O enchanting Lord Sai, with the flute in Your hands, You are none other than Lord Krishna also called Mukunda, Madhusudhana, the lotus-eyed one with the decorated hair. O extremely tender and gentle one, the destroyer of fear, the ruler of the Universe and the blessing of the Kaliyuga (age of Kali), our salutations to You.
