## Lyrics

Janani Janani Ma Shankari Durgati Nashini Ma
Mata Uma Sai Ma
Janani Janani Ma
Dashabhujadharini Bhairavi Ma

Jaya Bhuvaneshwari Jaya Partheeshwari
Jagadodharini Ma

## Meaning

Extol the Glories of Mother Uma, the origin of Auspiciousness and destroyer of misfortune. She is known as Bhairavi, when she bears ten Hands, negates the effects of sin and the Mother of Humanity, who dwells in Parthi.
