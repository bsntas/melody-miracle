## Lyrics

Giridhari Shyama Mukunda Murari
Madhura Madhura Hey Muralidhari [ Giridhari Shyama ... ]
Mathuradhipathey Madhava Shourie
Sri Madhusudhana Mayura Mukutadhara
Madana Mohana Krishna Kunja Vihari
Giridhari Shyam Giridhari Shyam

## Meaning

O Lord Giridhari (Lord Krishna who lifted the mountain) with dark blue complexion, the one who redeems us from miseries and gives liberation, You are the sweet enchanting flute player, the Lord of Mathura and valorous Madhava, the destroyer of demon Madhu and the one who is adorned with crown of peacock feather, the one who strolls the grooves and garden of Brindavan captivating everyone.
