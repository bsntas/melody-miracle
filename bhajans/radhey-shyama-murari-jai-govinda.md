## Meaning

Victory to Lord Shyam, to Govinda and Gopal, (nanmes of Lord Krishna), the one who slayed demon Mura, the one who moved about the forests playing on His enchanting Flute, the one who is adored by the residents and all the beings of Gokula. Victory to the one who performed the celestial dance, the one who captivates the body and mind of His devotees Victory to that Govinda Gopal.
