## Lyrics

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Sathya Sai whose abode is Prashanthi Nilayam at Puttaparthi. Glory to the one who is the eternal charioteer of our hearts and who is the very embodiment of Truth. O Lord, dance blissfully in the Vrindavana of the devotee's heart while the supremely sweet melody of Your name is being sung.
