## Lyrics

Sundara Sai Shankara Potri
Nangu Vedattin Nadane Potri [ Sundara Sai ... ]
Uma Mahesha Hara Hara Potri

Adi Andam Illa Ishane Potri
Ambalattadum Iraivane Potri

## Meaning

Glory be unto Lord Shankara (Shiva), the Lord of the four Vedas, the Lord of Uma, the only existing reality, without a beginning or end. Lord Sai embodies Shiva who dances in the temple of our hearts.
