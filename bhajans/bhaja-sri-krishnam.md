## Lyrics

## Meaning

Chant the name of Sai Krishna, the supreme redeemer, the son of Devaki and Vasudeva. He danced on the head of Kalinga serpent at Yamuna river playing the enchanting flute. He is the star of Yadu dynasty who lifted the Govardhana mountain and who is adorned with the garland of Tulasi.
