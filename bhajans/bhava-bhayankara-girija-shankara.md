## Lyrics

Bhava Bhayankara Girija Shankara
Nacho Nacho Prabho Hey Nataraj
Tandaveshwara Hey Nataraj
Nacho Hey Nataraj Nacho Nacho Hey Nataraj (3)

## Meaning

Dance, dance Nataraja also Shankara the consort of Girija (Parvathi) who removes fear and frees one from cycle of birth and death. Also hey, Nataraja you are cosmic dancer during creation.
