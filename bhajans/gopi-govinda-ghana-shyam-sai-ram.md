## Meaning

The Lord has many forms like Govinda, to the Gopi ladies of Brindavan who is also Shyam, the one who is dark complexioned God, Sri Sai Ram to the devotees of Nanda's son (Lord Krishna). He is the Sai Baba who resides in Shirdi. Sai is the friend to all those in trouble Sai is all merciful, He is full of compassion.
