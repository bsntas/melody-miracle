## Lyrics

Om Bhur Bhuvah Suvaha
Tat Savitur Varenyam
Bhargo Devasya Dheemahi
Dhiyo Yonah Prachodayaat

## Meaning

That which Permeates the three worlds – Body, Mind and Soul. We Meditate on this Most Venerable Self Effulgence. And Pray for the Enlightenment of our Intellect.
