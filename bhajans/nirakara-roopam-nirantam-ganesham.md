## Lyrics

Nirakara Roopam Nirantam Ganesham
Sada Sath Swaroopam Smarami Ganesham
Parabrahma Roopam Paramatma Roopam
Bhajami Mahesham Namami Suresham

## Meaning

We offer prayers to the infinite, formless Lord Ganesha. We contemplate on Him as He represents the ultimate Truth. He is the Supreme Brahman and the Supreme Soul. We pray to the master of celestial entities and sing His glory at all times.
