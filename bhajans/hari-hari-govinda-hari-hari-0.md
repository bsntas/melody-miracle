## Lyrics

Hari Hari Govinda Hari Hari Om
Parthi Pureesha Panduranga
Pandari Natha Hari Hari
Parama Dayala Pannaga Sai
Paramananda Hari Hari

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Hari, Govinda (names of Lord Vishnu) and to Lord Sai, the most compassionate Lord of Parthi. Lord Sai is none other than Lord Panduranga of Pandharpur, the supremely blissful one.
