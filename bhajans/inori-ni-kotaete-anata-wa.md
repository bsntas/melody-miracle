## Lyrics

Inori ni Kotaete Anata wa Korareta
Subete no Yami wo Hikari de Terashi
Michibiku tame ni
Rama, Krishna, Shiva, Iesu, Buddha
Ai no Kami
Shirdi Sathya Sai Prema Baba
Warera no Michi

## Meaning

Lord, You have answered my prayers and come to me. You illuminate all darkness and guide us. God of Love, You are Rama, Krishna, Shiva, Jesus, Buddha, Shirdi Sai, Sathya Sai and Prema Sai Baba.
