## Lyrics

Krishna Krishna Madhava Madhusudhana
Yadava Yadu Kula Yadu Nandana
Govardhanodhara Gopi Ramana
Bhaktha Janodhara Bhava Bhaya Harana

## Meaning

Sing the glories of Lord Krishna (Madhava), the slayer of demon Madhu and who is of Yadava lineage. Glory to Lord Krishna who held aloft the Govardhana Hill with His little finger in order to save Gopis and Gopas. He is the redeemer of His devotees and destroys the fear of rebirth.
