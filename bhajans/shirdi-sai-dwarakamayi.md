## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Sai |

## Lyrics

Shirdi Sai Dwarakamayi
Tum Ho Merey Bhagawan
Tum Ho Sai Bhagawan
Sab Bhakto Ke Tum Ho Daata
Sab Ko Hai Tum Prana Daata
Ananda Daata Hey Sukha Daata [ Tum Ho ... Sai Bhagawan ]

## Meaning

Lord Sai of Shirdi who resides in Dwarakamayi is my Lord and the Lord of all devotees. He bestows happiness and comfort to all and is their very life-breath.
