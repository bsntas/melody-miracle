| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

He Natavaralala Nandalala
Radha Ramana Hari Gopala [ He Natavaralala ... ]
Meera Ke Prabhu Giridhara Bala
Goparipala Gopala Bala

## Meaning

Lord Krishna, the beloved of Radha and the Lord of Meera is the Son of Nanda. He is the divine dancer who protects the cows (a cowheard)
