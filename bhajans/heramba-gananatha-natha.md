## Lyrics

Heramba Gananatha Natha
Hey Gananatha Anatha Natha
Bhuvanaadhara Pranava Swaroopa (3)
Siddhi Vinayaka Hey Gananatha (Vara)

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Ganesha, the leader of Ganaas, the Lord of the forsaken ones who is the embodiment of the vital force and basis of the Universe. He fulfills ones wishes.
