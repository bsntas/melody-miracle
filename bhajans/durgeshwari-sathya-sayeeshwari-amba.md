## Lyrics

Durgeshwari Sathya Sayeeshwari (Amba)
Paramesha Jagadeesha Partheeshwari [ Durgeshwari ... ]
Karunya Lavanya Kamaleshwari
Sathya Sai Jaganmata Parameshwari

## Meaning

O Universal Mother Durga, You are none other than Mother Sathya Sai, the Supreme Lord who resides at Parthi. You are also adored as Kamaleshwari, with elegant beauty and compassion.
