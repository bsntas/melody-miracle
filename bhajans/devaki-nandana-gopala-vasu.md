## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Gopala, the beloved son of Mother Devaki Chant the name of Lord Gopala, who is also known as Shyama, the one who is beautiful because of the color black and the conferrer of Liberation. Chant the name of the one who is as sweet as honey and who delights in participating in folk dances with Radha. Chant the name of the Lord, who is a supreme dancer.
