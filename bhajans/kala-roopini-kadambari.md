## Lyrics

Kala Roopini Kadambari
Naada Roopini Narayani
Sathya Dharma Roopini Sayeeshwari
Mangala Roopini Maheshwari (Sarva)
Kadambari Narayani (Amba)
Sayeeshwari Maheshwari (Sathya)

## Meaning

Kadambari is the personification of Fine arts, Narayani is the personification of Sound, Sayeeshwari is the embodiment of Truth and righteousness, Maheshwari is the embodiment of Auspiciousness.
