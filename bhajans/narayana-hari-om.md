## Lyrics

Narayana Hari Om
Sri Jagannatha Jyothi Swaroopa [ Narayana Hari ... ]
Dharani Dhara Hey Damodara

## Meaning

O Lord Narayana, You are the ultimate destination for all beings. O Lord Vishnu, You are the Lord of the Universe. You are the divine light who holds the earth. I bow down to You, O Lord of Bliss. You are Lord Krishna, the son of Nanda.
