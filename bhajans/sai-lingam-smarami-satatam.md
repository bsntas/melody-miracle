# Sai Lingam Smarami Satatam

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/sai-lingam-smarami-satatam)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Shiva |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Sai Lingam Smarami Satatam
Nama Roopa Prapanchatitam
Agama Sara Atma Lingam
Partisha Svayam Prakasha Lingam

## Meaning

Let me always meditate on Sai Lingam. His name and form transcend the phenomenal Universe. The self-effulgent Lingam is the essence of creation and life.
