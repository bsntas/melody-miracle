## Meaning

Glory to Mother Saraswathi, the bestower of Divine knowledge, who is none other than our Mother Sai. The counterpart of Goddesses Parvathi and Durga, Mother Saraswathi's form is the supreme manifestation of the primordial sound Om. She is bright and effulgent as lightning. Mother Sai is verily the Supreme Spirit.
