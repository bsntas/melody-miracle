## Lyrics

Sri Rama Rama Rama Ram
Japte Manabhi Ram
Sitapati Ramachandra
Dasharatha Priya Nandana
Hanumath Sri Charana Vandana
Pooran Hota Man Ki Kamna [ Japte Manabhi ... ]

## Meaning

Chanting in our minds the name of Lord Rama, the consort of Sita and Dasaratha's favorite son, and at whose Feet Hanuman served, will fulfill our desires and wishes.
