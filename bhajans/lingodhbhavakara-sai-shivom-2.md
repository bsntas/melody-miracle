## Meaning

Sing the Glory of Lord Sai who is none other than Lord Shiva supporting the Ganges. Lord Sai is the source of the Lingam (creative power). He is the Lord of the Universe, the personification of Love and the form of the Primeval Sound Om. Glory to the Supremely Sacred Lord Sai.
