## Lyrics

Jai Durge Durgati Pariharini
Jai Jai Durge Jaya Bhava Bhaya Harini
Jaya Durge Janani Jaya Sai Janani
Sathya Dharma Shanthi Prema Roopini
Jai Jai Janani Sai Janani

## Meaning

Hail Mother Durga for She absolves the misfortunes of Her devotees, releases one from the birth and death cycle. Victory be to Mother Sai who is the very manifestation of the Cardinal Values of Truth, Righteousness, Peace and Love.
