## Lyrics

Antes del Om solo eras Tu.
Tu lo quisiste y se hizo la Luz.
Por tu Voluntad aparecieron
La Tierra, el Hombre, el Mar y el Cielo.
Sin tu Amor yo no podria sobrevivir ni un solo dia.
Tu mirada me inspira Amor
tus palabras me dan valor.
Senor ayudanos a comprender q debemos cumplir nuestro Deber.

## Meaning

O Lord, only You existed before the Primodial Sound "Om". With Your Divine Will You created the Earth, Man, the Sea and the Heaven. Without Your Love, my Lord, I cannot survive even a single day. Your divine sight inspires divine Love in me. Your divine Words give me courage. O Lord, help us understand that we must accomplish our higher Duty (Dharma).
