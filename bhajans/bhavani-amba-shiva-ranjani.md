## Lyrics

Bhavani Amba Shiva Ranjani
Sai Maheshwari Vighna Vinashini [ Bhavani Amba ... ]
Lalita Madhavi Jai Sri Ranjani
Mangala Kaarini Lakshmi Narayani
Naada Swaroopini Veena Vadini
Devi Saraswathi Vidya Dayini

## Meaning

Glory to Mother Sai who is verily Mother Parvathi, the delight of Lord Shiva and the Mother of creation who removes all obstacles, Mother Lakshmi, the one who grants good fortune and Mother Saraswathi, the very embodiment of sound (music), the one who plays the Veena and the one who bestows knowledge.
