## Lyrics

Sri Purandara Sai Vittala
Sri Hari Madhava Govinda Vittala [ Sri Purandara ... ]
Pandari Natha Panduranga Vittala
Bhaktha Samrakshaka Sri Krishna Vittala
Parthi Purandara Sai Ranga Vittala

## Meaning

Chant the name of Vittala of Pandari (Lord Krishna). He is Lord Sai, the savior of His devotees and the resident of Puttaparthi.
