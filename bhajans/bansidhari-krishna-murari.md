## Lyrics

Bansidhari Krishna Murari
Govardhana Giridhari Shourie
Patithodhari Paapa Viduri
Leela Nataka Sutradhari
Parthi Vihari Sai Murari

## Meaning

Oh Krishna Murari, You are so enchanting with Your flute. You saved the devotees by lifting the entire Govardhana Mountain. You are the redeemer of the downtrodden and remover of their sins. You are the director of Your divine play and who resides in Parthi.
