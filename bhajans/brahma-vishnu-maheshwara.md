## Lyrics

Brahma Vishnu Maheshwara
Shirdi Sai Shankara
Parthi Baba Shankara
Namah Shivaya Namah Shivaya
Namah Shivaya Namo Namah Shivaya

Shirdi Sai Shankara

## Meaning

Pay homage to Brahma, Vishnu, Lord of Lords Shiva, Sai of Shirdi, and Baba of Parthi.
