## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Narayana who has an eternally blissful form. His form gives us supreme contentment and helps us get to the state of Advaita (non-duality - where the Lord and the devotee are one). Lord Sathya Sai is the very embodiment of supreme consciousness and bliss.
