## Lyrics

In my heart a blazing sun, in my mind, an endless sky,
Lord Sai has freed me, I am one.
Your wondrous grace, a gift divine, knows no why, no place or time.
Lord Sai has freed me, I am one.
I am all joy, all peace, all light, before the beginning, past the end,
Lord Sai has freed me, I am one.
