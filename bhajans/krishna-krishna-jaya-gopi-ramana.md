## Lyrics

Krishna Krishna Jaya Gopi Ramana
Bhaktha Vatsala Jeevana
Radha Madhava Mohana (3)

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Krishna, the Lord of the Gopis, the very life of His devotees and the one who is adored by Radha.
