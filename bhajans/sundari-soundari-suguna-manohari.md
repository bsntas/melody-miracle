## Lyrics

Sundari Soundari Suguna Manohari
Shankari Sambhavi Sayeeshwari (2)
Nithya Niranjani Niramayi
Ranjani Rakshaki Sathya Sayeeshwari

## Meaning

The Divine Mother is beauty personified, highly virtuous, consort of Shankara (Shiva), ever blemishless, the one who protects Her devotees and is none other than Mother Sathya Sai.
