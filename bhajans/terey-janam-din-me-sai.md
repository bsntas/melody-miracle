## Meaning

O Lord Sai, we bow to You a million times on Your Birthday. We, brothers and sisters, will congregate in Prashanthi, Your birth place, to worship You. Blessed Mother Eshwaramba gave birth to my Sai on November 23rd, so that I may sing His praises.
