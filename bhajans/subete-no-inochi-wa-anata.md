## Lyrics

Subete no Inochi wa Anata Kara Umareta
Subete wa Kami Desu, Subete wa Ai Desu
Anata no Kotoba wa Nani yori mo Amaku

## Meaning

O Lord Sathya Sai, everything in this world was born for You. Everything is God, Everything is Love. Any word You speak is sweet. You give me the strength to return to You. Sathya Sai Baba, Your love has guided us.
