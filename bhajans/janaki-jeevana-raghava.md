## Lyrics

Janaki Jeevana Raghava
Radhika Jeevana Keshava

Kaurava Nashana Keshava O Leela Madhava Keshava

## Meaning

Lord Raghava is the life force of Mother Janaki (Sita). He vanquished Ravana who abducted Mother Sita during the Treta Era. Lord Keshava is the life force of Radha. He rooted out the Kaurava race and installed Righteousness during the Dwapara Era.
