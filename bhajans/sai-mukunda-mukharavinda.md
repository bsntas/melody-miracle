## Meaning

Chant the sweet name of Lord Sai, whose countenance is described as soft as that of a Lotus, the one whose lips are painted bright red from chewing the betel leaves, the one with the ever smiling visage, the one whose countenance is filled with Bliss Divine, the one whose countenance embodies Truth, Auspiciousness and Bliss.
