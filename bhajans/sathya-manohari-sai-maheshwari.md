## Lyrics

Sathya Manohari Sai Maheshwari
Brindavana Sanchari (Amba)
Characharamayi Sarva Shubhakari
Yuga Yuga Avatari (Amba)

## Meaning

Glory to Mother Sai who is ever enchanting and the embodiment of Truth and Wisdom, the one who is eternal and confers Her grace and auspiciousness upon one and all. She is the one who is present across all times and ages.
