## Meaning

Sing the glorious Name of Lord Narayana, also known as Hari, who is verily Lord Krishna, the one who lifted the Govardhana mountain and the resident of Brindavan. He is the Lord of the cowherds and the son of Gokul who now lives in Puttaparthi as our Lord Sathya Sai. Victory to the Lord Sai Krishna.
