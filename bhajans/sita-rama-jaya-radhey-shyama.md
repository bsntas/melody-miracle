## Lyrics

Sita Rama Jaya Radhey Shyama Jaya
Mangala Charana Jaga Vandana (Shubha)
Sita Pathey Ramachandra
Raghava Raghu Nandana

## Meaning

Salutations to You, Lord Rama from Raghu dynasty and the consort of Mother Sita. You are the same as the dark-complexioned Krishna. You bestow auspiciousness and the whole world prostrates at Your feet.
