## Lyrics

Raghava Rama Raghuveera
Sridhara Madhava Govinda [ Raghava ... ]
Keshava Madhava Madhusudhana
Achyutha Anantha Narayana
Gopiya Lola Gopala

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Rama, also known as Raghava, who is the champion of the Raghu lineage. Chant the various names of Krishna, Sridhara (the one who bears Mother Lakshmi in His heart), Madhava, Govinda, Keshava (the one with long hair), Madhusudhana (the one who vanquished the demon Madhu), Achyutha (un-decaying), Anantha (infinite), Narayana (indweller) and Gopala (sustainer), who is dear to the Gopis.
