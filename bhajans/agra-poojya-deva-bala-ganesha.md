## Lyrics

Agra Poojya Deva Bala Ganesha
Parvathi Priya Sutha Vighna Vinasha
Jaya Jaya Vishwa Vidhayaka Jaya Hey
Mangala Dayiney Jaya Gananatha Hey
Jaya Gurudev Jaya Parthi Pureesha [ Parvathi Priya Sutha ... ]

## Meaning

Foremost prayers to the young Lord Ganesha, the beloved son of Mother Parvathi and the one removes all the obstacles. Victory to the Lord of the Ganas who sustains the Universe and bestows auspiciousness. Victory to the Divine Preceptor Sai, the Lord of Parthi who is verily Lord Ganesha.
