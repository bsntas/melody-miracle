## Lyrics

Parthi Pureesha Panduranga

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi, who is none other than Panduranga, the Lord of Pandarpur. He is Lord Vishnu (Narayana), who incarnated as Krishna, the stealer of the hearts and the divine child of Nanda.
