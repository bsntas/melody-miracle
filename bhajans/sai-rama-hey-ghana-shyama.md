## Lyrics

Sai Rama Hey Ghana Shyama
Dasharatha Nandana Sri Raghu Rama [ Sai Rama ... ]
Parthi Pureeshwara Pavana Naama
Pavana Naama Pavana Naama [ Parthi Pureeshwara ... ]
Sharanam Sharanam Hey Sai Rama

## Meaning

Salutations to You O Lord Rama, the son of King Dasharatha and the scion of the Raghu dynasty. Glory to You O Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi whose name is the most sacred one. We surrender ourselves to You O Sai Rama.
