## Lyrics

Sri Raghu Nandana Bhava Bhaya Bhanjana
Sita Pathey Ramachandra (Hey)
Parama Pavitra Varija Netra
Jagadodhara Nirakara [ Sita Pathey ... ]
Ahalyodhara Sabari Raghuraya
Raghukula Bhooshana Rajeeva Lochana [ Sita Pathey ... ]

## Meaning

O Lord Rama, You are the ornament of Raghu Dynasty, You are the destroyer of fear of this materialistic world. O Lord of Mother Sita, Your eyes are like Lotus. O the Formless one, the upholder of the Universe, the one who liberated Ahalya from a curse, our salutations to You.
