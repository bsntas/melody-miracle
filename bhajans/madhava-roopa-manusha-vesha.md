## Lyrics

Madhava Roopa Manusha Vesha
Sai Maharaj Sathya Sai Maharaj [ Madhava Roopa ... ]

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Krishna (Madhava) who has taken a Human Form as Lord Sathya Sai. He is verily Lord Dattatreya, the embodiment of Dvinity and the Lord of Lords.
