## Lyrics

Tu Hai Anatha Bandhu
Karuna Sindho Sri Sai Bhagawan [ Tu Hai ... ]

Main Kya Janoon Teri Leela (Mahima)
Mamata Maya Mey Sab Hai Andhera (2)

## Meaning

Lord Sai is the friend of the forlorn and HE is full of compassion. How can we ever know your grandeur, Oh Sai ? You are the cause of happiness and remover of sorrow when we are stooped in the darkness of ignorance ?
