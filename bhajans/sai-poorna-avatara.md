## Lyrics

Sai (Poorna) Avatara Parthi Pureesha
Jagadodhara Namo
Eshwaramba Nandana Premavatara
Tum Ho Merey Pran (Baba)
Parthipuri (Shirdipuri) Bhagawan
Daya (Krupa) Karo Bhagawan [ Jagadodhara ... ]

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Sai, Lord of Parthi who has incarnated with all the divine attributes. Salutations to the redeemer of the world, an incarnation of love who is the beloved son of Eshwaramba. O Lord who resides at Shirdi and Puttaparthi, You are my very life. Shower Thy mercy on us, O Lord.
