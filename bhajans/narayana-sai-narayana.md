## Meaning

Oh Narayana! The abode of all beings! Oh Lord Sai! You are the lion-faced Lord Narahari! Prostrations to you. The form of peace oh Hari! my Obeisance! Oh Hrishikesha (Lord of the senses, Vishnu)! You are the inhabitant of the devotee's heart! Oh the refuge of the poor! Oh Lord Hari! Salutations to you. Oh Lord You are the author of Vedas. Oh Lord Sai! You are the incarnation of Lord Narayana!
