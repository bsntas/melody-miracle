Punya Laganey Hamey Hari Charan Poojey [ Mohana Murali ... ]

## Meaning

The melodious flute of Lord Gopala (Krishna) is playing while He is swinging in His decorated cradle. All the devotees are playing drums and other musical instruments while decorating Lord Krishna with sandal wood and fragrances. Let us all go to see that beautiful form of our Lord Giridhar who is none other than our Lord Sai. He came to rescue us, the devotees. Let us worship and surrender at His Lotus Feet.
