## Lyrics

Govardhana Giridhari Murari
Hey Vraja Bala Hey Vanamali
Gokula Brindavana Sanchari
Meera Ke Prabhu Hey Giridhari
Parthi Vihari Sai Murari

## Meaning

Oh Govardhana Giridhari Murari (the little mischievous Krishna who lifted the mountain to save the people of Gokula). You are the one who is from Mathura and you are the one who is wearing a garland made of jungle flowers. You are the one from Gokula and wanderer of Brindavan forest. You are the Lord of Meera also presently you are Sai rambling in Parthi.
