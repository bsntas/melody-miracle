## Lyrics

Daasharathey Rama Kalyana Guna Dhama
Rama Sri Rama Rameshwara Rama [ Daasharathey Rama ... ]
Bhadrachala Rama Patitha Pavana Rama
Ahalyodharaka Rama Puttaparthi Sai Rama

## Meaning

O Lord Rama, son of Dasharatha, You are the abode of all virtues and auspiciousness, saviour and up-lifter of afflicted. You freed Ahalya from her curse. You reside in Rameshwaram, Bhadrachalam and in Puttaparthi as Lord Sai Rama.
