## Lyrics

Sathya Dharmamu Shanthi Premalato
Ni Nithya Jeevana Yatra Saginchu (Jeeva)
Manasu Madakada Gunamule Yaddul
Vivekamanu Chala Kolanun Tisi
Vishalamagu Mee Hrudayamu Dunnudo [ Sathya Dharmamu ... ]
Hrudaya Bhumini Sagu Cheyyandi

## Meaning

Truth, Righteousness, Peace, and Love are the guiding tools of the journey of life. With mind as the tiller, character as the pulling bulls and intellect as the reins, we must cultivate broadmindedness and love for everybody.
