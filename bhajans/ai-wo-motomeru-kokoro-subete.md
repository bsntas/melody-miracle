## Lyrics

Ai wo Motomeru Kokoro, Subete Aru ga mamani
Sai no Miashi ni Sasage, Kami no Goishi no mamani

Toutoki Mono wa Anata he no Inori

## Meaning

This heart seeks the love that is present wherever you go. Devote yourself to the divine feet of Lord Sai. God's divine will is present wherever you go. A heart that knows love is a beautiful thing. We present our noble actions in prayer to reach You. Victory to Lord Rama and Lord Sai, we journey towards You, to the origin of the light of love.
