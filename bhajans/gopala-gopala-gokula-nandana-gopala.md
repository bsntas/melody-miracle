## Lyrics

Gopala Gopala Gokula Nandana Gopala (3)
Nanda Kishora Gopala

Yadu Kula Tilaka Gopala

## Meaning

Chant the name of Gopala, who is the darling of Gokul and Nanda. The One who stole butter, son of Yashoda and star of Yadu clan.
