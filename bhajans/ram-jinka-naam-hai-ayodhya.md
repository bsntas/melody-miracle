## Lyrics

Ram Jinka Naam Hai Ayodhya Jinka Dham Hai
Hamarey Raghunath Ji Ko Hamarey Lakh Pranam Hai
Krishna Jinka Naam Hai Gokul Jinka Dhaam Hai
Hamarey Yadunath Ji Ko Hamarey Lakh Pranam Hai
Sai Jinka Naam Hai Parthi Jinka Dhaam Hai
Hamarey Pran Nath Ko Hamarey Lakh Pranam Hai

## Meaning

We offer a lakh (hundred thousand) obeisance to our Lord of Raghu lineage, whose Name is Ram and Ayodhya His abode. We offer a Lakh obeisance to Lord of Yadu lineage, whose Name is Krishna and Gokul being His abode. We offer a Lakh obeisance to the Lord of our Life, whose Name is Sai and Parthi His abode.
