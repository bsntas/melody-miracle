## Lyrics

Yashoda Bala Nanda Gopala
Gokula Bala Gopala Bala [ Yashoda Bala ... ]
Yaduvara Giridhara Krishna Gopala
Jaya Jaya Meera Prabhu Nandalala

## Meaning

Oh son of Nanda born of Yadu clan who held aloft the hillock Govardhana, who moves about in Gokula, Victory to you Oh Lord of devotee Meera.
