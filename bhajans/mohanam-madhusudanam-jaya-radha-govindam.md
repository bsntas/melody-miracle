## Meaning

Victory to the the Lord Krishna, the beautiful cowherd who is the beloved of Radha and the slayer of the demons Madhu and Keshi. He is known by various names, including Narayana, Madhava, and Mukunda. The son of Nanda, Lord Krishna, captivates the mind with His beautiful flute. He is imperishable as He is the Supreme Self, none other than our beautiful Lord Sathya Sai.
