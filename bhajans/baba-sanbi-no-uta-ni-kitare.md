## Lyrics

Baba Sanbi no Uta ni Kitare

## Meaning

Beloved Baba, come to us as we sing to You the song of Your glory; O Lord who resides within everything, come to us; You have reincarnated to help us; You shine the divine light within us; You glow within our hearts, Lord Sai.
