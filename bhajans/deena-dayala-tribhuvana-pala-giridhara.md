## Lyrics

Deena Dayala Tribhuvana Pala Giridhara Bala Nandalala
Murali Manohara Maya Vilola
Natavara Sundara Shyama Gopala
Radha Madhava Sai Gopala

## Meaning

O Giridhara (Krishna), darling son of Nanda, You are the protector of three worlds, compassionate towards weak and meek. You are captivating and enchanting with the flute in Your hands. You play tricks on devotees with Your illusion (Maya). You danced on the serpent Kalinga. You are the Lord of Radha. You are none other than Lord Sai.
