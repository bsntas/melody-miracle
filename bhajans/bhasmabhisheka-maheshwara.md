## Lyrics

Bhasmabhisheka Maheshwara
Bhasma Vibhushitha Sundara
Hara Om Hara Om Haalaahala Dhara
Hara Hara Hara Hara Hey Shiva Shankara
Natavara Shashi Dhara Gowri Manohara
Chandrashekara Jatadhara

## Meaning

Worship the Lord Maheshwara who is bathed and smeared with sacred ashes all over the body looking beautiful. Oh Shiva Shankara you drank Haalaa-hala (poison) to save troubled world. You are the consort of Gowri who danced decorating with moon on head with huge flock of hair.
