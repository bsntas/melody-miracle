## Lyrics

Yuga Avatara Palanhaara
Kaivalya Dayaka Ram O Sai [ Yuga Avatara ... ]

## Meaning

Oh Baba! You have incarnated in this age, you are the one who grants liberation, you are the ocean of love and compassion, friend and Lord of the oppressed, ever pure and resident of Prashanthi Nilayam.
