## Meaning

Sri Ranga is all charming and Lord Sai is resting on serpent "Sesha". He is all compassionate and worshipped in different forms as Sri Ranga, Govinda, holder of Govardhana mountain, Mother Sai and Gopala. He is all attractive, charming Lord Sai.
