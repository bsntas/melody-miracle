## Lyrics

Hey Deena Bandhu Deena Natha Sai Avatara
Prem Ke Sindho Karuna Sindhu Sai Avatara
Sarva Dharma Ke Ek Swaroopa Sai Avatara
Bhajorey Sai Avatara

## Meaning

Let us sing the Glory of Sai the Incarnation of the Modern Era who is the friend and Emancipator of the forlorn. Sai is the Single Godhead of all the different Faiths and the Ocean of Supreme Love and compassion.
