## Lyrics

Amba Sahita Samba Sadashiva
Hara Hara Hara Hara Mahadeva
Hara Hara Hara Hara Mahadeva Shambho
Parvathi Ramana Sadashiva [ Hara Hara ]

## Meaning

We Pray to Shiva, who is accompanied by Mother Parvathi. We chant the names of Mahadeva, the divine consort of Parvathi.
