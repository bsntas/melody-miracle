## Lyrics

Arjuna noyō ni tachiagare, mezameyo kami no michakara de
Uchū no kami yo Krishna, Sai Baba, Sai Baba
Uchi naru chikara yo shin no yūki wo atae kudasai, Kami Sama
Uchi naru chikara yo shin no yūki wo atae kudasai Sai Baba

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, please stand up and awaken within me with Your beautiful, godly power like You did in Arjuna before. O Sai Baba, You are the Lord of the Universe, the embodiment of Krishna. Please bestow the true inner power of fearlessness upon me, O Lord Sai Baba.
