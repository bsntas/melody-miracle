## Lyrics

Sai Sathya Sai Sai Guru Deva
Darshana Deejo Baba Tribhuvana Pala
Darshana Deejo Baba Sai Nandalala
Krupa Karo Sai Deva

## Meaning

Our Lord and Preceptor, Sai, the protector of the three worlds, please be kind and let us have Your darshan (vision of a divine being).
