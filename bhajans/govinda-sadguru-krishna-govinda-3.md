## Lyrics

Govinda Sadguru Krishna Govinda (3)

## Meaning

Glory to the Divine Preceptor Sai, You are verily Lord Krishna, the protector of the cows (Govinda). You are Vahey Guru and verily the Truth. Sing the names of Krishna, Hari and Govinda.
