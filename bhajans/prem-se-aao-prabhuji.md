## Meaning

O Lord, please come and decorate our heart with Your presence with Love. O Lord Narayana, let our eyes be filled with Your enchanting form as the dancing Nandalala (Krishna). O Sai, Lord of Shirdi and Parthi, let our ears listen to absorbing stories of Your plays. May we ever remain absorbed in Your presence.
