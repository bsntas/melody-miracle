## Lyrics

Tu que eres la lluvia
Tu que eres el sol
Haz que mi mente sea limpia
Y puro mi corazon, oh! Senor
Tu que vaz por la vida.
Cosechando amor
Toma mi mano y perdona.
Mis faltas oh! Sen`or
Toma mi mano y camina.
Dentro de mi corazon, dame amor

## Meaning

O Lord, You are the rain and the sun, please cleanse my mind. Make my heart pure, my Lord. You sow the seed of love all through my life. Take my hand and forgive all my faults. O my Lord, take my hand and walk. Within my heart, let me develop love.
