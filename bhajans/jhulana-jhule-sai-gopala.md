## Lyrics

Jhulana Jhule Sai Gopala
Krishna Natavara Nandalala [ Jhulana Jhule ... ]
Govinda Giridhara Gokula Bala
Shyama Manohara Muraliwala
Shobhita Sundara Prem Hindola

## Meaning

Oh Gopala, rock in the swing of my heart, the mischievous son of Nanda. Govinda, the dark-complexioned boy of Gokul who held the Govardhana mountain high, has the Flute in His hand and a bewitching smile on His face.
