Shiva Shakthi Roopini Sai Narayani [ Sai Saraswathi ... ]

## Meaning

Glory to Mother Saraswathi, the Goddess of learning and music who is adorned in white (represents purity) and a garland of flowers. She holds the Veena and the books. Lord Sai embodies the Mothers Saraswathi, Lakshmi and Shakthi and is the protector of the meek.
