## Lyrics

Jagadambe Bhavani Lalita
Jaya Samba Sadashiva Dwaita [ Jagadambe Bhavani ... ]
Chaturanana Brahma Vanita
Jaya Vishwa Priya Sri Mata
Jaya Vishnu Priya Sai Mata

## Meaning

Glory to Mother Parvathi, the Mother of the Universe and the consort of Lord Shiva. She is verily Mother Sai who is adored by Brahma, Vishnu, and the entire Universe.
