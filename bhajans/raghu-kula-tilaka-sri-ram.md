## Lyrics

Raghu Kula Tilaka Sri Ram Sri Rama (3)
Kausalyesha Jaya Jaya Rama
Ravana Mardhana Sita Rama [ Raghu Kula ... ]
Raghupathi Raghava Raja Rama
Patitha Pavana Sita Rama

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Rama, the jewel of Raghu dynasty and son of mother Kausalya. The Lord of Sita, who destroyed Ravana is the savior of the destitute.
