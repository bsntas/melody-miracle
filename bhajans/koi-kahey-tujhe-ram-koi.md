|---|---|

## Lyrics

Koi Kahey Tujhe Ram Koi Kahey Tujhe Shyam
Koi Kahey Tujhe Yeshu Bhagawan
Koi Kahey Allah Tero Naam
Sai Baba Sai Baba Parthipuri Sai Baba

## Meaning

People say You are Lord Rama, Shyam (Lord Krishna), Lord Jesus and some say Your name is Allah. O Lord Sai Baba, You are verily all the Gods and You reside in Puttaparthi.
