## Meaning

Oh Lord Shiva adorning the snake as an ornament! Oh Sai Shiva in the form of a Linga beneath a snake. Oh Shiva! Who has the Nandi or the divine bull as your vehicle, Oh Shiva! Who is the form of Divine music. Oh Shiva! We worship you.
