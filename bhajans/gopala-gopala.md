## Lyrics

Gopala Gopala
Giridhara Bala Gopala [ Gopala ... ]
Murali Lola Gopala
Murahara Sai Gopala

## Meaning

Chant the name of Gopala - one who lifted the mountain, the player of the flute, the slayer of the demon Mura and who is our Sai Gopala.
