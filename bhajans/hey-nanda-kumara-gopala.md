## Lyrics

Hey Nanda Kumara Gopala
Brindavana Sanchara Gopala
Mana Mohana Murali Gopala
Mana Mohana Radhey Gopala

## Meaning

Lord Gopala is a Mind charmer, who plays the Flute, consort of Radha, son of Nanda and the One who moved about Brindavana.
