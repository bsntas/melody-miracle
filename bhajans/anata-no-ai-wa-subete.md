## Lyrics

Anata no Ai wa Subete wo Tsutsumu, Kono Yo no Kanashimi Kurushimi Tsutsumu

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, Your love surrounds everything, even the sadness and pain in this world. You guide us through illusion towards You. You bless us and forgive us at every moment. Your kindness pierces our hearts.
