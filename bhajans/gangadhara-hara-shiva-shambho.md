## Lyrics

Gangadhara Hara Shiva Shambho
Vibhuti Sundara Sai Shambho
Om Hara Hara Hara Hara Shambho
Haalaahala Dhara Shiva Sai Shambho

## Meaning

This is a prayer to Lord Shiva who Has the river Ganges on His head; Who has Vibhuti all over His body; Who swallowed the deadly poison (Haalaa-hala) ; Who is also our Lord Sai.
