## Meaning

Glory to the beautiful elephant-faced Lord Ganesha. The lover of the sweet Modak and bearer of the single tusk, His presence is auspicious. Lord Ganesha is the son of Goddess Parvathi and bestows intellect and ability to His devotees. We worship Lord Ganesha first among all deities so that He can remove our obstacles, as He is verily the embodiment of all divine qualities.
