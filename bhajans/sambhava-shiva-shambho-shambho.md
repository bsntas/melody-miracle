## Lyrics

Sambhava Shiva Shambho Shambho
Narthana Nataraja Nataraja [ Sambhava Shiva ... ]
Mruthyunjaya Mahesha Mahesha
Parthi Pureeshwara Sai Paramesha

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Shiva who is the Cosmic dancer Nataraja, also known as Mruthyunjaya (Conqueror of death). He is none other than Lord Sai residing in Parthi.
