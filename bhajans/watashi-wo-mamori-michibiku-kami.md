## Lyrics

Watashi wo Mamori Michibiku Kami yo Sai Baba yo
Tomadoi Mayou Watashi no Kokoro no Sasae
Anata ni Meguri Aeta Yorokobi wo
Ookina Ai ni Kaeta Sasagemasu
Arigatou Sai Baba, Arigatou Kokoro kara
Subete no Ai wa Hitotsu, Anata to Hitotsu

## Meaning

Lord Sai Baba, You protect and guide us. When we have lost our way, You support our hearts. When I meet You, it brings me supreme joy. I devote myself to the grand love You have given us. Thank You Sai Baba from the bottom of my heart. All love is one, and everything is one with You.
