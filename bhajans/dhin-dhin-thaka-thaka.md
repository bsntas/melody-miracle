## Meaning

Behold as Lord Shiva dances gracefully to the beats of the Damaru (miniature drum), Manjira (cymbals), Dholak (folk drum). His temple resounds with the beautiful sound of the Ghungroo (anklet bells). Worship Lord Shiva as the King of all creation.
