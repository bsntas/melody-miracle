## Lyrics

Jaya Krishna Madhusudhana Jaya Radha Mana Mohana
Brindavana Sanchari Govardhana Giridhari
Jaya Parthi Vihari Sai Narayana
Narayana Sai Narayana

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Krishna, the one who vanquished the demon Madhu and who charms Radha. He is Lord Narayana's incarnattion who moved about in Brindavan and held aloft the Govardhana mountain. That very Lord has incarnated as Lord Sai who now dwells in Parthi.
