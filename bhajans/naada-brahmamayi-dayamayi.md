## Lyrics

Naada Brahmamayi Dayamayi
Bhava Raga Laya Gaana Vinodini [ Naada Brahma ... ]
Veena Venu Mrudanga Vinodini
Aashritha Phala Dayini Kalyani (2)

## Meaning

O Divine Mother who is the form of divine music, the embodiment of compassion, You are the one who enjoys the tune, the expression, the rhythm of music, the one who is delighted by the string instrument Veena, the flute and the percussion instrument Mrudanga, the one who grants boons to those who surrender to You.
