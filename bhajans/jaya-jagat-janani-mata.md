## Lyrics

Jaya Jagat Janani Mata
Parthi Nivasini Trishoola Dharini [ Jaya Jagat ... ]

## Meaning

Victory to Mother Sai, the Mother of the Universe, who holds the Trident and dwells in Parthi. She is dressed in white and is glorified as Amba, Bhavani and Durga. She is the very personification of Divine Energy.
