## Lyrics

Mathuradhipathey Nanda Mukunda
Brindavana Jaya Paramananda
Radha Madhava Jaya Govinda (2)
Radha Madhava Hey Nanda Nanda

## Meaning

O Lord Krishna, Lord of Mathura, the son of Nanda, You are the beloved of Radha. Victory to You, O the blissful Lord of Brindavan.
