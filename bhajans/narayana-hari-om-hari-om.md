## Meaning

O Lord Narayana, You rescue us once we call out Your name. O Lord Hari, You are the protector of the three worlds and the most compassionate one. You have Garuda, the mighty Divine Eagle as Your vehicle. Prostrations to Thee, Lord Hari, the lotus-eyed one.
