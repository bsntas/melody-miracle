## Lyrics

Yesu Masiha Sarva Loka Nayaka
Allah Ho Akbar Ilahi Sathya Sai Mahadeva
Sai Shirdi Sai Sathya Sai Jagannatha
Zohrashtra Buddha Mahavir Sathya Sai Mahadeva

## Meaning

Lord Sathya Sai, the master of the entire world and of all faiths, responds to all names such as Yesu Masiha, Allah, Zohrashtra, Buddha and Mahavir.
