## Meaning

Chant the names of Lord Krishna - Govinda and Mukunda. He is the crown jewel of Brindavan. While Lord Krishna endows one with Supreme Bliss, the lotus-faced Lord Sai is the very personification of Truth, Awareness and Bliss.
