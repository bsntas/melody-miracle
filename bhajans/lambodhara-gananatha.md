## Lyrics

Lambodhara Gananatha (2)
Lambodhara Vighneshwara
Mayureshwara Siddeshwara
Namo Namo Gananatha Gajanana Lambodhara

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Ganesha, the large-bellied leader of the Ganas, the remover of obstacles. Sing the glory of the elephant-faced Lord who is worshipped as Mayureshwara (Lord of the peacocks) and Siddheshwara (Lord of all powers). Glory to Lord Ganesha who blesses His devotees with wisdom, prosperity, and success.
