## Lyrics

Govinda Bhajo Sri Hari Gopala Bhajo
Gopi Gopala Bhajo Shyama Bhajo
Govinda Giridhari Gopala Bhajo
Rakumayi Panduranga Vittala Bhajo
Sri Sai Parabrahma Ananda Bhajo (Hey)

## Meaning

Chant and worship the various names of Krishna, Govinda, Hari, Gopala, Shyama, Giridhari. Chant the names of Rakumayi Panduranga Vittala (the beloved of Rakumayi and the Lord of Pandharpur) who is also presently Sai of Parthi, who is ever supreme and blissful.
