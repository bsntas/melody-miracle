## Lyrics

Saa Utaou Ai wo Utaou
Ai wo Wakachiau Yorokobi wo
Kami to Tomo ni Utaouyo
Let's sing a song for Love, Saiai no Tomo yo
Let's love all serve all Ai ni Ikiyou

## Meaning

Let us sing out with love and share this joy with everyone. Let us sing out to be together with Swami. Let us sing a song for Love to our beloved friend. Let's live with love and serve everyone.
