## Meaning

O Lord Sai, I arose from Your existence. I felt a profoundly deep and warm feeling in my heart. I bloomed into a small flower and I saw Your beautiful form. The wide sky is Your domain. You healed our sorrow and pain with Your warmth. Swami, no matter how, I will be together with You. Swami, no matter what, You will always exist in eternity.
