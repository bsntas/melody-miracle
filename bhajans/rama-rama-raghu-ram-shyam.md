## Lyrics

Rama Rama Raghu Ram Shyam Shyam Ghana Shyam
Koi Kahey Ram Koi Kahey Rahim Koi Kahey Ghana Shyam
Nanak Yesu Buddha Mahavir
Sab Hai Sai Bhagawan (2)

## Meaning

Sing of Lord Rama, the dark-hued Krishna, Some call Him Ram, some Rahim, some say the dark blue hued one. Some bow to Nanak, Jesus, Buddha, Mahavir. Lord Sai is one with all of them.
