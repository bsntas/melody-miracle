## Lyrics

Murali Gaana Lola Krishna Madhava
Venu Gaana Lola Krishna Keshava
Mathuradhipathey Radha Lola
Madhura Madhura Hey Yadu Nandalala
Hey Nandalala Yadu Nandalala (3)

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Krishna, the player of soulful music on the flute who is also glorified as Keshava. Glory to the Lord of Mathura and the beloved of Radha. Glory to the sweet son of Nanda of Yadava dynasty.
