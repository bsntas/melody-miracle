## Lyrics

Kamisama Sai Baba, Subete no Kamisama
Haato no Owasu Kamisama Douzo Negai wo
Okiki Kudasai Watashi no Kamisama
Kurushimu Hito no Tameni Watashi wo Otsukai Kudasai
Sore ga Watashi no Negai Sai Baba

## Meaning

Lord Sai Baba, You are the Lord of everything. My wish is for You to enter my heart. Please hear me, my dear Lord. Please use me as Your instrument my Lord, for I am suffering. This is my wish to You, Sai Baba.
