## Lyrics

Sai Baba Sakala Bhuvana Ke Daata (3)
Roopa Manohara Kamala Kantha
Yuga Avatar Sai Baba Daya Karo Parthi Baba
Shirdi Parthi Hrudaya Nivasi
Prashanthi Sundara Sai

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai Baba, who is the Protector of the universe. Glory to Lord Sai, whose Form is bewitchingly beautiful and soft like that of a Lotus. Glory to you, Avatar of this Age, shower Your Mercy, who is the Avatar of Shirdi and we pray to you to enshrine Yourself in Our Hearts, Oh Lord Prashanthi Sai.
