## Lyrics

Geeta Dayaka Hey Giridhari
Govinda Govinda Narayana
Peetambara Dhara Parthi Vihari
Patithodhari Sai Murari

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Krishna, the one who gave the knowledge of the Bhagavat Geeta to the world, the one who lifted mountain 'Govardhana', the one who is dressed in yellow silk robes, the one who helps the downtrodden and who is called by many names such as Govinda, Narayana and Murari. He is none other than Lord Sai who resides at Puttaparthi.
