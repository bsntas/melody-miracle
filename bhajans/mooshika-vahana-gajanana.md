## Meaning

Glory to Lord Ganesha, the elephant-faced one who rides on Mooshika, the divine mouse. Hail the son of Parvathi and the one who bestows intelligence and Divine Power. Glory to the leader of the Ganas (Gananayaka), the destroyer of sins and the one who prevails over good things. Chant the name of Lord Sai who resides in Parthi, who bestows auspiciousness.
