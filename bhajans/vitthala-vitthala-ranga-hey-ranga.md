## Meaning

Worship Lord Vitthala who is also praised as Vithoba and Ranga. The lord of Rakumayi (Rukmini), He invests divine energy in maintaining the garden of our hearts. As the slayer of demons (like Mura), He lovingly bestows His protection on us. He is verily our Lord Sathya Sai.
