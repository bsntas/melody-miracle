## Lyrics

Darshan Do Ghana Shyam Shyama
Radhey Govinda Radhey Gopala [ Darshan Do ... ]
Mathura Natha Deena Dayala
Parthi Puri Ghana Shyam Shyama

## Meaning

Oh the dark-hued Lord Krishna, bless us with Your vision, the Lord of Radha. You are the Lord of Mathura, the most compassionate one of the weak and meek. You are none other than the Sai, the Lord of Parthi.
