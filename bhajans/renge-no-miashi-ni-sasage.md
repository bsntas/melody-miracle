## Lyrics

Renge no Miashi ni Sasage yo Ai no Kokoro Sai Ram
Inochi no Hikari Mamorita mou Ai no Inori Sai Ram
Ai wa Kami, Kami wa Ai, Shinri Tsutaeta mou
Sai Isu, Sai Buddha, Sai Baba, Sai Ram
Sai Rama, Hari Sai Krishna, Hari Sai Baba, Sai Ram

## Meaning

Bow at the divine lotus feet of Sai Ram who dwells in our heart with love. Sai Ram, You are my divine protector, the light of my life, and the love of my prayers. God is Love, Love is God, this is the divine truth You convey to us. Sai, You are Jesus, Buddha, and Baba. Sai, You are Rama, Krishna, and Baba.
