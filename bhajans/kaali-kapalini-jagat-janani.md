## Lyrics

Kaali Kapalini Jagat Janani
Lakshmi Narayani Jagat Janani

Jayatu Sai Janani Janani

## Meaning

Glory to the Mother of the Universe, Mother Kaali, who wears a garland of Skulls. Glory to Goddess Lakshmi, the consort of Lord Narayana,. Glory to the Guardian of the Three worlds, the one who bears three eyes and is the eternal one. Glory to the Universal Mother Sai who has Her abode in Parthi.
