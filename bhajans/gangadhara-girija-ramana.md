## Meaning

Chant the holy name of Shiva who adorns the Ganges in his matted flock of hair, who is the beloved of Girija. Chant the holy names Shambho, Shankara, Samba Sadashiva (one who bestows auspiciousness and happiness) who is also the Lord of Shirdi and Parthi.
