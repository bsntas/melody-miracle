## Lyrics

Que todos los seres de todos los mundos
Tengan paz y felicidad
Samastha loka sukhino bhavanthu
Porque los sabios y santos oraron
Samastha loka sukhino bhavanthu

## Meaning

May all beings in all worlds have peace and happiness. ‘Samastha loka sukhino bhavanthu’. That’s why You came, my divine Sathya Sai. Because the sages and saints prayed ‘Samastha loka sukhino bhavanthu’.
