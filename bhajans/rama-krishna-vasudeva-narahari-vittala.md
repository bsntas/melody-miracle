## Meaning

Chant the names of Lord Rama, Krishna, Vasudeva, Hari who have incarnated in human form. Glory to Lord Vittala, who is Rama and Krishna. Lord Panduranga, the indweller of the heart, is verily Lord Sai of Shirdi and Parthi. He who preaches Truth, Righteousness, Peace is Sai, who encompasses all. Sing the glory of Panduranga Vittala.
