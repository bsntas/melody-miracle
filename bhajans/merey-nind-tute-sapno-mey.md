## Lyrics

Merey Nind Tute Sapno Mey
Adhi Rath Prabhu Aye

## Meaning

In the middle of the night, Lord Sai was kind enough to interrupt my sleep by appearing in a dream in His beautiful, all-merciful and infinite form.
