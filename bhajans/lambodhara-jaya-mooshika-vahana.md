|---|---|

## Lyrics

Lambodhara Jaya Mooshika Vahana
Pashupathi Nandana Bhava Bhaya Bhanjana [ Lambodhara Jaya ... ]
Vighna Vinasha Vijaya Vijaya Gajanana
Hey Gananatha Gowri Nandana
Parama Niranjana Paahi Gajanana
Paahi Gajanana Parama Niranjana

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Ganesha, the pot-bellied son of Lord Shiva, who removes the fear of our worldly existence and life's obstacles, and bestows successes. The son of Mother Gowri, Lord Ganesha, is supremely pure and merciful.
