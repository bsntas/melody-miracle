Balaji Sai Parthi Nivasa

## Meaning

The bestower of Auspiciousness, the Lord of the Earth, destroyer of Sins has come down in human form as Lord Srinivasa at the Holy place Tirumala and who has Mother Lakshmi in His Heart. Lord Srinivasa is also known as Govinda, Venkatesha and Balaji has now incarnated in Parthi as Lord Sai.
