## Meaning

We pray to Lord Narayana from whose navel springs the Lotus of creation (Padmanabha), who is the Overlord of the sensory world, the lotus-eyed one who removes ignorance of devotees and helps them escape the cycle of birth and death. We pray to Lord Damodara (Krishna), the one who was tied by the waist, by His mother, Yashoda. O Lord, You are the all pervasive Universal Form, the preceptor of the Vedic knowledge and the one with blemishless character.  We pray to that primordial Lord born as Sai in Parthi.
