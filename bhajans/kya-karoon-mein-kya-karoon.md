# Kya Karoon Mein Kya Karoon

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Sai |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Kya Karoon Mein Kya Karoon
Koi Nahin Mera Terey Bina
Shirdi Vasa Hey Prabho
Parthi Vasa Daya Karo
Mangala Arathi Kab Logey
Mangala Guna Gaoon Mein

## Meaning

What will I do, O Lord Sai of Shirdi, Lord of Parthi? Kindly have mercy on me. I have no one but You. While I sing Thy glory, please accept the auspicious waving of the flame.
