## Lyrics

Anata kara Watashi ni Sosogareru Ai wa
Nani yori mo Amaku, Tsuyoku, Yasashii
Watashi wa Anata no Moto he Aruite Imasu ka

## Meaning

Lord Sai, You bless us with Your divine love. You are more sweet, strong, and kind than anything in this world. I am walking towards You. I am continuing my journey towards You. Please keep watching over me, for I shall be with You soon. Sai Ram, I am moving towards You.
