## Meaning

Glory to Lord Shankara, the consort of Mother Shankari (Parvathi) and the One who holds the Ganges in His Locks, who is as beautiful as the Moon and has Lotus eyes, who adorns serpents as ornaments and who holds the crescent Moon, We pray and offer our salutations to Thee.
