## Lyrics

Radha Jeevana Raasa Vilola
Rakumayi Vittala Vittala Bala
Gopi Vallabha Gokula Bala (2)
Kali Yuga Sai Krishna Gopala

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Gopala (Krishna), the very life of Radha and the one who performed the celestial dance with the cowherds. Glory to the consort of Rakhumayi, Lord Vittala. Glory to the beloved cowherd boy of Gokula. Glory to Lord Sai who has incarnated in the present Kali age and who is verily Lord Krishna.
