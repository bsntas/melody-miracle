# Jaya Jagadeeshwari Akhilandeshwari

> **Source:** [Sai Rhythms](https://sairhythms.sathyasai.org/song/jaya-jagadeeshwari-akhilandeshwari)

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Devi |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Jaya Jagadeeshwari Akhilandeshwari
Parthi Pureeshwari Palaya Maam
Jaya Bhuvaneshwari Raja Rajeshwari
Vara Dayini Amba Palaya Maam

## Meaning

Victory to the Mother of the Universe, Mother Sai, the resident of Puttaparthi and the bestower of wishes. O Mother Sai, kindly protect me.
