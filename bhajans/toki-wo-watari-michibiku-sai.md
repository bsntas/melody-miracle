## Meaning

Lord Sai Baba who is verily Lord Rama and Krishna, You cross through time itself to guide us. Sai's love gives us power, so let us proceed towards Him. Victory to Thee, Lord Sai Ram, You give us courage so that we may undertake the journey to be with You.
