## Lyrics

Anata ni Michibikare Watashi wa Ayumu
Subete wa Kono Hikari Kaeru Ai no Shirube
Tataeyo Sai Gopala, Jaya Gopala, Sai Gopala
Kami no Ai Amaku, Amaku, Watashi wo Mitasu

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, I walk the path that leads to You. Everything returns to this light through the guidance of love. Sing praise to Sai Gopala., victory to Lord Gopala. God's love is the sweetest thing, and it gratifies me.
