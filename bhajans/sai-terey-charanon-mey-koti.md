## Lyrics

Sai Terey Charanon Mey Koti Pranam
Sharan Mey Lelo Sai Prabho [ Sai Terey ... ]

## Meaning

O Lord Sai, I offer a million salutations at Thy Lotus feet, please take me in Your fold. You are my mother, father, kith and kin. You are my every thing O Sai, I offer myself at Your feet.
