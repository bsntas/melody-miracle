# Lambodhara Shiva Gowri Sutha

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Ganesha |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Lambodhara Shiva Gowri Sutha
He Shiva Nandana Bala Gajanana
Gajanana Shubanana [ Lambodhara Shiva ... ]
He Shiva Nandana Bhava Bhaya Bhanjana
Shambhu Kumara Gajanana (2)

## Meaning

Chant the name of pot-bellied son of Lord Shiva and Mother Gowri, Lord Gajanana, who destroys the fears of worldly existence and bestows good fortune and auspiciousness.
