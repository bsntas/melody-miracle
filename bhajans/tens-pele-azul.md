## Lyrics

Tens a pele azul, olhos de lótus, dança de serpente
Shiva, Senhor do Universo, destrói o mal em minha mente
Tu que reges a vida e tens o poder da tranformoção
Shiva, transforma o meu karma, conserva o meu dharma em meu coração

## Meaning

O Lord Shiva, You have blue skin, lotus eyes and a  snake dances around Your neck. You are the Lord of the Universe, destroy the evil in my mind. You rule over all life and You have the power of transformation. O Lord Shiva, transform my karma (past actions), and keep my dharma (righteousness) in my heart.
