## Lyrics

Sai Ramanin Naamam Solvom
Pinigalum Vinaigalum Neengidume [ Sathya Sai ... ]
Sathya Dharmamum Shanthi Premaiyum
Nittamum Manadil Nilai Pera Vendum
Siddiyum Perave Arul Purivaye
Shirdi Parthi Baba Sai
Sai Ram Sathya Sai Ram (3)

## Meaning

Let us chant the name of Sai Ram, so all our ills and troubles will pass. May truth, righteousness, peace, and love prevail in our minds forever. Grace us with Your blessings, O Lord of Shirdi and Parthi.
