## Lyrics

Hara Hara Shankara Namami Shankara
Uma Pathey Kailasa Shankara
Gangadhara Hara Chandrakaladhara
Parthi Pureeshwara Shiva Sai Shankara

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Shankara (Shiva) the consort of Uma, whose abode is Mount Kailasa. Lord Shiva has Mother Ganges and the crescent moon over His Head. He is the same Sai who dwells in Parthi.
