## Lyrics

## Meaning

Oh Lotus-Eyed Lord Sai Narayana! You are the jewel of the Ratnakara dynasty. Oh Darling son of Mother Eshwaramba and the sustainer of all creation. Your soft, Lotus Feet are worshipped and adored by the entire universe.
