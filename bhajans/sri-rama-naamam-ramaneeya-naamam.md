## Meaning

Oh Lord Rama, your name is as enchanting as your form. You are beautiful and full of divine qualities worshipped by sages and pious people You are the Consort of Janaki and the Lord of Universe Sai Rama! Your name provides deliverance from sins. (Taraka Naamam)
