## Meaning

The songs of God and Love, fill the entire sky to the brim. Your love touches all of time, You are the only object of my thoughts. Without pause, You dwell within our prayers. Sathya Sai Baba, I live within Your wind that blows everywhere. Sathya Sai Baba, I will become one with Your song.
