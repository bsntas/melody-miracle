## Lyrics

Natavara Natavara Nanda Kumara
Nanda Kumara Jaya Goparipala
Yaduvara Yaduvara Brinda Vihara
Brinda Vihara Jaya Parthi Vihara

## Meaning

Hail the son of Nanda, who appears mischievous. Hail the son of Nanda who lovingly cares for all the cows. The jewel of the dynasty of Yadu, who strolls in Brindavan. Hail, Sai of Parthi, who also strolls as he did in Brindavan.
