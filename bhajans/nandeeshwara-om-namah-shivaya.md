## Meaning

O Lord Shiva, Lord of the divine bull Nandi, prayerful prostrations to Thee. O Lord of cosmic dance, the one who adorns the snake as an ornament, salutations to Thee. Humble salutations to the bestower of auspiciousness, Lord Shiva, who is none other than Lord Sai.
