## Lyrics

Karunalavala Hey Nandalala
Ravela Ravela Rave Gopala
Brindavana Bala Hey Gopi Lola
Mana Mohana Hey Shyama Gopala
Madhusudhana Hey Sai Gopala [ Ravela Ravela ... ]

## Meaning

O Compassionate son of Nanda, Gopala (Krishna), residing in Brindavan, the enchanter of Gopis and the destroyer of demon Madhu, please come. O Lord Sai, please come, You are none other than Gopala.
