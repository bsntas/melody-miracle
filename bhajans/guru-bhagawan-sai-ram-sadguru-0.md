## Lyrics

Guru Bhagawan Sai Ram Sadguru Natha Sri Bhagawan
Dayamaya Deena Dayalu
Chinmaya Roopa Sai Bhagawan

Nithya Lola Sri Sai Bhagawan

## Meaning

Worship and pay obeisance at the lotus feet of the most supreme, noble teacher, Lord Sai Baba. He is the embodiment of truth, love and peace. He grants spiritual knowledge.
