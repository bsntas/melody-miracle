## Lyrics

Omkareshwara He Shiva Nandana
Girija Sutha Gananatha Gajanana [ Omkareshwara He ... ]
Siddhi Vinayaka Mooshaka Vahana
Lambodhara He Sankata Mochana

## Meaning

Lord Ganesha, son of Lord Shiva and Mother Girija (Parvathi) is the Supreme Lord, who grants success, removes misfortunes and is the Lord of divine powers.
