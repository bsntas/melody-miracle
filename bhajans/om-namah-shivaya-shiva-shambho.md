## Meaning

We offer our prayerful salutations to Lord Shiva. He is the embodiment of Supreme bliss, though supporting the turbulent Ganges in his matted hair. He is the ever-blissful, formless form, who bestows Nirvana (enlightenment), who is our Lord Sai Shambho.
