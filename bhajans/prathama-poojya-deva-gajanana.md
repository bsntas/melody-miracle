## Meaning

Offer worship primarily to Lord Ganesha, the elephant-faced one. He is verily Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi. Glory to Lord Ganesha, the son of Mother Parvathi, the embodiment of the Pranava (Om). He destroys illusion and bestows good fortune.
