## Lyrics

Kaminaru Itoshii Sai no Ai ni Tsutsumare

## Meaning

Surround yourself with the divine love of Sai. Everything melts away in this love and overflowing joy. I will surround myself with this gentle love. I sing out to Sai Krishna with this true love in my heart.
