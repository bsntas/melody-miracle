## Lyrics

Hari Om Tat Sat Hari Om Tat Sat Hari Om Tat Sat Hari Om
Krishna Krishna Krishna Krishna Jaya Radha Krishna
Govinda Govinda Bhajo Radhey Govinda Gopala Gopala Bhajo Krishna Mukunda

## Meaning

Understand O Mind, the Primordial Sound Om is that Effulgent pure Consciousness. Chant the names of Lord Krishna, Govinda who are also embodiments of that Supreme Consciousness.
