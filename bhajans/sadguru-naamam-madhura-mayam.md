# Sadguru Naamam Madhura Mayam

## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Guru |
| **Language** | Tamil |

## Lyrics

Sadguru Naamam Madhura Mayam
Sundara Roopam Jyothir Mayam (Ati)
Shyamala Vadanam Shanthi Mayam
Prema Pradayaka Pavana Naamam
Paapa Vinashaka Prabhu Naamam
Sada Bhajeham Sai Naamam

## Meaning

Let us ever chant the name of the true Guru, Lord Sai, suffused with sweetness, beauty, divine effulgence, and peace. His sanctified name grants love and dispels sins.
