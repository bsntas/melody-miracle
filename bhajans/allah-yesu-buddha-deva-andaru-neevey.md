## Meaning

Salutations to You, Lord Sai, You are Allah, Jesus, Buddha, Mahavir and Nanak. We also adore You as Rama, Krishna and Shiva. You are the essence of Veda, Geeta and other Scriptures. You are the One God worshipped by all religions. O Sai, You love and foster all faiths.
