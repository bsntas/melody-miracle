## Meaning

Salutations to the Lord of Lords, Sai, who is verily the effulgence of Divine Light and the bestower of Divine Love. Salutations to Lord Sai, the destroyer of miseries and the one who enthralls His devotees. Salutations to Lord Sai who uplifts the entire universe and from whom the Vedas originated. Salutations to Lord Sai, the source of the primordial sound Om and the liberator of the downtrodden.
