## Lyrics

Ganapathey Paahi Maha Mathey
Gnana Swaroopa Namostuthey
Pankaja Charana Daya Nidhey
Mangala Kara Hey Krupa Nidhey
Sapta Swarapathi Vidya Pathey
Sayeeshwara Twam Suguna Nidhey

## Meaning

O Lord Ganapathi (Ganesha), the highly intellectual one, protect us. Salutations to Thee, the embodiment of knowledge. You are the treasure house of compassion with a lotus feet. You are the master of all forms of knowledge and music. O Lord Sai, who is verily Lord Ganesha, You are the reserve of divine qualities.
