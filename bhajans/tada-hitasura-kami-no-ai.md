## Lyrics

Tada hitasura Kami no ai wo omoi ikiru
Ah Anata ga subete Oh Kami no ai ni ikiru
Ah Anata wa Watashi no subete Oh Kami no ai ni ikiru

## Meaning

Always think about the God of Love with all your heart. Oh God of Love Sai, You are everything in this world, and You are everything to me.
