## Meaning

Always remember the name of the son of Kausalya, Lord Rama, who is the beloved of Mother Janaki and who belongs to the Raghu dynasty. Lord Rama's name liberates us from the cycle of birth and death. He fulfills the desires of devotees. He is all mercy for the hapless and got Ahalya liberated from the curse.
