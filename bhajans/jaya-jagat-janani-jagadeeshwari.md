## Lyrics

Jaya Jagat Janani Jagadeeshwari
Parthipuri Parameshwari
Anantha Koti Brahmanda Nayaki
Dasha Maha Vidya Veda Vilasini
Mukthi Pradayini Bhava Bhaya Haarini
Sathya Sai Bhuvaneshwari

## Meaning

Hail the Universal Sovereign Mother Sathya Sai Bhuvaneshwari, who dwells in Parthi, the Goddess of innumerable Galaxies that exist, an Exponent of all Ten Texts (4 Vedas and the 6 Scriptures) who confers Liberation and negates the vicious cycle of Birth and Death.
