## Lyrics

Harey Murarey Madhukaitabhaarey
Govinda Gopala Mukunda Shourie
Anantha Sridhara Govinda Keshava
Mukunda Madhava Narayana [ Harey Murarey ... ]
Devaki Tanaya Gopika Ramana
Bhaktha Uddharana Trivikrama

## Meaning

Sing the Glory of the Lord Krishna by chanting His names - Govinda, Gopala, Sridhara, Keshava, Mukunda, Madhava. Glory to the son of Devaki, the one who is dear to the Gopikas and the one who annihilated the demons Madhu and Kaitabha.
