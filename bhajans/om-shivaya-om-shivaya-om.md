## Meaning

Chant the mantra " Om Shivaya". Chant the name of the Lord of Uma, Resident of Kailasa (pure hearts), Holder of the Trishoola (Trident), the Three-Eyed One, Lord Shiva. Lord Shiva, who enjoys the cosmic dance along with the rhythm of the Damaru (small drum) and the sound of bells.
