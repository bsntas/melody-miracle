## Lyrics

Shambho Shankara Samba Sadashiva
Sai Shankara Shambho Sai Shankara Shambho
Girija Shankara Hara Hara Shiva Shiva [ Sai Shankara ... ]

## Meaning

Chant the names of Shiva, the beautiful divine dancer who wears the Ganges in His matted hair. Bow to the Supreme Lord Sai who resides in Puttaparthi.
