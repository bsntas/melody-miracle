## Lyrics

Jai Ma Jai Ma Jai Ma Sai Ma [ Jai Jai Ma ... ]

## Meaning

Glory be to Mother Vaishnov Devi and Victory be to Her, the One of dazzling Light, who has Lion as Her Vehicle. Victory to Her who dwells among the mountain ranges and who is the Mother of the Entire Universe.
