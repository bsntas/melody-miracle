## Meaning

Chant the divine name of Lord Krishna, the one who has the face like that of the moon, the one who belongs to the Yadu Dynasty and the one who lifted the Mandara mountain. Chant the name of Lord Sai, the lovely son of Mother Eshwaramba and the one who has an impeccable history.
