## Meaning

Hail Lord Muruga, the six-faced Lord, son of Lord Shiva and Mother Parvathi. We surrender to Him as He destroys the ill effects of our earthly activities and grants us welfare by His powerful weapon, Vel (spear). He has come as the bestower of Happiness, Lord Sathya Sai, for the sake of His devotees.
