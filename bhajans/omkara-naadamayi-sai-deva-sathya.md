## Meaning

Lord Sathya Sai is the embodiment of the Omkara. Pranava, the primal sound, represents the Almighty. That very sound realized in Truth has taken the form of our dear Lord Sathya Sai. Continuous chanting of His name destroys ill effects of all sins committed by us. The touch of his Lotus Feet redeems us to lead a spiritual and purposeful life and become an example.
