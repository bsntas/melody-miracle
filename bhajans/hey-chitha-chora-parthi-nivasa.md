## Lyrics

Hey Chitha Chora Parthi Nivasa
Indu Vadana Sri Sayeesha
Gopi Vallabha Gokula Nandana
Govardhanadhara Gopala ( Sai )

## Meaning

Our Salutations to Sayeesha, the stealer of our Hearts, who has the countenance of the cool Moon. He is the same Lord Gopala who had earlier incarnated at Gokula as the Lord of the Gopis and the one who held aloft the Govardhana mountain.
