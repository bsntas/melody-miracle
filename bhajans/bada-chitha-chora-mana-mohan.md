## Lyrics

Bada Chitha Chora Mana Mohan
Mera Chitha Chora Jaganmohan
Ayodhya Ke Sita Ram
Brindavan Ke Radhey Shyam
Shirdi Ke Sai Baba
Parthi Ke Sathya Sai

## Meaning

Chant the names of the Sita Ram - the Lord of Ayodhya, Radhey Shyam - the Lord of Brindavan, Sai Baba of Shirdi and Puttaparthi, the darling of every heart.
