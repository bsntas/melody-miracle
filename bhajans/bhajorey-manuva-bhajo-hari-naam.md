|---|---|

## Lyrics

Bhajorey Manuva Bhajo Hari Naam
Bhajo Ghana Shyam Naam Bhajo Radhey Shyam [ Bhajorey Manuva ... ]
Harey Krishna Harey Ram Bolo Sai Raja Ram
Govinda Gopala Bolo Bhajo Sai Ram

## Meaning

Chant the names of Lord Hari, the dark-complexioned Lord Krishna (known by many names such as Govinda and Gopala), Lord Rama and Lord Sai.
