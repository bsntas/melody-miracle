## Meaning

Worship and chant the divine name of Lord Shiva, Shankara, the embodiment of auspiciousness who dances to the rhythm of Damaru, the drum that sounds "dama dama" and Ghanta, the bell that sounds "ghana ghana". Lord Sai is verily Lord Shiva.
