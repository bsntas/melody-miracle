## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Ram Jaya Megha Shyam
Dasharatha Nandana Danava Bheema
Jagadabhi Ram Janaki Ram
Suramuni Vanditha Sundara Ram

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Rama, the one who is deep blue in complexion, the son of Dasharatha and the uprooter of the demonic clan. He is the consort of Mother Janaki, the Lord of the world and is worshipped by the sages.
