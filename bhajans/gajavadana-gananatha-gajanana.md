## Lyrics

Gajavadana Gananatha Gajanana
Hey Shiva Nandana Paahi Gajanana
Gajavadana Hey Gajanana
Mukthi Pradatha Shakthi Pradatha
Sharanam Sharanam Gajanana (2)

## Meaning

I take refuge in Lord Ganesha the elephant-faced son of Lord Shiva who bestows strength and liberation.
