## Lyrics

Jagadambe Amba Bhavani
Jaganmata Mangala Dayini
Jagadeeshwari Sai Janani [ Jagadambe Amba ... ]
Parameshwari Parthi Shivani
Narayani Mangala Karini
Sayeeshwari Sankata Harini
Sarveshwari Sai Janani

## Meaning

Bhavani, Parameshwari, Jagadeeshwari, Narayani, Jagadambe, Sarveshwari are the various names of the same Mother who confers auspiciousness, the one who removes misery who is verily Mother Sai of Parthi.
