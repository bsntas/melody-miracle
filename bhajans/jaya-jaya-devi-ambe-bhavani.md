## Lyrics

Jaya Jaya Devi Ambe Bhavani
Durga Saraswathi Lakshmi Narayani [ Jaya Jaya Devi ... ]
Abhaya Pradayini Tribhuvana Dharini
Parthi Nivasini Sai Janani

## Meaning

Victory to Mother Amba Bhavani the personification of Durga, Lakshmi and Saraswathi and Narayani. She is verily Mother Sai, who dwells in Parthi, confers freedom from fear and the one who bears the three worlds.
