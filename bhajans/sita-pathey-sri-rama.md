## Meaning

Victory to You O Lord Rama, the consort of Mother Sita. Victory to the one who is the son of King Dasharatha and the most merciful one, the one with Lotus shaped eyes, the most beautiful one from Raghu Dynasty and who bestows eternal bliss.
