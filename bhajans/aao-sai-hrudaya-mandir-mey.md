## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Sai, Guru |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Aao Sai Hrudaya Mandir Mey
Antar Jyothi Jalao Sai [ Aao Sai ... ]
Mata Pitha Guru Prana Sakha Sai
Sab Tiratha Merey Terey Charan Mey

## Meaning

Welcome, Lord Sai. Please kindle the flame of wisdom in the temple of my heart. You are my life breath, my father, mother, divine guide and kith and kin. Your divine Feet are my pilgrimages.
