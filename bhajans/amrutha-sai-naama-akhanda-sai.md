## Lyrics

Amrutha Sai Naama Akhanda Sai Prema
Aganita Guna Dhama Anantha Sai Prema
Sathya Sai Rama Sarva Samana Prema
Jaya Jaya Ram Jaya Raghu Ram Sathya Sai Rama

## Meaning

The name of Sri Sathya Sai is verily an infinite stream of love in the form of nectar. His glories are endless. His Love is universal. Praise be to Lord Sai Rama.
