## Meaning

Glory to Divine Mother Sathya Sai, the most compassionate one who fosters the Universe. O Mother, You are the most beautiful of the three worlds, the one who likes rhythm and music. You are the bestower of boons, the very embodiment of the Pranava (Om). You are the Supreme Being who grants us good lives.
