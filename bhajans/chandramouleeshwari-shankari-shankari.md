## Lyrics

## Meaning

Glory to Mother Parvathi, the consort of Shiva who is adorned with crescent moon on His matted hair. The goddess Shankari bestows auspiciousness. Her beauty is equivalent to crores of Madanas (God of Love). She is the sister of Madhava (Krishna) who is captivating to the whole world. She is none other than Sai of Parthi.
