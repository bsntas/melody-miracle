## Meaning

Worship the Goddess Shiva Shankari, the one who protects all who surrender to her, the one rides on the lion, carries the trident, one who is always full of bliss and happiness, one who is free from sorrow and fear, the consort of Sadashiva, the Goddess Sayeeshwari.
