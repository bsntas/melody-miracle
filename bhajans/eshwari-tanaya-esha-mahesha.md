## Lyrics

Eshwari Tanaya Esha Mahesha
Shambho Shankara Sadashiva
Parthi Pureesha Hey Paramesha
Sharanam Sharanam Partheeshwara
Sharanam Sharanam Sayeeshwara (2)

## Meaning

O Lord Mahesha (Shiva), You have incarnated as Sai, the son of mother Eshwaramba. O Lord, You are the embodiment of bliss and auspiciousness. You are the supreme Lord of Parthi. We surrender unto Your lotus feet, O Lord Sai.
