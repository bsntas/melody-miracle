## Lyrics

Namo Gajendra Vadana Ganapathi Jai
Namo Shiva Nandana Jaya Sura Pathi Jai
Siddhi Vinayaka Moksha Pradayaka
Jaya Shubha Mangala Partheeshwara Jai [ Namo Shiva Nandana ... ]

## Meaning

I worship Lord Ganesha, the elephant-faced one who is the son of Lord Shiva and the leader of the Suras (demi-gods). He is the bestower of success and liberation. Victory to the Lord who grants auspiciousness and who is verily Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi.
