Parthi Puri Vasa Sai Rama

## Meaning

Chant the name of Sai Rama who resides in Parthi. Sing of Sai Rama of Prashanthi Nilayam. The Sai Rama - with eyes like Lotus petals. Chant the blessed name - Sai Rama.
