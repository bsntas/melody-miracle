## Lyrics

Sri Ganesha Jaya Ganesha Sathya Sai Deva
Sadguru Sai Deva Jagat Guru Deva [ Sri Ganesha ... ]
Vighna Harana Dukha Harana Omkar Swaroopa

Mangala Kara Mangala Kara Sri Ganesha Deva

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Ganesh, and Lord Sai, the true and Universal Guru. He removes obstacles and sorrows. He bestows good fortune and is the embodiment of the life-force (om)
