|---|---|

## Lyrics

Hum Ko Deejo Teri Duwaayen
Merey Baba Merey Sai
Raat Aur Din, Din Aur Raat
Terey Sharan Mey Rahte Hain
Hum Ko Sikhao Apaara Vidya
Apaara Prema Apaara Bhakthi
Sathya Dharma Ki Raaha Dikhao
Karo Prabhu Jeevana Ujiyaara (2)

## Meaning

Please give us Your blessings my Baba, my Sai, night and day. We live under Your protection. Please teach us Your infinite knowledge, infinite love, we are infinitely devoted to You. Show us the way to Truth and Righteousness and enlighten our life.
