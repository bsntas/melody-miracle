## Lyrics

Atmabhi Rama Ananda Rama
Sita Rama Sai Rama
Sundara Naama Hey Ghana Shyama

## Meaning

Chant the sweet names of Lord Rama, Ghana Shyama, Radhey Shyama, Sai Rama, the indweller of our hearts and embodiment of bliss.
