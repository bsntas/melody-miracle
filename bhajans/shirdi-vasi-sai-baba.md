## Meaning

O Sai Baba, residing in Shirdi and Parthi, You are my Lord, the one who carries the vital force of life. We have come to Your doorstep, O Lord, please give us a glimpse of Your Divine Form. Sai, You are the Lord of all, and You are the light of life and life itself.
