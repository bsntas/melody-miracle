## Lyrics

Rama Krishna Hari Mukunda Murari
Panduranga Panduranga Panduranga Hari
Makara Kundala Dhari Bhaktha Bandhu Shourie
Mukthi Daata Shakthi Daata Vittala Narahari [ Rama Krishna ... ]
Tulasi Dhara Kandhara Bhaktha Hrudaya Mandira
Mandaradri Dhara Mukunda Indiresha Sri Hari

## Meaning

Chant the names of the Lord Rama, Krishna, Hari, Mukunda, Murari. He is also Lord Panduranga of Pandharpur, who wears fish shaped earrings. He is the near and dear of the forlorn devotee. He is Vittala, who bestows liberation and power. Decorated by a garland of Tulasi leaves, He resides in the temple of the heart. He is the one who supported Mount Mandara, He is the one who liberates, Lord of Indra and is the Supreme Lord, Sri Hari.
