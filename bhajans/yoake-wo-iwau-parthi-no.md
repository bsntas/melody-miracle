## Lyrics

Yoake wo Iwau Parthi no Chi ni
Furi Sosogareru Darshan no Ai
Kokoro no Uchi ni Ai no Mau
Fushigi Naru Yorokobi yo
Oh Baba Ai Naru Kami
Subete ni Yadoru Shin Naru Chikara
Oh Baba Ai Naru Sai
Mezame Ataeru Onchou yo

## Meaning

At dawn, we celebrate at Parthi. The love of Darshan (divine vision) rains down incessantly. The love within my heart flutters about. The joy is delightful. Oh Baba, You are the God of Love. You dwell in everything with Your true power. Oh Sai Baba, You are love itself. You bless us with Your divine grace.
