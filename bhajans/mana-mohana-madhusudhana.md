## Lyrics

Mana Mohana Madhusudhana
Mangala Charana Narayana
Govardhanodhara Gopalana
Govinda Gopala Narayana (3) [ Mangala Charana ... ]

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Krishna who captures the minds of all, the slayer of demon Madhu. He is verily Lord Narayana, the origin of auspiciousness. He is the cowherd Gopala who lifted the mountain Govardhana to save the devotees.
