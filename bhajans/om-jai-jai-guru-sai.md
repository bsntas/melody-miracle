## Lyrics

Om Jai Jai Guru Sai Mukunda
Sai Mukunda Sai Mukunda [ Om Jai ... ]
Satchidananda Ananda Chandha
Devaki Nanda Paramananda

## Meaning

Glory and victory to Divine Preceptor Sai the very embodiment of Mukunda, the bestower of liberation (Mukti) who is embodiment of truth, awareness and bliss. Prince Mukunda is the darling son of (mother) Devaki and (father) Nanda. He is the eternally blissful Sai Krishna.
