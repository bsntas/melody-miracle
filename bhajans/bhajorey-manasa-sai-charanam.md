## Lyrics

Bhajorey Manasa Sai Charanam
Pavana Taraka Sai Charanam
Nirmala Nishchala Nirupama Charanam
Adi Anantha Sai Charanam
Bhakthi Mukthi Pradayaka Charanam

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Sai and contemplate on Sai's Feet that save and protect the afflicted. The incomparable, pure, unchanging and the eternal Feet of Sai endow one with devotion and help attain liberation.
