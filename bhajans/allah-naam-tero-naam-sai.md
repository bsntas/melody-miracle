| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## Lyrics

Allah Naam Tero Naam Sai Naam Ek Hi Naam
Yeshu Nanak Baba Naam
Buddha Mahavira Sai Naam (2)
Sathya Dharma Shanthi Prema Baba Naam
Sai Naam Ek Hi Naam

## Meaning

Glory to the names of the Almighty - Allah, Jesus, Nanak, Buddha, Mahavir. All names and forms of God are Lord Sai's who is the very embodiment of Truth, Righteousness, Peace and Love.
