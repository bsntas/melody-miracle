## Lyrics

Jaya Guru Shankara Girija Ramana
Jaya Jaya Mangala Divya Charana [ Jaya Guru ... ]
Jaya Muni Vanditha Nagabharana
Jaya Sarveshwara (Sayeeshwara) Saroja Nayana

## Meaning

Victory to the beloved of Mother Girija whose Divine feet confers all that is auspicious, who adorns snakes on His body and meditated upon by the Sages and who is none other than the lotus eyed Sayeeshwara.
