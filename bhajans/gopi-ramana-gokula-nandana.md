## Lyrics

Gopi Ramana Gokula Nandana
Shritha Jana Palana Narayana
Lakshmi Ramana Aganita Guna Gana
Sai Narayana Namo Namo
Sathya (Parthi) Narayana Namo Namo

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Krishna, the beloved of Gopis and the boy from Gokula. O Lord, You are Narayana, the protector of the Holy and the pious people, You are the consort of Lakshmi, and is the immeasurable treasure house of virtuous qualities. Salutations to Lord Sai residing in Puttaparthi who is verily Lord Narayana.
