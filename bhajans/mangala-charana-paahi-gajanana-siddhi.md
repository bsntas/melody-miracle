## Lyrics

Mangala Charana Paahi Gajanana Siddhi Vinayaka Deva
Parvathi Tanaya Sindura Vadana Siddhi Vinayaka Deva
Mooshika Vahana Shambho Nandana
Bhava Bhaya Haari Sathya Sanathana [ Parvathi Tanaya ... ]

## Meaning

Praise be to Lord Ganesha, the granter of auspiciousness. His vehicle is the mouse. He removes the fears of the world. He is the son of Mother Parvathi. He grants spiritual prowess.
