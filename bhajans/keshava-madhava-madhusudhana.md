## Lyrics

Keshava Madhava Madhusudhana

## Meaning

Chant the various names of the same Lord Narayana: Keshava, Madhava, Madhusudhana, Sridhara, Janardhana, Achyutha, Govinda and Gopala. Victory to the Lord who has the most beautiful form holding the flute.
