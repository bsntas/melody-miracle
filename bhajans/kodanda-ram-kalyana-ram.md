## Lyrics

Kodanda Ram Kalyana Ram
Janaki Ram Jaya Jaya Ram
Ananda Ram Ayodhya Ram
Parthi Pureeshwara Sri Sai Ram (2)

## Meaning

Victory to Rama wielder of Kodanda (bow), bestower of auspiciousness. You are the life of Janaki (Sita). You are the blissful Rama of Ayodhya and at present Parthi as Shri Sai.
