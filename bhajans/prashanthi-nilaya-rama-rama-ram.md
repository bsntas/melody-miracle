## Lyrics

Prashanthi Nilaya Rama Rama Ram
Parthi Pureeshwara Rama Rama Ram

## Meaning

Chant the name of Rama incessantly. He is the same as Lord Narayana who incarnated as Krishna and Panduranga. He is none other than Lord Sai residing in Prashanthi Nilayam.
