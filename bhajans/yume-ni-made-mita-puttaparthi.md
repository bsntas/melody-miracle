## Lyrics

Yume ni Made Mita Puttaparthi ni Kimashita
Ai no Kami, Sai Baba ga Orareru Tokoro
Rinne no Kusari wo Tachi Kiru Tame ni
Osazuke Kudasai Anata no Ai wo, Jiai no Chikara wo

## Meaning

I first saw You in a dream as You came down to Puttaparthi. Sai Baba, God of Love, I wish to be with You. You came to us in order to break our chain of rebirth so that we may join You. Please grant us the power of Your divine love.
