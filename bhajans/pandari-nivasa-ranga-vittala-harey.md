## Meaning

O Lord Panduranga (Krishna), residing at Pandharpur, You are the one who protects the weak. Hey Sai, the Director of the play of life, You are the one who resides in the devotee's heart. Hey Sai of Parthi, You are the one who protects us. You are verily Vittala and Krishna.
