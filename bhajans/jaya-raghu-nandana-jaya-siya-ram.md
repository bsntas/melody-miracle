Mahima Avatara Sai Ram

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Rama who belongs to the Raghu clan and who is the consort of Mother Janaki and who redeems devotees. Lord Krishna (Shyam) always has a smiling countenance and is also the Lord of Meera and the one who held high Mountain Mandara to protect tribesmen from the wrath of torrential rain. He is our own Sai Rama of untainted reputation.
