## Meaning

I pray to Mother Durga, the Divine Mother. She is Mother Ambe, born out of the flame that emanated from the bodies of Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva. She is full of compassion and gives us courage, protection and joy. I sing to You, O Divine Mother, and praise Your Supreme Power and Form in Mother Sai.
