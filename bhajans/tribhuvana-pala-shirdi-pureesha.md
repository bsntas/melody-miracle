## Lyrics

Tribhuvana Pala Shirdi Pureesha
Araj Suno Merey Baba
Deena Janon Ke Tum Rakhwaley
Shyama Manohara Muraliwale
In Aankhon Ke Pyaas Bujhadey
Darshana Do Parthi Baba [ Araj Suno ... ]

## Meaning

Oh Lord of Shirdi, who sustains the three worlds, guardian of the helpless, You are verily Lord Krishna with the enchanting form with the flute in His hand. Oh Lord, pay heed to our prayers and satiate our hunger for Your vision, Oh Lord of Parthi.
