## Lyrics

Hey Parameshwara Parthi Pureeshwara
Karuna Sagara Shankara
Daya Karo Sai Shankara (3)

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Sai, the Lord of Lords, the ocean of compassion and the Lord of Parthi. O Lord Sai, the bestower of auspiciousness (Shankara), shower Your mercy on us.
