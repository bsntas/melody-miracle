## Lyrics

## Meaning

O Mother of the Universe, You have descended in the form of Bhairavi, Shankari, Durga, Bhavani. You are the beloved of devotees and who supports the three worlds. We adore You as Bhagawathi, Bharati and the Brahma Sanathani (primordial goddess).
