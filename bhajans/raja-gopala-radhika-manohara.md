## Meaning

Glory to Lord Gopala (Krishna), the King of Mathura and the beloved of Radha. He is the dear child of Gokula, ever ready to shower His Grace on the forlorn. He is the child of Nanda who stole the hearts of one and all,  the one reclining on the great serpent Adi Sesha and the supreme benefactor of the needy.
