## Lyrics

Aao Aao Antaryami
Adi Narayana Sai Narayana [ Aao Aao ... ]
Hrudaya Vihari Hey Giridhari
Hey Giridhari Hey Giridhari
Hari Hari Sri Hari Sai Murari

## Meaning

Welcome Lord Sai Narayana, the indweller of our hearts, the one who lifted the Govardhan hill. You are the one who assumed the form of Hari and Murari to remove the demonic characteristics in our minds.
