## Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Devi, Sai |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Anatha Rakshaki Sai Maheshwari
Abhaya Pradayini Ananda Roopini [ Anatha Rakshaki ... ]
Ambuja Lochani Akhilandeshwari
Abhayam Abhayam Dehi [ Anatha Rakshaki ... ]
Sajjana Vanditha Sankata Nashini
Sharanam Sharanam Sai

## Meaning

Mother Sai protects and gives refuge to the destitute. The lotus-eyed Goddess of the Universe frees us from fear, destroys grief and is worshipped by all living beings. Let us surrender to Sai.
