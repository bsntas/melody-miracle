## Meaning

Glory to the valorous Hanuman, the one endowed with supreme strength, the one who incessantly chants the Holy name of Lord Rama and who has installed himself in the heart of the Lord. Victory to Lord Rama, His consort Sita and to His brother Lakshmana. Victory to Hanuman. Victory to Lord Krishna, the Lord of Radha. Glory to the King of Raghu clan, Lord Rama, the redeemer of the downtrodden.
