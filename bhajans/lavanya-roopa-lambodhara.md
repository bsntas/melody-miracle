Lavanya Roopa Lambodhara

Eka Dantha Jai Jai Ganesha

## Meaning

Victory to Lord Ganesha, Lord of the Ganaas (Shiva's army), who has a charming form, a pleasant face, a big belly and who is the son of Shiva and Shankari (Parvathi). Protect me O Lord with a single tusk, the source of the Primordial sound and who is verily Lord Sai, the Lord of Parthi.
