## Lyrics

Sri Rama Charanam Smaramyaham
Sthira Sukha Dayaka Sai Charanam [ Sri Rama ... ]
Shritha Jana Palaka Shirdi Charanam
Srikara Shubha Kara Parthi Charanam

## Meaning

Worship the feet of Sri Rama, which bestows eternal bliss, the feet of Shirdi Sai, which bestows austerity to those who worship them and the feet of Sai of Puttaparthi, which bestows wealth and prosperity.
