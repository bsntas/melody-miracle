## Lyrics

Murali Manohara Shyama Bhajo
Munijana Sevitha Sai Bhajo [ Murali Manohara ]
Shyama Bhajo Radhey Shyama Bhajo
Shyama Krishna Sai Krishna Bhajo

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord Krishna the one who conquers the mind with his divine music of the flute, the dark blue complexioned Lord. Sing about the one who was served by the pious sages. The dark blue Krishna is none other than my Sai Krishna.
