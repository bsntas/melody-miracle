## Lyrics

Namah Om Sathya Sai Krishnaya Namah Om
Gopi Jana Vallabhaya Govindaya Namah Om
Madhavaya Keshavaya Yadavaya Namah Om
Radha Ramana Madhusudhana Mohanaya Namah Om
Bansidhara Muralidhara Giridharaya Namah Om

## Meaning

Salutations to Lord Sathya Sai who is verily Krishna, the beloved of the cowherd girls (Gopikas). Salutations to You, the sweet one, the descendant of the Yadu clan and the slayer of the demon Kesha! You are the Lord of Radha and the slayer of the demon Madhu. You are the one who lifted the Govardhana mountain, we worship You, O divine Flutist.
