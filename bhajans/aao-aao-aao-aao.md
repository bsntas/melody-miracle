## Lyrics

Aao Aao Aao Aao
Aao Sundara Nanda Gopal

Darshana Do Nandalala (2)

## Meaning

Welcome the charming Gopala, the son of Nanda who lovingly stole butter and who revels in the hearts of devotees. O Nandalala, grace us with your Darshan.
