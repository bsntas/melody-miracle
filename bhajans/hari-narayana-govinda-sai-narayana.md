## Lyrics

Hari Narayana Govinda Sai Narayana Govinda
Murali Madhava Govinda
Govardhanadhara Govinda

## Meaning

Blissfully contemplate on the names of the Lord Narayana the indweller in Man, Govinda the cowherd boy, the master of senses, Murali the one who holds flute and the one who held aloft the Govardhana mountain.
