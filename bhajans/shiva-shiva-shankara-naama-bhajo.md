## Meaning

Worship Lord Shiva and sing His name. Praise be unto the name of Lord Shiva. He grants fearlessness and destroys all evil. His dance enchants all. He is beyond the three worlds. The Lord of Vibhuti (sacred ash) has incarnated. Victory to Lord Shiva, who has come to this world as Lord Sathya Sai.
