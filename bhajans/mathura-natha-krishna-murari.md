## Lyrics

Mathura Natha Krishna Murari
Radha Madhava Hey Giridhari [ Mathura Natha ... ]
Gokula Brindavana Sanchari
Kunja Vihari Raasa Vihari
Sri Sai Natha Parthi Vihari

## Meaning

Chant the name of Lord of Mathura, Krishna, destroyer of Mura, resident of Gokul and Brindavan who roamed the woods and liked the "Raasa" dance. He is none other than Lord Sai of Parthi.
