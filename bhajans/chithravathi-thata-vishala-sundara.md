## Lyrics

Chithravathi Thata Vishala Sundara Sai
Avadhootha Chinthana Guru Deva Sathya Sai
Merey Baba Sai, Merey Baba Sai [ Chithravathi Thata ... ]
Shirdi Baba Sai Parthi Baba Sai
Yamuna Teera Sri Krishna Kanhaiya Sai [ Avadhootha Chintana ... ]

## Meaning

Glory be to the Divine Preceptor, Lord Sathya Sai, the beautiful one who resides on the banks of river Chithravathi, the one who is contemplated on by the Saints of highest order. He is the Lord of Shirdi and Parthi and is verily Lord Krishna who resides on the banks of river Yamuna.
