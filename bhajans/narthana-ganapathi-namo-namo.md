## Lyrics

Narthana Ganapathi Namo Namo
Naga Bhooshana Namo Namo
Yaksha Kinnara Gandharva Sevitha
Sowbhagya Dayaka Siddhi Vinayaka
Parthi Vinayaka Namo Namo
Paapa Vinashaka Namo Namo (Hey)

## Meaning

Bow to the dancing Lord Ganesha, adorned with a serpent. Worshipped by demi-Gods, He confers auspiciousness and removes sins. Lord Sai of Parthi is the embodiment of Lord Ganesha.
