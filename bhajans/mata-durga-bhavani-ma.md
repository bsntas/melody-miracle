|---|---|

## Lyrics

Mata Durga Bhavani Ma
Jaya Jagat Vandini Mata [ Mata Durga ... ]
Mangala Gowri Sowbhagya Dayini
Pranava Swaroopini Mata
Shiva Shaktheeshwari Palaya Maam
Ambe Bhavani Rakshaya Maam (Jagad)

## Meaning

Glory to Mother Goddess Durga, the origin of creation, bestower of good luck and good fortune, and worshipped by one and all. The consort of Lord Shiva protects and guards all and is the personification of "Om".
