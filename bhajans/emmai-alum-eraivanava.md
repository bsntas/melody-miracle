## Meaning

Come, O Lord who rules us, who permeates the whole Universe and who occupies my mind. Come, O Lord, to rule our bodies by Your eternal Cosmic dance. Come, O Lord and become one with the soul and shine high in consciousness. Come, O Lord who is verily Om, the source of life. Come, O Lord Nataraja of Thimmakudi. O the embodiment of Love, please come beautifully dancing.
