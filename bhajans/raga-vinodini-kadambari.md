## Lyrics

Raga Vinodini Kadambari
Mata Mata Kalavathi
Veena Vadini Brahma Sanathani
Veda Vilasini Shastra Vikasini
Saraswathi Devi Sai Avatarini
Mata Kalavathi

## Meaning

Salutations to the Mother of all arts, Saraswathi, the consort of Lord Brahma. The goddess Saraswathi who plays the veena (string instrument) is the shining exponent of all vedas and scriptures. She has incarnated as Lord Sathya Sai.
