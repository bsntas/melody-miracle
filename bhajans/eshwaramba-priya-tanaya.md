## Lyrics

Eshwaramba Priya Tanaya Sri Sathya Sai (2)
Ni Divya Charithamu Chadivina Vinina (2)
Vinina Chadivina Anandamu
Dayamaya Sai Karunamaya (2)
Karunamaya Karunincha Vayya
Satatamu Ni Nama Smaranamu Make (2)

## Meaning

The darling Son of mother Eshwaramba, Lord Sri Sathya Sai. Your Divine Story, the one who reads or listens. the one who listens or reads is blissful. He is full of compassion and merciful. Oh Lord with Compassion, show the mercy. We need the chanting of your name always.
