| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Deity** | Ganesha |
| **Language** | Sanskrit / Hindi |

## Lyrics

Gajavadana He Gajanana
Gowri Tanaya Gajanana
Gangadhara Shiva Nandana
Girija Priya Nandana

## Meaning

Lord Ganesha is the beloved elephant-faced son of Mother Goddess Gowri (Parvathi, Girija) and Lord Shiva.
