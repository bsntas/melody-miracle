## Lyrics

Hey Parthipuri Bhagawan Bhagawan
Daya Karo Bhagawan

Hey Patitha Pavana Ram Bhagawan [ Daya Karo ... Sai Bhagawan ]

## Meaning

Lord Sathya Sai of Parthi and Prasanthi Nilayam, is the Supreme Guru. He is the friend of the afflicted and forsaken ones. He is Lord who destroys our sins. Glory to the Supreme Guru.
