## Lyrics

Sadguru Deva Namo Sai
Parthi Pureesha Paapa Vinasha
Bhava Bhaya Bhanjana Parama Dayala
Brahma Vishnu Maheshwara Roopa
Jagadoddhara Deena Dayala (2)

## Meaning

Chant the name of the divine preceptor, Lord Sai. Residing in Puttaparthi, He removes sin, fear, and ignorance from this world. Lord Sai is the supremely compassionate one who liberates the world and uplifts the downtrodden. He is verily the embodiment of the Divine Trinity of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva (Maheshwara).
