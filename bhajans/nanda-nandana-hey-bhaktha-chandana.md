## Lyrics

Nanda Nandana Hey Bhaktha Chandana
Giridhari Govinda Radhika Jeevana [ Nanda Nandana ... ]
Meera Ke Nath Prabhu Madana Mohana
Bansidhara Dharanidhara Gopi Ranjana
Shirdeesha Partheesha Gopalana Sai Gopalana

## Meaning

Chant the many names of the son of Nanda, Lord Krishna - Govinda, Gopala, and Giridhari. He is the very life of Radha and the Lord of Meera. He delights His devotees and Gopis with His flute playing. He is none other than Lord Sai, Lord of Shirdi and Parthi.
