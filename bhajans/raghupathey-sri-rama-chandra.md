## Lyrics

Raghupathey Sri Rama Chandra
Raghava Raghava [ Raghupathey ]
Ravikula Soma Sarvabhowma
Ramya Susheela Rama Rama

## Meaning

We bow to Rama, Lord of the Raghu Dynasty, Who is still and shining as the moon. He is Raghava, Lord of Raghu dynasty. Raghava, who is the doyen of the Solar lineage, respected by everyone and has a charming personality.
