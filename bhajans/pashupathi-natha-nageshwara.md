## Lyrics

Pashupathi Natha Nageshwara
Hara Hara Bham Bham Shiva Shankara
Parvathi Pathey Maheshwara
Lingodhbhavakara Lingeshwara
Partheeshwara Sai Parameshwara

## Meaning

Chant the names of Lord of all beings, Lord Shiva, the one who drapes the cobra around his neck. Glory to Lord Shankara, bestower of auspiciousness, who is the Supreme Lord of Parvathi. He is the Supreme Lord Sai of Parthi, the one who brings out the Linga.
