## Lyrics

Kamala Nayana Sri Sai Jaya Jaya
Karunakara Narayana Jaya Jaya
Sri Raghu Nandana Rama Jaya Jaya
Yadu Kula Bhooshana Shyama Jaya Jaya
Esha Manusha Vesha Jaya Jaya
Meera Ke Prabhu Natha Jaya Jaya

## Meaning

Victory to Sri Sai whose eyes resembles the lotus. Victory to compassionate Narayana, Rama the beloved of Raghu clan, Shyama (Krishna) the jewel of Yadu clan, the Supreme Lord who is in the human form and Lord of Meera.
