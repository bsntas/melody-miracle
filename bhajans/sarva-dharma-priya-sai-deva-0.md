## Meaning

Glory to you Oh Lord Sai who protects and nourishes all faiths and religions. Glory to the Eternal one who is the embodiment of Christ, Mohammad, Buddha and Mahavira Nanak, Zohrashtra and Shankara. Glory to you Oh Lord Sai who bestows and sustains Truth, Peace and Love.
