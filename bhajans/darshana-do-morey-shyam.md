## Meaning

Grant Your Divine Darshan, O the most beautiful Lord Krishna of Brija (Brindavan), the Lord of Radha. You are the dark complexioned one playing the Flute. You are the curly haired one draped in yellow silk whose words are nectarine. You have incarnated as Lord Sai at Parthi. Chant the name and sing the glory of the Lord, O Heart! Sing the name and meditate on Lord Krishna and Sai every moment of life.
