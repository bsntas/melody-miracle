## Lyrics

Krishna Krishna Mukunda Murari

## Meaning

Glory to Krishna, Mukunda and Murari, the One who lifted the Mountain for sake of Gopis. The Lord of Meera's devotion, one with the Flute, the dark colored charmer of Radha.
