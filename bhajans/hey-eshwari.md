## Meaning

O Mother, You are the consort of Eshwara (Shiva), You are the lotus-eyed one and is loved by all. You are also known as Shankari, Sambhavi, Katyayani and Kamalambikey and has a very pleasing form. You are the one who destroys the cycle of births and who bestows liberation.
