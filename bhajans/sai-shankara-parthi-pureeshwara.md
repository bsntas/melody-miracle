## Meaning

Lord Sai of Parthi is none other than Lord Shiva whose attributes are described as, the one supporting the sacred river Ganges in His matted hair, adorning the Crescent moon on His head, adorning the serpent as a necklace, having a third eye in the forehead and smeared with ash all over the body.
