## Lyrics

Madhava Sodari Mangala Gowri
Sundara Vadani Meena Lochani [ Madhava Sodari ... ]
Pashupathi Ranjani Parvathi Gowri
Tribhuvana Palini Devi Bhavani
Shiva Shakthi Roopini Sai Narayani

## Meaning

Glory to the auspicious, radiant faced Mother Gowri (Parvathi), Vishnu's sister. She is the consort of Lord Shiva and protects all three worlds. Lord Sai is the embodiment of Shiva and Shakti.
