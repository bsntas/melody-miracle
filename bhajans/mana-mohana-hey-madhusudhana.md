## Lyrics

Mana Mohana Hey Madhusudhana
Mano Ramana Hey Nanda Nandana
Radha Ramana Hey Jaganmohana
Govinda Govinda Sai Jaga Vandana

## Meaning

Sing the glory of Lord Krishna, the cowherd boy who attracts and enchants the mind. He is the son of Nanda, the slayer of demon Madhu, is adored by Radha and is worshipped by the world.
