## Meaning

O Divine Mother, You are the nourisher and charmer of the Universe. You are the provider of lasting happiness and the destroyer of misfortune. You are known by several names, Parvathi, Bhairavi, Sambhavi, Gowri, Parameshwari and Bhuvaneshwari and presently You have incarnated as Mother Sai of Parthi.
