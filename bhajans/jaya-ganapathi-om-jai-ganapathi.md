## Meaning

Victory to the Lord of Ganaas, Lord Ganesha, the son of Mother Parvathi. Victory to the one who rides on the mouse. Just as His brother, Lord Subrahmanya, worships Him, we too surrender at the feet of Lord Ganesha.
