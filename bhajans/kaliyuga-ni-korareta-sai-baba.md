## Lyrics

Kaliyuga ni Korareta Sai Baba
Sekai no Minna no Sachi no tame
Sai Baba Anata wa Subete wo Michibiite Kureta
Itsudemo Watashi wa Anata to Hitotsu
Subete wo Anata ni Sasagemasu

## Meaning

O Lord Sai Baba, You have come in the Kali age to bless everyone with good fortune. You guide us all so that we may become one with You. We devote everything to You my Lord.
