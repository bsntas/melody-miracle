## Lyrics

Jaya Ganesha Gajanana Daya Nidhey
Mangala Dayaka Siddhi Vinayaka
Lambodhara Gajavadana Manohara
Parvathi Nandana Siddhi Vinayaka

## Meaning

Victory to the Elephant faced Lord, compassion personified, who grants auspiciousness and Occult powers. Lord Ganesha has an enormous belly, son of Parvathi and is also known as Vinayaka (the remover of obstacles).
