## Lyrics

Om Muruga Va Muruga Shanmukha Natha
Shiva Shakthi Ganapathaye Shanmukha Natha
Partheeshwara Parameshwara Shanmukha Natha
Sharanagatha Rakshakane Shanmukha Natha
Shanmukha Shanmukha, Shiva Shakthi Shanmukha
Shiva Shakthi Ganapathaye, Sathya Sai Shanmukha

## Meaning

Come O Lord Muruga, Lord of six faces. We worship Thee and Lord Shiva, Mother Shakthi (Parvathi) and Lord Ganapathi. You are verily Lord Sai, Lord of Parthi. O Protector of those who take refuge in You, we pray to Thee to grant us salvation.
