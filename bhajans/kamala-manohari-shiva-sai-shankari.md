## Meaning

Salutations to the enchanting form of Mother Parvathi, the consort of Lord Shankara, seated on the Lotus, Mother Kaali adorning the garland made of skulls and wild flowers. Victory to Mother Katyayani, Mother Saraswathi also known as Bharati and Mother Kamakshi, the presiding deity at Kanchi.
