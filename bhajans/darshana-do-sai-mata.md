## Lyrics

Darshana Do Sai Mata
Aao Aao Sai Mata [ Darshana Do ... ]

## Meaning

O Mother Sai, please come and bless us with the vision of Your divine form. O sustainer of the three worlds, the resident of Parthi, the embodiment of Love, come bless us all.
