## Meaning

Glory to Lord Shiva who resides in the Arunachala Hill, along with His consort, Mother Parvathi. The crescent moon adorns His locks of hair where He holds the Holy river Ganga. He is none other than our beloved Lord Sai, residing in Puttaparthi.
