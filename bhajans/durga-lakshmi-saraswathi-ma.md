## Meaning

Adore the universal Mother, who is worshipped as Durga, Lakshmi and Saraswathi. You are the remover of sorrows and the one who frees us from the cycle of birth and death by washing away our sins. You are the Supreme Goddess of Parthi who is compassionate and who bestows bliss to all.
