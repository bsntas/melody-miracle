## Meaning

Offering prayers to Narayana, who reclines on the Lotus, He is Hari and Narayana with enchanting blue hue. He holds the Conch and Sudarshan (wheel of liberation) Disc to protect his devotees. Lotus garlands enhance his glory, resting on the snake Sesha. We bow to Sai, who is Narayana.
