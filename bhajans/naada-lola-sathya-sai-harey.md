## Lyrics

Naada Lola Sathya Sai Harey

## Meaning

I bow to the lover of music, Sathya Sai One who adorns the snake as an ornament The one who rides on the bull - Nandi. I bow down to that Shiva, who is none other than Sathya Sai.
