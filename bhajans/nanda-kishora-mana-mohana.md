## Lyrics

Nanda Kishora Mana Mohana
Navaneetha Chora Madhusudhana
Devaki Nandana Yadu Nandana
Devadi Deva Jagat Palana

## Meaning

Glory to the son of Nanda, Lord Krishna, the one who enchants the mind. Glory to the one who steals butter, the slayer of demon, Madhu. Glory to the son of Devaki, and the child of the Yadava clan. He is the Lord of the Lords and the protector of the universe.
