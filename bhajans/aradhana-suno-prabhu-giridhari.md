| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## Lyrics

Aradhana Suno Prabhu Giridhari
Radha Madhava Kunja Vihari [ Aradhana Suno ... ]
Bhaktha Janon Ke Prana Dhari
Daya Karo Mujhey Mohana Pyaarey
Parthi Vihari Sai Murari

## Meaning

Worship Lord Krishna who held the Govardhana mountain up. He walked the garden (in Brindavan). He is the life sustainer of devotees. Lord Sai, of Parthi, please bestow mercy on us.
