## Lyrics

## Meaning

Chant the name of the most beautiful and charming Lord Govinda (Krishna), the beloved of Radha. Chant the name of the child from Yadu dynasty and the annihilator of demon Madhu. Chant the name of the Lord with lotus eyes, the one who gives succor to the downtrodden and the one who removes our afflictions.
