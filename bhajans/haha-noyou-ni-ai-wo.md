## Meaning

O Lord Sai, Your love nurtures us like a mother. Your love guides us like a father. Your love gratifies our hearts like a beautiful song. You illuminate our minds like the moon's light. Your love dwells within me. Your light settles upon all things. Sai Ram, O Swami, You are the God of Love.
