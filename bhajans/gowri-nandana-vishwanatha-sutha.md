## Lyrics

Gowri Nandana Vishwanatha Sutha
Skandagraja Sri Gananatha
Mooshika Sevitha Vakratunda
Lambodara Sri Vighna Nashaka
Prashanthi Nilaya Dwara Palaka

## Meaning

O Lord Ganesha, son of Parvathi and Shiva and elder brother of Skanda (Karthikeya), You have a curved tusk and a large stomach and You are worshipped by the mouse (Mooshika). You are the gatekeeper at the entrance of Lord Sathya Sai's ashram, Prasanthi Nilayam.
