## Lyrics

Jagadodhara Shyama Gopala
Murali Lola Hrudaya Vihara [ Jagadodhara ... ]
Jaya Brindavana Madana Gopala
Madana Gopala (2) [ Jaya Brindavana ... ]
Jaya Partheeshwara Sai Gopala

## Meaning

Glory to Lord Gopala (Lord Krishna), the Lord of the universe and the indweller of hearts. Victory to the one who is dark in complexion and who entices everyone with His Flute playing. Glory to Lord Gopala who moves about in Brindavan - who is verily Lord Sai residing in Parthi.
